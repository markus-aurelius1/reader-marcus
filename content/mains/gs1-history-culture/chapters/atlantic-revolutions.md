## American Revolution {#american}

| Economic — revolt against mercantilism (2013) | But not merely economic |
|---|---|
| {{Mercantilism}} → colonies supply raw material, serve as markets | Natural rights — Locke |
| {{Navigation Acts}} restricted colonial trade | Consent, social contract |
| Stamp Act, Townshend Acts, Tea Act → extraction + subordination | Representative government vs Parliament's sovereignty over the unrepresented |
| Merchants wanted free trade, economic self-determination | Self-government → claim to sovereignty |

- Bridge → taxation became constitutional because revenue came without representation → "no taxation without representation".

> [!FLOW] Mercantilist grievance → constitutional protest → revolution → popular sovereignty

## French Revolution {#french}

**Structural causes**
- Ancien Régime → legal privilege of clergy, nobility; burdens on the Third Estate.
- Fiscal crisis → war spending + unequal taxes.
- Rising bourgeoisie → economic power without political status.
- Enlightenment → challenged divine right, inherited privilege.
- Food prices, peasant grievance → elite crisis became mass revolution.

| Transformation | From → to |
|---|---|
| **Sovereignty** | Monarch → nation / people |
| **Status** | Subject → citizen |
| **Law** | Privilege → formal legal equality |
| **Society** | Feudal dues, corporate privilege challenged |
| **Politics** | Republicanism, representation, written constitution |
| **Religion–state** | Toward a secular public sphere |
| **Identity** | Dynastic loyalty → nationalism |

## Enduring Relevance (French) {#relevance}

- **Liberty** → civil liberties, constitutional restraint.
- **Equality** → legal equality; later widened to social, gender, racial.
- **Fraternity** → civic solidarity.
- **Popular sovereignty** → elections, representative legitimacy.
- **Citizenship** → rights tied to citizenship, not estate.
- **Nationalism, self-determination** → national unifications, anti-colonial movements.
- **Secular authority** → legitimacy without divine right.
- **Social revolution** → hierarchy can be dismantled politically.
- **Codified rule of law** → universal, not estate-specific.
- **Mass politics** → ordinary people as historical actors.

Indian echoes → rights vocabulary in reform and nationalism → [Awakening → National Consciousness](@socio-religious-reform#awakening-nation) · constitutional values → [GS2 Polity](@mains/gs2-polity)

## Limits & Interpretations {#limits}

- Equality incomplete → class, gender, racial, colonial exclusions.
- France → Terror → Napoleonic authoritarian rule.
- America → liberty beside ==slavery== and restricted participation.
- Nationalism double-edged → emancipatory (self-determination) and exclusionary (aggressive rivalry).
- So revolutions created a ==normative vocabulary==, not instant universal fulfilment.
