## Why a Developmental State {#why}

- Colonial inheritance → weak industrial base · low farm productivity, food insecurity · infrastructure and industry concentrated in a few regions · poverty, low capital formation · feudal intermediaries.
- Consensus → development could not be left to markets alone.
- Even industrialists' {{Bombay Plan (1944)}} accepted large state intervention.

Colonial roots → [Deindustrialisation](@colonial-economy#deindustrialisation)

## Planning {#planning}

- Planning Commission {{1950}} → First Plan 1951–56 → NDC {{1952}} → Second Plan 1956–61.

| Plan | Priority | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **First** | Agriculture, irrigation, dams, land reform | Food security, rural reconstruction |
| **Second (Mahalanobis)** | Heavy industry, capital goods, public sector | Autonomous industrial capability |
| **Third** | Self-reliance + agriculture again | Balance industry with food constraints |

- Model → mixed economy + import substitution + public-sector commanding heights; Industrial Policy Resolution 1956.

**Planning as regional balancer (2026)**
- Centre directs ==public investment== instead of letting capital flow only to old commercial centres.
- Multipurpose dams (Bhakra-Nangal), steel plants, fertiliser and engineering units widened the Union's economic geography.
- Allocation, irrigation, community development aimed at backward regions.
- Land reform targeted intra-regional inequality too.

> [!NOTE] Qualifier: some infrastructural gaps narrowed, imbalance never ended — unequal state capacity, irrigation, human capital and political power; the Green Revolution itself favoured Punjab, Haryana, western UP.

## Land Reform {#land-reform}

| Pillar | Content |
|---|---|
| **Intermediaries abolished** | Zamindari abolition — e.g. UP Act, 1950 |
| **Tenancy reform** | Security + rent regulation |
| **Ceilings, redistribution** | Surplus land to the landless |
| **Consolidation, cooperatives** | Viable holdings |

- Diluted by → state-dependent implementation · concealed tenancy · weak records · benami transfers · landed political influence.
- Verdict → abolition of intermediaries was the clearest success; ==redistribution and secure tenancy far more uneven==.
- Frame → from political freedom to agrarian citizenship.

Reform detail → [Land Reforms](@prelims/agriculture/agricultural-economics#land-reforms)

## Bhoodan–Gramdan {#bhoodan}

- **Bhoodan** → {{1951}}, Vinoba Bhave, {{Pochampally}} — voluntary gift of land to the landless.
- **Gramdan** → from individual gifts to village / community ownership.

| Objectives | Achievements | Limits |
|---|---|---|
| Peaceful redistribution | National moral mobilisation on land | Pledges ≠ legally transferred land |
| Avert class conflict | Millions of acres pledged | Much land uncultivable or disputed |
| Gandhian {{Sarvodaya}} | Showed reach of constructive action | Weak records, transfer machinery |
| Change the ethics of property | | No credit, irrigation, seed support |
| | | Persuasion could not overcome entrenched class power |

> [!VERIFY] Sources differ on the acreage actually distributed. Write "only a fraction of pledged land was effectively distributed" — do not quote a figure.

## Green Revolution & "Jai Jawan Jai Kisan" {#green-revolution}

- Trigger → chronic shortages + droughts + dependence on {{PL-480}} → food security became strategic autonomy.
- Package → HYV seeds + assured irrigation + fertiliser / pesticide + credit + procurement / MSP + research and extension.

| Outcomes | Contradictions |
|---|---|
| Wheat and productivity gains | Gains concentrated in Punjab, Haryana, western UP |
| Import dependence fell → self-sufficiency | Larger irrigated farmers gained most |
| Market-oriented agriculture | Regional and class inequality widened |
| Agro-industry, rural investment | Chemical, groundwater, ecological costs |

- **"Jai Jawan Jai Kisan"** → Shastri, {{1965}}: joined the Republic's two existential crises — ==border security + food security==.
- Reading → farm productivity after the mid-1960s was national-security policy, not just farm policy → [1965 war](@foreign-policy-security-crises#war-1965).

## Assessment {#assessment}

- Planning built the industrial and infrastructural base; state-led development did not automatically produce balanced development.
- Land reform changed institutions more than it redistributed assets.
- Verdict → the developmental state solved ==economic capacity== better than ==economic equality==.
