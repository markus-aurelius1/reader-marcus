## Starting Conditions, 1947 {#conditions}

- Partition, refugees, communal violence.
- Princely-state question.
- Weak economy, food deficit.
- Mass illiteracy.
- Cold War environment.
- Remarkable feature → state-building + nation-building + development attempted ==together, under universal democracy==.

## Consolidation Matrix {#consolidation}

| Dimension | Institution-building | Significance |
|---|---|---|
| **Constitutional** | Constitution {{1950}}; FRs + DPSPs; parliamentary-federal system | Legitimacy moved to popular sovereignty |
| **Democratic** | First general elections {{1951–52}}; Election Commission; opposition | Universal franchise as nation-building |
| **Administrative** | Civil services continued under constitutional accountability; judiciary, CAG | No administrative vacuum; inherited institutions democratised |
| **Territorial** | Princely integration; state reorganisation | Geographic unity → political-federal unity |
| **Economic** | Planning Commission {{1950}}; Five-Year Plans; mixed economy | Development became the state's duty |
| **Rural** | Zamindari abolition; Community Development Programme | Democratise the countryside |
| **Education** | IIT Kharagpur, UGC, AIIMS, university expansion | Human capital |
| **Science** | Atomic Energy Commission; CSIR; Scientific Policy Resolution | Scientific temper; sovereignty through technology |
| **External** | Non-alignment; Bandung / Panchsheel | Policy space in a bipolar world |

- Sequence → 1950 Constitution → 1951–52 elections → 1951 First Plan → 1953 Andhra → 1956 reorganisation + Industrial Policy Resolution → 1961 NAM.
- Frame → ==political democracy + economic planning + cultural pluralism + strategic autonomy==.
- India borrowed models but adapted them within a democratic constitution — neither Soviet nor Western wholesale.

## Maulana Azad {#azad}

| Phase | Contribution |
|---|---|
| **Before 1947** | Composite nationalism — religious identity need not contradict Indian nationhood; held to it as communal polarisation grew |
| **After 1947** | First Education Minister, {{1947–58}} — education as the infrastructure of citizenship |
| **Institutions** | IIT Kharagpur, UGC; {{Sangeet Natak, Sahitya, Lalit Kala}} Akademis |
| **Meaning** | Technical modernisation ==plus== cultural integration |

## Assessment {#assessment}

| Achievement | Limit |
|---|---|
| Democracy survived poverty and Partition | Mass education, health inadequate |
| Institutional continuity | Caste–land inequality persisted |
| Durable framework for dissent | Regional disparities |
| | Centre-heavy state capacity |

- Verdict → durable institutional framework; ==substantive socio-economic democratisation incomplete==.

## Rapid Recall {#rapid-recall}

- **Keywords** → democratic consolidation · constitutionalism · composite nationalism · scientific temper · developmental state · strategic autonomy
- **Examples** → 1951–52 elections · Planning Commission · IIT Kharagpur / UGC · the three Akademis · Panchsheel / NAM
- **Visual** → 1947 crisis → Constitution → elections → federal accommodation → planning → education / science → strategic autonomy → consolidated Republic
- **Intro** → India attempted nation-building before economic transformation — through democracy rather than after it.
- **Conclusion** → The achievement was not ending India's divisions but creating institutions through which they could be negotiated constitutionally.
