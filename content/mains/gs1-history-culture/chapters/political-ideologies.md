## Ideology Matrix {#matrix}

| | Capitalism | Socialism | Communism |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Property** | Mostly private | Mixed / social / public | Collective / state (historical communist states) |
| **Allocation** | Market, price | Market + planning | Central planning |
| **Core concern** | Enterprise, accumulation | Social justice, redistribution | Class abolition, common ownership |
| **State** | Limited to regulatory / welfare | Strong welfare, development role | Revolutionary party-state |
| **Promise** | Individual, economic freedom | Equality + welfare | Classless society |
| **Risk** | Inequality, concentration | Bureaucracy, fiscal burden | Authoritarianism, over-centralisation |

| Effect on society | Gains | Costs |
|---|---|---|
| **Capitalism** | Innovation, mobility, productivity, consumer choice | Class / wealth inequality, labour insecurity |
| **Socialism** | Welfare, redistribution, labour protection | Bureaucracy, state dependency |
| **Communist systems** | Rapid resource mobilisation, land redistribution, basic services | Political centralisation, coercive collectivisation in some variants |

> [!TRAP] Ideology ≠ one fixed institutional form. Distinguish !!theory, institutional form, policy instrument!!; do not equate a theory with one historical regime.

- Spectrum → market ← capitalism — mixed / social democracy — state planning → communism; real states sit at ==hybrid positions==.
- Convergence → capitalist states built welfare and regulation · socialist economies kept markets · communist systems reintroduced markets (NEP).

## Russian Revolution, 1917 {#russian-revolution}

| Cause | Points |
|---|---|
| **Political** | Discredited autocracy; Tsarina / Rasputin; weak reform after {{1905}} |
| **War** | WWI defeats, casualties, supply crisis, collapsing morale |
| **Economic** | Food and raw-material shortage, inflation |
| **Social** | Strikes, military mutiny, peasant land hunger |
| **Ideological** | Marxism; Bolshevik organisation; Lenin's {{April Theses}} |

| | February | October |
|---|---|---|
| **Nature** | Spontaneous mass uprising | Organised Bolshevik seizure |
| **Result** | Tsar overthrown | Provisional Government displaced |
| **Power** | Liberal / socialist duality | Bolshevik revolutionary state |
| **Change** | Political regime | ==Socio-economic transformation== |
| **War** | Continued | Exit by {{Brest-Litovsk}} |

**Significance of October**
- First durable socialist revolutionary state.
- Property and class relations radically challenged; land redistribution, nationalisation.
- USSR formed {{1922}}.
- Marxism moved from critique to state practice.
- Inspired communist parties in Europe, Asia; influenced anti-colonial movements, including India.
- Set capitalism vs communism as the century's defining conflict.

- Readings → structural (war + agrarian / class crisis) vs agency (Lenin, Bolshevik organisation).
- Synthesis → structural collapse created the opportunity; ==organisation converted it into Bolshevik power==.

Effect on Indian nationalism → [Left & Socialist Currents](@ideological-currents-participation#left)

## New Economic Policy, 1921 {#nep}

- Context → civil war and economic breakdown made extreme centralisation unsustainable.
- Character → ==socialist state control + limited market and private activity== to revive production.

| Feature | Content |
|---|---|
| **Commanding sectors** | Kept by the state |
| **Private trade, small enterprise** | Revived within limits |
| **Agriculture, commerce** | Market incentives restored |
| **Political power** | Firmly Bolshevik |

- Meaning → Lenin separated the strategic socialist goal from temporary economic instruments: ideological goal ≠ policy purity.

| Reading | View |
|---|---|
| **Ideological retreat** | Concession to capitalism |
| **Pragmatic state-building** | Necessary recovery |
| **Better answer** | Strategic economic retreat to preserve revolutionary power and restore production |

## NEP & India {#nep-india}

| Similarities (2014) | Critical differences |
|---|---|
| Mixed economy | Constitutional democracy, not one-party rule |
| State control of strategic sectors | Larger legitimate private sector |
| Industrialisation as the route to development | Consensus, plural politics |
| Self-reliance as objective | Also drew on {{Fabian socialism}} and other experiences |

- Judgement → NEP is ==one comparative influence / analogy==, not the sole origin of India's mixed economy.
