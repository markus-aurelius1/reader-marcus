## Position & Boundaries {#boundaries}

- International border → {{Nepal}}.
- Neighbours → {{Uttarakhand, Haryana, Delhi, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Bihar}}.

> [!TRAP] UP does !!not!! touch Himachal Pradesh.

## Regions {#regions}

| Region | Where | Character |
|---|---|---|
| **Terai** | Along the Nepal border | Marshy, high water table; forest and wetland; Sharda, Ghaghara, Rapti |
| **Ganga Plain** | Most of the State | Deep alluvium |
| — Western UP | Upper Ganga–Yamuna Doab, Rohilkhand | {{Highest irrigation and cropping intensity}} |
| — Central UP | Awadh | |
| — Eastern UP | Purvanchal | More floods |
| **Bundelkhand** | South-west | Hard rock, dissected upland; water-scarce, drought-prone |
| **Vindhyan fringe** | South-east — Mirzapur, Sonbhadra | Plateau, forest, minerals |

- **Upper Gangetic plain** = western + central UP.

## Climate, Soils & Vegetation {#climate-soils-vegetation}

- Humid subtropical monsoon. Rain {{decreases east → west}}.
- Summer → **loo**. Winter rain → western disturbances, more in the west.
- Soils → alluvial throughout (khadar, bhangar); **usar / reh** = saline–alkaline patches; red, yellow and mixed red-black soils in Bundelkhand and the Vindhyan south.
- Forest → Terai (moist deciduous, **sal**) and the Vindhyan belt (Sonbhadra, Chandauli, Mirzapur); the plain is almost entirely cultivated.
