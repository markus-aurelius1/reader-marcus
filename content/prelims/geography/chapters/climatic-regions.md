## Identification Matrix {#identification-matrix}

| Region | Signature | Cause | Vegetation clue | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Equatorial** (Af) | Hot all year; tiny annual range; heavy rain all year; {{afternoon convectional thunderstorms}} | High insolation + ITCZ | Multi-layer evergreen forest; epiphytes, lianas | Amazon, Congo, Indonesia–Malaysia |
| **Savanna** (Aw) | Hot; {{wet summer, dry winter}} | ITCZ migrates seasonally | Tall grass + scattered fire-resistant trees | Africa, Llanos, Campos, N Australia |
| **Hot desert** (BWh) | Rain usually < 25 cm; large daily range | Subtropical sinking air; cold currents, rain shadow | Xerophytes | Sahara, Arabian, Atacama, Namib, Australian |
| **Steppe** (BS) | Semi-arid; {{extreme temperature range}}; scant rain | Continental interior, rain shadow | Short grass; herding + wheat | Eurasian Steppe, Prairies, Pampas, Veld, Downs |
| **Mediterranean** (Cs) | {{Dry summer, wet winter}} | Summer subtropical high; winter westerlies shift equatorward | Evergreen shrubs; olive, vine, citrus | Mediterranean, California, central Chile, Cape, SW Australia |
| **Marine west coast** (Cfb) | Mild; {{small annual and daily range}}; {{rain all year}} | Onshore westerlies, cyclones, warm currents | Temperate broadleaf / mixed forest | W Europe, British Columbia, S Chile, Tasmania, New Zealand |

- **Monsoon** type → defined by {{seasonal reversal of winds}} → [Indian monsoon](@india-climate-soils-vegetation#monsoon).

## Rainforest Soil — Why It Is Poor {#rainforest-nutrients}

> [!FLOW] Heat + moisture → litter rots fast → roots take nutrients up at once → nutrients sit in the **living biomass**, not the soil → heavy rain leaches the rest.

- Lush forest ≠ fertile soil.
- Clearing removes the biomass and breaks the cycle → exposed soil leaches and erodes → forest {{regenerates slowly}}.

> [!TRAP] Rainforest soil is not "devoid of minerals" — it is **leached**, with nutrients locked in vegetation and litter.

Nutrient cycling as ecology → [Ecosystem functions](@prelims/environment/ecology-ecosystems#nutrient-cycles)

## Pairs That Get Confused {#comparisons}

| Pair | Difference |
|---|---|
| **Savanna vs Steppe** | Savanna → tropical, tall grass + trees, summer rain. Steppe → semi-arid, short grass, large annual range |
| **Mediterranean vs Marine west coast** | Dry summer / wet winter vs rain through the year |
| **Equatorial vs Monsoon** | No real dry season + daily convection vs wind reversal + one concentrated wet season |
| **Hot desert vs Steppe** | Arid vs semi-arid transition belt |

> [!TRAP] Do !!not!! assume steppe gets more rain than savanna. · Mediterranean winter rain comes from **shifting westerlies**, not the ITCZ.
