## Latitude, Longitude & Great Circles {#latitude-longitude}

| | Latitude (parallel) | Longitude (meridian) |
|---|---|---|
| Measures | Angular distance N / S of the Equator | Angular distance E / W of the Prime Meridian |
| Shape | Complete circles | Pole-to-pole **semicircles**, all equal |
| Length / spacing | Circles shrink poleward; 1° ≈ {{111 km}} everywhere | Gap between meridians → widest at Equator, {{zero at the poles}} |

- **Great circle** → plane passes through Earth's centre. Shortest surface route = great-circle arc.
- Equator = the only parallel that is a great circle. Every meridian pair = a great circle.
- Latitude questions ask "which State / place lies on it" → solve from the map, not from memory lists → [Tropic of Cancer States](@india-physiography#political-map).

## Time — Solar, Standard & Civil {#time}

- Earth turns 360° in ~24 h → {{15° = 1 hour}} → {{1° = 4 minutes}}.
- East of you → local time **ahead**. West → **behind**.
- **IST** → {{82°30′E}} → {{UTC + 5:30}}.

| | Geometric model | Real civil time |
|---|---|---|
| Zones | 24 one-hour zones | Irregular, **politically defined** |
| Offsets | Whole hours | Half-hour and quarter-hour offsets exist |
| Boundaries | Along meridians | Bend around States and territories |

- A country's zone count depends on longitudinal span **and overseas territories** → never infer it from mainland width alone.

> [!TRAP] "The world has exactly 24 time zones" → only the ideal model. · Daylight Saving Time has !!no universal date!! — rules vary by country and year.

### International Date Line

- Roughly 180°, zigzags around island States.
- Cross it going **west** (east → west, e.g. America → Asia) → calendar {{advances one day}}.
- Cross it going **east** → calendar {{goes back one day}}.

> [!TRAP] "Gain / lose a day" is ambiguous wording — convert it to the calendar operation above.

## Rotation, Revolution & Seasons {#rotation-revolution-seasons}

- Rotation → {{west to east}}.
- **Mean solar day** ≈ 24 h. **Sidereal day** ≈ {{23 h 56 min}} — measured against {{distant stars, not the Sun}}.
- Axial tilt → {{23.5°}} from the perpendicular to the orbital plane = {{66.5°}} with the orbital plane.

> [!FLOW] Seasons = revolution **+** axial tilt **+** axis staying parallel to itself through the orbit.

- Earth–Sun distance is !!not!! the primary cause of seasons.
- Seasonal change in day length → revolution on a tilted axis (rotation alone gives only day and night).

## Solstice, Equinox & Day Length {#solstice-equinox}

| Event | Subsolar point | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **June solstice** | {{Tropic of Cancer (23.5°N)}} | NH longest day · SH shortest |
| **December solstice** | {{Tropic of Capricorn (23.5°S)}} | Mirror image |
| **Equinoxes** | Near the Equator | Day ≈ night everywhere |

- June solstice at the **Arctic Circle** → about {{24 hours}} of daylight.
- **North Pole** → polar day approaching {{six months}}.
- Equator → day length ≈ 12 h all year. Contrast in day length **rises poleward**.
- Overhead Sun → only between the Tropics: on a Tropic {{once a year}}; inside the Tropics {{twice a year}}.
- "Exactly 12 h everywhere at equinox" → modified slightly by refraction and the Sun's disc.

> [!TRAP] "Everywhere beyond the Arctic Circle has six months of daylight" → wrong. Six months is the **pole**; the Circle itself gets ~24 h at the solstice.

## Mass Redistribution & Earth's Rotation {#mass-redistribution}

- Moving mass changes Earth's **moment of inertia** → changes rotation speed and the position of the axis (polar motion).
- Ice melt → water spreads from high latitudes towards lower latitudes → moment of inertia {{increases}} → rotation {{slows slightly}}, axis shifts slightly.
- Cause = mass + angular momentum. Solar flares do !!not!! redistribute Earth's mass.

Ice and sea-level side → [Oceanography](@oceanography#water-distribution)
