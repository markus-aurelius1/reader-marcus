## Indicators {#indicators}

| Indicator | Meaning | Trap |
|---|---|---|
| **Crude Birth Rate** | Live births per {{1,000 population}} in a year | Not per woman |
| **Crude Death Rate** | Deaths per 1,000 population in a year | |
| **Total Fertility Rate** | Average children a woman would have over her child-bearing years | {{Per woman}}, not per 1,000 |
| **Replacement fertility** | About {{2.1}} | Population keeps growing for a while after reaching it (momentum) |
| **Infant Mortality Rate** | Deaths under age 1 per {{1,000 live births}} | Not per 1,000 population |
| **Sex ratio** (India) | {{Females per 1,000 males}} | |
| **Child sex ratio** | Girls per 1,000 boys, age {{0–6}} | |
| **Density** | Persons per sq km | |
| **Dependency ratio** | Dependants ÷ working-age population × 100 | Age cut-offs differ by source |

- **Natural growth** = births − deaths.
- **Actual growth** = births − deaths {{+ in-migration − out-migration}}.
- Migration → push (no jobs, conflict, disaster) and pull (jobs, education, safety).
- Literacy and urbanisation → lower birth rates.

## Demographic Transition {#demographic-transition}

| Stage | Birth rate | Death rate | Growth |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I** | High | High | Low |
| **II** | High, then falling | {{Falls fast}} | {{High}} — population explosion |
| **III** | Low | Low | Low |

> [!FLOW] High birth + high death → high birth + low death → low birth + low death.

- Theory → {{Warren Thompson}} (later Notestein).

## Demographic Dividend, Dependency & Ageing {#dividend-ageing}

- **Dividend** → growth potential when the working-age share rises relative to dependants.
- Working age → {{15–64}} (UPSC, UN) or {{15–59}} (Indian sources, UPPCS). Read the question.
- It is a **potential** → needs health, education, {{skills}}, jobs, women in the workforce.
- Dependency ratio falls as the working-age share rises.
- **Ageing** ← falling fertility + longer life → more old-age dependants, pension and care costs.

> [!TRAP] Large population ≠ dividend. · Ageing ≠ shrinking — a country can age while still growing.

Jobs side of the dividend → [Employment indicators](@prelims/economy/poverty-employment#employment-indicators) · Population in Mains → [GS1 Society](@mains/gs1-society)

## Population Pyramids {#pyramids}

| Shape | Means |
|---|---|
| **Triangle** (broad base) | High fertility, young, fast growth |
| **Bell** | Low, stable growth |
| **Narrow base** | Low fertility, ageing, possible decline |

## Malthus & Theories {#theories}

- **Malthus** → population grows {{geometrically}}; food grows {{arithmetically}}.
- Checks → **preventive** (fewer births) · **positive** (famine, disease, war raise deaths).
- **Optimum population** → {{Edwin Cannan}}.

Malthus among economic thinkers → [Population theories](@prelims/economy/economic-thinkers#population-theories)

> [!VERIFY] "Social maladjustment theory" of population → attribution not confirmed (usually given as Henry George); the paper that asked it is poorly recorded.

- {{1872}} → first census, **not** held at one time everywhere (non-synchronous), under Lord Mayo.
- {{1881}} → first synchronous census, under Lord Ripon; every ten years since.

## Urbanisation & Settlement {#urbanisation}

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Statutory town** | Has a municipality, corporation, cantonment board or notified area |
| **Census town** | Population {{≥ 5,000}} + density {{≥ 400 per sq km}} + {{≥ 75%}} of male main workers outside agriculture |
| **City** | Town of 1 lakh or more |
| **Suburbanisation** | Homes shift to the city's edge; people still commute in |
| **Rurbanisation** | Term credited to {{G. S. Ghurye}} (UPPCS) |

- Urbanisation curve → slow start → {{stage 2 = acceleration}} → levelling off.
- Among religious communities, {{Jains}} are the most urbanised.

Municipal bodies in the Constitution → [Municipalities](@prelims/polity/local-government#municipalities-part-ix-a)
