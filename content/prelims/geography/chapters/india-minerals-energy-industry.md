## Classification & Mining Law {#classification-law}

| Basis | Categories |
|---|---|
| Geography | Metallic (ferrous / non-ferrous) · non-metallic · mineral fuels · atomic minerals |
| **Law (MMDR Act)** | {{Minor minerals}} vs the rest ("major") |
| Supply security | Critical / strategic minerals |

- "Minor" is a **legal label** — not small, cheap or non-metallic.
- Minor minerals → building stone, gravel, ordinary clay, ordinary sand + anything the {{Central Government notifies}}.
- **States** → make rules for minor-mineral concessions and against illegal mining.
- States auction blocks of non-coal major minerals too, under the Central law.
- **District Mineral Foundation (DMF)** → for the benefit of {{people and areas affected by mining}}. !!Not!! an exploration or licensing body.

> [!VERIFY] Which mineral is "major" or "minor" changes by notification. As tested in 2020: chromite, kyanite, sillimanite = major; bentonite = minor — recheck the current list.

## Coal {#coal}

- Rank → {{peat → lignite → bituminous → anthracite}} (carbon rises).
- Indian coal → mostly Gondwana; {{high ash, low sulphur}}.
- **Coking coal** (for steel) is short → India keeps importing coal despite large reserves.
- Nationalised → coking coal 1971–72, non-coking 1973. Blocks are allotted by **auction / allotment**, not lottery.

| Coalfield | State |
|---|---|
| **Raniganj** | {{West Bengal}} — oldest |
| **Jharia** · Bokaro · Karanpura · Ramgarh | {{Jharkhand}} — Damodar valley; Jharia = coking coal |
| **Talcher** · Ib Valley | {{Odisha}} |
| **Korba** | Chhattisgarh |
| **Singrauli** | MP – UP |
| **Singareni** | Telangana |
| **Namchik–Namphuk** | Arunachal Pradesh (Tertiary) |
| **Mawlong** | {{Meghalaya}} |
| **Neyveli** | Tamil Nadu — {{lignite}} |

**Coal Controller's Organisation** → coal statistics · monitors captive coal / lignite blocks · hears objections on acquiring coal-bearing land.

**Coal gasification** → coal + steam + oxygen → {{syngas (CO + H₂)}} → urea / ammonia, methanol, synthetic gas, ethanol. !!Not!! nitroglycerine.

## Oil, Gas & Unconventional Gas {#hydrocarbons}

- Oil and gas → sedimentary basins: Assam, Gujarat (Cambay), Mumbai offshore, Krishna–Godavari, Cauvery.

| Resource | Held in | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Coal-bed methane** | Coal seams | Methane-rich — not pure methane |
| **Shale gas** | Fine shale rock; needs {{horizontal drilling + fracking}} | Mostly methane — not just propane and butane. Basins → {{Cambay, Cauvery, Krishna–Godavari}} |
| **Methane hydrate** | Ice-like cages under the sea floor and permafrost | Warming can release methane |

> [!UPPCS] Refineries: **Vadinar** {{Gujarat}} · **Manali** {{Tamil Nadu}} · **Numaligarh** {{Assam}} · **Tatipaka** {{Andhra Pradesh}}.

## Atomic & Beach-Sand Minerals {#atomic-beach-minerals}

| Mineral | Gives |
|---|---|
| **Ilmenite, rutile** | {{Titanium}} |
| **Monazite** | {{Thorium + rare earths}} |
| **Zircon** | Zirconium |

- Found in patches on **both** coasts — Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Odisha. !!Not!! along the whole coast.
- Monazite → controlled atomic mineral; only government bodies process and export it.
- **Thorium** → India has plenty; it is {{fertile, not fissile}} — it breeds U-233.
- India has uranium (Jaduguda, Tummalapalle). Power reactors use natural or low-enriched uranium.

**Critical minerals** → needed for technology and energy transition, with risky supply. Lithium, cobalt, nickel, graphite, rare earths. India depends on imports for lithium, cobalt and nickel.
