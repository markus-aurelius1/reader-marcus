## Modes — Which Suits What {#modes}

| Mode | Best for | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Road | Short haul, door to door | Costly for bulk over distance |
| Rail | Bulk freight, long distance | Sparse in hills, desert, forest |
| Water (inland, coastal) | Heavy, low-value cargo; {{cheapest per tonne}} | Slow; needs navigable water |
| Air | Light, high-value, perishable | Costly |
| Pipeline | Oil, gas, slurry | One commodity |

## Railways {#railways}

- Dense in the northern plains; sparse in the Himalaya, NE, desert.
- **Konkan Railway** → Maharashtra – Goa – Karnataka, through the Western Ghats.
- **Kavach** → {{indigenous}} Automatic Train Protection; uses RFID tags on the track. !!Not!! German.
- **National Rail Plan** → long-term capacity plan.

> [!UPPCS] Zone HQ: **Northern** {{New Delhi}} · **North Eastern** {{Gorakhpur}} · **South Eastern** {{Garden Reach, Kolkata}} · **North Central** Prayagraj · **East Central** Hajipur.

## Bridges & Tunnels {#bridges-tunnels}

- **Atal Tunnel** → 9.02 km; under Rohtang in the {{Pir Panjal}}, Himachal; Manali ↔ Lahaul. Longest highway tunnel **above 10,000 ft** — !!not!! the world's longest.
- **New Pamban Bridge** → Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu; India's first {{vertical-lift}} railway sea bridge.
