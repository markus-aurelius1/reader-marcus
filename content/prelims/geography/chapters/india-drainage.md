## Drainage Logic {#drainage-logic}

| | Himalayan rivers | Peninsular rivers |
|---|---|---|
| Flow | {{Perennial}} — snow + rain | Mostly rain-fed, seasonal |
| Valley | Deep gorges; meanders in the plains | Broad, shallow, graded |
| Course | Antecedent; river capture | Fixed, mature |
| Mouth | Large deltas | East → deltas · West → {{estuaries}} |

| Pattern | Sign |
|---|---|
| **Dendritic** | Tree-like; uniform rock |
| **Trellis** | Tributaries join at right angles; hard and soft rock alternate |
| **Rectangular** | Controlled by joints and faults |
| **Radial** | Streams flow outward from a height → {{Amarkantak}} |
| **Centripetal** | Streams flow into a basin → Rajasthan |
| **Antecedent** | River older than the mountains it cuts → {{Indus, Sutlej, Brahmaputra}} |

- Rivers have shifted between the Indus and Ganga systems in the geological past (old courses of the Yamuna / Sutlej).

Rivers under their ancient names → [Rivers in ancient texts](@prelims/history-culture/historical-geography#ancient-rivers)

## Indus System {#indus}

- **Indus** → Tibet, near Kailash–Mansarovar → Ladakh → Pakistan → Arabian Sea.

| River | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **Jhelum** | Verinag spring → Kashmir Valley → flows through {{Wular Lake}} |
| **Chenab** | {{Chandra + Bhaga}} meet at Tandi |
| **Ravi** | Joins the Chenab |
| **Beas** | Near Rohtang → joins the Sutlej at {{Harike}} |
| **Sutlej** | Tibet → enters India at {{Shipki La}}; antecedent |

- Order of joining → Jhelum and Ravi join the **Chenab** → Beas joins the **Sutlej** → Chenab + Sutlej = Panjnad → Indus.
- **Shyok** and **Nubra** → Indus tributaries in Ladakh.

## Ganga System {#ganga}

- **Bhagirathi** → from {{Gaumukh, Gangotri Glacier}}. The river is called **Ganga** only from {{Devprayag}}.

**Panch Prayag** — Alaknanda meets, in order downstream:

| Prayag | Joined by |
|---|---|
| **Vishnuprayag** | {{Dhauliganga}} |
| **Nandprayag** | {{Nandakini}} |
| **Karnaprayag** | {{Pindar}} |
| **Rudraprayag** | {{Mandakini}} |
| **Devprayag** | {{Bhagirathi}} → Ganga |

- **Left bank** (from the north) → Ramganga, Gomti, Ghaghara, Gandak, Kosi.
- **Right bank** → Yamuna, Son. (Damodar joins the Hooghly.)
- Below Prayagraj, west → east → {{Gomti → Ghaghara → Gandak → Kosi}}.
- **Yamuna** → Yamunotri (Bandarpunch) → joins at Prayagraj. Its right-bank tributaries → {{Chambal, Sindh, Betwa, Ken}}.
- **Ghaghara** = Karnali (rises in Tibet, via Nepal). **Gandak** = Narayani. **Kosi** → shifts course, floods.
- **Bengal delta** → partly "moribund" (old, inactive distributaries in the west).

Rivers inside Uttar Pradesh → [UP rivers](@up-geography#rivers)

## Brahmaputra System {#brahmaputra}

- Tibet → **Yarlung Tsangpo**, flowing east.
- U-turn around {{Namcha Barwa}} (eastern syntaxis) → enters Arunachal as **Siang / Dihang**.
- Siang + {{Dibang + Lohit}} → Brahmaputra in Assam.
- Tributaries → Subansiri, Kameng (Jia Bharali), Manas (north bank); Lohit, Dibang (east).
- Huge silt load → braided channel, shifting course, floods.
- **Teesta** → rises in Sikkim; tributary {{Rangeet}}; joins the Brahmaputra in Bangladesh.
- The Irrawaddy and Mekong also turn sharply south near the eastern Himalaya — young fold ranges force the bend.

## Groundwater & Interlinking {#groundwater}

- Groundwater depends on rock porosity, aquifer thickness and recharge — not on rainfall alone.
- **Indo-Gangetic alluvium** → huge storage, heavy pumping. **Peninsular hard rock** → limited, in fractures.
- India → world's largest user of groundwater; most of it goes to {{irrigation}}.
- Regulator → {{Central Ground Water Authority}}, set up under the Environment (Protection) Act.
- Excess **riverbed sand mining** → riverbed and water table fall, river salinity and pollution rise.
- Natural arsenic, fluoride in groundwater → [Groundwater contaminants](@prelims/environment/pollution#heavy-metals).
- **Interlinking** → move water from surplus to deficit basins.

> [!DYN] Named interlinking projects, withdrawal percentages and "over-exploited block" counts change — keep only the concept.
