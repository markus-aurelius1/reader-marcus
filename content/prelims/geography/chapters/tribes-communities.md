## Practices & Languages {#practices-languages}

- **Changpa** → Scheduled Tribe; rear yak, sheep and the {{Pashmina (Changthangi) goat}}.
- **Living-root bridges** → {{Meghalaya}} (Khasi, Jaintia hills); grown from the roots of rubber-fig trees, stronger with age.
- **Manganiyar** → folk-music tradition of NW India.
- **Tribal languages** → {{Halbi}} (Bastar, Chhattisgarh) · {{Ho}} (Jharkhand–Odisha; Munda family) · {{Kui}} (Odisha, Kondh; Dravidian).

> [!TRAP] Halbi, Ho, Kui are languages — !!not!! dances or instruments.
