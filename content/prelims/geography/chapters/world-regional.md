## Map Grammar {#map-grammar}

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Strait** | Narrow natural **water** passage joining two seas |
| **Canal** | Artificial waterway |
| **Isthmus** | Narrow strip of **land** joining two landmasses |
| **Landlocked** | No sea or ocean coast (may still touch an inland sea) |

- Pair questions → check in order: feature type → region → bordering countries → waters joined → direction.
