## Six Divisions {#divisions}

1. Himalayan Mountains
2. Northern Plains (Indus–Ganga–Brahmaputra)
3. Peninsular Plateau
4. Indian Desert
5. Coastal Plains
6. Islands

| | Himalaya | Peninsular Block |
|---|---|---|
| Age | {{Young fold mountains}} | Ancient, stable shield |
| Relief | Steep, deep gorges, landslides, earthquakes | Residual hills, plateaus, {{broad shallow valleys}} |
| State | Still rising | Stable; faulted / rifted in places |

General process → [Plate boundaries](@geomorphology#plate-tectonics)

## Himalaya {#himalaya}

**North → south**

| Belt | Features |
|---|---|
| **Trans-Himalaya** | {{Karakoram, Ladakh, Zanskar}} |
| **Greater Himalaya (Himadri)** | Highest, most continuous; glaciers |
| **Lesser Himalaya (Himachal)** | {{Pir Panjal, Dhauladhar}}; hill stations, valleys |
| **Shiwalik** | Youngest, outermost; loose sediments; {{duns}} lie between Shiwalik and Lesser Himalaya |

- West → east → Kashmir / Punjab Himalaya → Himachal / Kumaon → Nepal → Assam Himalaya.
- Syntaxial bends → west {{Nanga Parbat}} · east {{Namcha Barwa}}.
- Eastern hills (Purvanchal) → Patkai → Naga → Manipur → Mizo (Lushai).
- **Siachen** → Karakoram; {{north of the Nubra valley}}, east of the Saltoro ridge.

**Evidence that the Himalaya are young** → deep gorges · rivers that cut across and U-turn · parallel ranges · steep unstable slopes · earthquakes · marine sedimentary rocks with fossils at height.

## Peninsular Plateau {#peninsular-plateau}

- Central Highlands → Malwa, Bundelkhand, Baghelkhand, Chota Nagpur.
- Deccan Plateau → south of the Narmada; **Deccan Trap** = basalt from fissure eruptions → [black soil](@india-climate-soils-vegetation#soils).

| | Western Ghats | Eastern Ghats |
|---|---|---|
| Height | Higher | Lower |
| Continuity | {{Continuous}} escarpment | {{Broken}} by east-flowing rivers |
| Rain | Strong orographic barrier | Weak |

- Parts of the **west coast** show submergence. Deep V-shaped valleys with fast rivers are a Himalayan feature, !!not!! a peninsular one.

Minerals of the shield → [Mineral belts](@india-minerals-energy-industry#mineral-belts)

## Coasts {#coasts}

| West coast | East coast |
|---|---|
| Narrow; submergent; {{estuaries}} | Broad; {{large deltas}} |
| Konkan → Kanara → Malabar | Northern Circars → Coromandel |
| Short, swift rivers | Long rivers |
| Backwaters and lagoons of Kerala | Chilika, Pulicat |

- **Chandipur** (Odisha) → sea retreats up to several km at low tide, twice a day.
- **Rann of Kutch** → salt marsh; formed as the sea withdrew and silt filled a shallow arm of the sea.

Ports and harbours → [Ports](@india-transport#ports)

## Islands & Channels {#islands}

**Andaman & Nicobar** (Bay of Bengal) → island arc continuing the Myanmar–Indonesia mountain chain.

| Channel | Separates |
|---|---|
| **Ten Degree Channel** | {{Andaman group from Nicobar group}} |
| **Duncan Passage** | South Andaman from Little Andaman |
| **Great Channel** | Great Nicobar from Sumatra |
| **Nine Degree Channel** | {{Minicoy from the rest of Lakshadweep}} |
| **Eight Degree Channel** | {{Minicoy from Maldives}} |

- **Barren Island** → India's only active volcano; in the Andaman Sea, east of the main Andaman chain. !!Not!! near Great Nicobar.
- **Narcondam** → dormant / extinct volcanic island.
- **Lakshadweep** (Arabian Sea) → {{coral atolls}} → [Coral reefs](@prelims/environment/marine-ecology#corals).
- Southernmost point of India → {{Indira Point, Great Nicobar}}. Mainland → Kanyakumari.

Island communities → [Andaman & Nicobar tribes](@tribes-communities#andaman-nicobar)

## Political Map {#political-map}

- Easternmost State → {{Arunachal Pradesh}}. Westernmost → {{Gujarat}}.
- **Tropic of Cancer** crosses 8 States, west → east: {{Gujarat → Rajasthan → Madhya Pradesh → Chhattisgarh → Jharkhand → West Bengal → Tripura → Mizoram}}.
- **IST meridian 82°30′E** → passes near Mirzapur (UP).
- Route questions ("fewest States from Kohima to Kottayam") → count along **touching States**, not a straight line.
- **Durand Line** → {{Afghanistan–Pakistan}}. !!Not!! an India–Afghanistan line.
- **Radcliffe Line** → India–Pakistan → [Radcliffe](@prelims/history-culture/communal-politics-ina-partition#radcliffe). **McMahon Line** → India–China (eastern sector).

How States were formed and renamed → [Reorganisation of States](@prelims/polity/union-states-citizenship#reorganisation-of-states)
