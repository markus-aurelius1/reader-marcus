## Monsoon {#monsoon}

- Monsoon climate = {{seasonal reversal of winds}}.

> [!FLOW] Land heats → ITCZ / monsoon trough shifts north → SE trades cross the Equator → turn into SW winds → Arabian Sea and Bay of Bengal branches. Tibetan Plateau heating and upper-air jets help.

| Branch | Path | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Arabian Sea** | Hits the Western Ghats | Heavy rain on the windward side; {{rain shadow}} inland. Runs **parallel** to the Aravalli → little rain in west Rajasthan |
| **Bay of Bengal** | Hits the NE hills, turns west up the Ganga plain | Very heavy rain in Meghalaya; rain {{decreases east → west}} |

- Monsoon lasts longer in the {{south}} — arrives earlier, leaves later. Duration falls from south to north.
- **Tamil Nadu** stays dry in the SW monsoon → rain shadow of the Western Ghats + coast parallel to the Bay branch. Gets rain from the {{NE (retreating) monsoon}}.
- **Winter rain in NW India** → {{western disturbances}} (carried by westerlies from the Mediterranean); weakens eastward.
- **Andaman & Nicobar** → humid tropical; rain from **both** SW and NE monsoons; peak is !!not!! December–May.

Why winds reverse, ENSO and IOD → [ENSO & IOD](@climatology#enso-iod)

## Soils {#soils}

Formation → parent rock + climate + relief + organisms + time.

| Soil | Features | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Alluvial** | Transported; fertile; khadar (new), bhangar (old) | Northern plains, deltas |
| **Black (regur)** | From {{Deccan basalt}}; clayey, holds moisture, cracks when dry; rich in lime, iron, magnesia; poor in N, P | Maharashtra, MP, Gujarat, Telangana |
| **Red & yellow** | Red from {{iron oxide}}; poor in humus, N, P | East and south peninsula, on crystalline rock |
| **Laterite** | {{Heavy leaching}} in hot, wet climate; rich in {{iron and aluminium}}; poor in N, P, K | Western and Eastern Ghats, NE hills; cashew, tapioca |
| **Arid** | Sandy, saline, little humus; kankar below | West Rajasthan, Gujarat |
| **Saline / alkaline** | Salts at the surface; poor drainage | Dry and over-irrigated tracts |
| **Peaty / marshy** | High organic matter, waterlogged, acidic | Kerala, Sundarbans |
| **Forest / mountain** | Varies with height and slope | Himalaya |

Soils and crops → [Crops](@prelims/agriculture/crops) · Erosion, salinity and conservation → [Soil conservation](@prelims/agriculture/soil-conservation#conservation-methods)

> [!TRAP] Laterite is !!not!! rich in nitrogen or potash, and is !!not!! typical of Rajasthan or UP. · Black soil is !!not!! Himalayan.

## Natural Vegetation {#vegetation}

| Forest | Rainfall / sign | Where | Species |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tropical wet evergreen** | Very heavy rain, no dry season; many layers | Western Ghats (windward), NE India, A&N | Ebony, rosewood, mahogany |
| **Semi-evergreen** | Transition | Edges of the above | Mixed |
| **Tropical moist deciduous** | 100–200 cm; sheds leaves in the dry season | Foothills, east and central India | {{Teak}}; sal in the north and east |
| **Tropical dry deciduous** | 70–100 cm | Interior peninsula, plains | Teak, sal, open canopy |
| **Thorn / scrub** | < 70 cm | Rajasthan, Gujarat, rain-shadow Deccan | Babul, acacia, euphorbia |
| **Montane** | Changes with altitude | Himalaya, southern hills | Sub-tropical → temperate conifers → alpine meadow |
| **Mangrove** | Tidal, saline | Sundarbans, east-coast deltas, A&N, Gujarat | Sundari, Rhizophora, Avicennia |

- **A&N** → mangrove + evergreen + deciduous together.
- Wet evergreen → Arunachal, Mizoram. !!Not!! Himachal Pradesh.
- Deciduous trees → teak, sal, mahua, jackfruit.
- Himalayan vegetation changes with height because temperature and moisture change.

> [!DYN] Forest-cover percentages and State rankings come from each India State of Forest Report — never a permanent fact.
