## Earth's Interior {#earth-interior}

| Layer | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **Crust** | Continental → thicker, less dense, older · Oceanic → thinner, denser, **basaltic**, younger |
| **Mantle** | Down to ~{{2,900 km}}; solid, but convects over geological time |
| **Core** | Fe–Ni · outer core {{liquid}} · inner core {{solid}} |
| **Lithosphere** | Rigid crust + uppermost mantle → broken into plates |
| **Asthenosphere** | {{Solid but weak / ductile}} upper mantle below the lithosphere; local partial melt; seismic low-velocity zone |

- **Direct** evidence → mines, boreholes, volcanic material.
- **Indirect** → seismic waves, gravity, magnetism, meteorites, heat flow.
- **Kola Superdeep Borehole** → on land, Kola Peninsula (Russia). !!Not!! ocean drilling.

> [!TRAP] Asthenosphere is !!not!! "semi-liquid" — it is solid rock that flows slowly.

## Seismic Waves & Earthquakes {#seismic-waves}

| Wave | Motion | Speed | Travels through | Tells us |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P** | Compressional / longitudinal | Fastest | Solid, liquid, gas | Refracts at the core boundary |
| **S** | Shear / transverse | Slower | {{Solids only}} | Stops at the outer core → {{outer core is liquid}} |
| **Surface** (Love, Rayleigh) | Along the surface | Slowest | Surface | Most damage |

- P-wave shadow zone → about {{103°–143°}} from the epicentre. S waves absent beyond ~103°.
- **Focus / hypocentre** = rupture point inside Earth. **Epicentre** = surface point above it.
- **Magnitude** = energy released at source. **Intensity** = effects felt at a place.
- +1 magnitude → {{10×}} wave amplitude, about {{31.6×}} energy.

> [!TRAP] "+1 magnitude = 10× energy" is wrong — 10× is amplitude.

## Continental Drift → Plate Tectonics {#plate-tectonics}

**Continental drift (Wegener) — evidence**
- Jig-saw fit of coastlines · rock and mountain belts continuing across the Atlantic.
- Matching fossils · tillite (old glacial deposits) · Gondwana sediments across southern continents.
- {{Placer gold of Ghana with source veins in Brazil}}.
- Weak point → the driving forces he proposed were inadequate.

**Sea-floor spreading**
- New crust forms at mid-ocean ridges → rocks get **older away from the ridge**.
- Symmetric magnetic-reversal stripes on both sides of the ridge.
- Ridges → shallow quakes, high heat flow. Deep-focus quakes → {{subduction zones}}.

| Boundary | Lithosphere | Landforms | Quakes / volcanoes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Divergent** | Created | Mid-ocean ridge, continental rift | Shallow quakes; basaltic lava |
| **Ocean–continent convergent** | Oceanic plate subducts | Trench + volcanic arc / fold belt | Shallow → deep; explosive |
| **Ocean–ocean convergent** | One plate subducts | Trench + {{island arc}} | Shallow → deep; volcanic |
| **Continent–continent** | Crust thickens | {{Fold mountains}}, high plateaux | Strong quakes; little volcanism |
| **Transform** | Neither created nor destroyed | Strike-slip fault | Shallow quakes; little volcanism |

## Rocks & Weathering {#rocks-weathering}

| Rock | Formed by | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Igneous** (primary) | Cooling of magma / lava | Intrusive, coarse → granite, gabbro · Extrusive, fine → {{basalt}}, rhyolite, andesite |
| **Sedimentary** | Weathering → transport → deposition → lithification | Clastic → sandstone, shale, conglomerate · Chemical → rock salt, gypsum, geyserite · Organic → coal, chalk, many limestones |
| **Metamorphic** | Heat, pressure, fluids — **without melting** | Limestone → {{marble}} · sandstone → {{quartzite}} · shale → slate → phyllite → schist · granite → gneiss |

> [!TRAP] Limestone has several origins (chemical and organic) — not simply "mechanically formed". · Geyserite is **chemical**, not organic. · "Coal → diamond" is !!not!! a standard metamorphic sequence.

| Weathering | Processes |
|---|---|
| Physical | Thermal expansion, frost action, salt crystals, pressure release |
| Chemical | Solution, {{carbonation}}, hydrolysis, oxidation, hydration |
| Biological | Roots, organisms, organic acids |

- Rain + CO₂ → carbonic acid → carbonation. Dissolved O₂ → oxidation. So rainfall drives **both**.

Fossil-fuel and mineral side of rock systems → [Rock systems](@india-minerals-energy-industry#rock-systems)

## Volcanoes & Mountains {#volcanoes-mountains}

- **Magma** = below the surface. **Lava** = erupted at the surface.
- Eruption products → lava · pyroclasts, ash, dust · gases (mostly {{water vapour}}, with CO₂, SO₂ and others).

| Type | Lava | Form |
|---|---|---|
| **Shield** | Fluid basalt | Broad, gentle; quiet eruptions |
| **Composite / strato** | Viscous, intermediate | Layers of lava + ash; explosive |
| **Fissure** | Flood basalt | Lava plateaux → [Deccan Trap](@india-physiography#peninsular-plateau) |

- Volcano and earthquake belts follow **plate margins**; hotspots are the intraplate exception.

| Mountain type | Cause | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Fold** | Compression at convergent margins | Himalaya, {{Alps}}, {{Andes}}, Appalachians (old fold) |
| **Block / fault** | Uplifted blocks between faults | {{Vosges, Black Forest}} |

> [!UPPCS] Volcano → country: **Etna** {{Italy}} · **Rainier** {{USA}} · **Parícutin** {{Mexico}} · **Apo** {{Philippines}}.

## Earth History & Geological Time {#earth-history}

- Hierarchy → {{Eon → Era → Period → Epoch → Age}}.

| Unit | Span |
|---|---|
| Hadean | ~4.54 – 4.0 billion years |
| Archean | 4.0 – 2.5 billion |
| Proterozoic | 2.5 – 0.541 billion |
| **Phanerozoic** | ~541 million years → present |
| Paleozoic · Mesozoic · Cenozoic | 541–252 · 252–66 · 66 million → present |
| Quaternary · Holocene | ~2.58 million · ~11,700 years → present |

- Early atmosphere → {{no free oxygen}}; volcanic outgassing gave H₂O, CO₂, N₂.
- Photosynthetic microbes slowly oxygenated ocean and air → life changed the atmosphere.
- **Geomagnetic reversals** → at {{irregular intervals}}; recorded in rocks (palaeomagnetism).
- Continental drift + glacial cycles → long-run controls on where species evolved and spread.

> [!TRAP] "Reversals occur every 2–3 lakh years" → wrong, there is no fixed period.
