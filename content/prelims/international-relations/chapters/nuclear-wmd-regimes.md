## NPT {#npt}

- **Nuclear-Weapon States under the NPT** → only five: {{USA, Russia, UK, France, China}}.
- Outside the NPT → {{India, Israel, Pakistan}}.
- India's objection → the treaty makes nuclear weapons legitimate for five states only.

> [!TRAP] Joining an export-control regime does !!not!! change a country's NPT status.

## IAEA Safeguards {#iaea}

- **IAEA** → {{Vienna}}; autonomous body in the UN system; promotes peaceful nuclear use + verifies non-proliferation (safeguards).
- Why some Indian reactors are under safeguards and others not → those using {{imported uranium / fuel}} are; those on domestic fuel are not.
- **Additional Protocol** → wider information and inspection access for the IAEA.
  - India → signed {{2009}}, ratified {{2014}}.
  - Covers India's {{civilian}} facilities placed under safeguards.

> [!TRAP] Additional Protocol does !!not!! mean inspection of military sites, and does !!not!! bring automatic NSG membership.

Reactors and fuel → [Nuclear energy](@prelims/science-tech/energy-technologies#nuclear)

## Export-Control Regimes {#export-controls}

| Regime | Controls | India |
|---|---|---|
| **NSG** (Nuclear Suppliers Group) | Nuclear material, equipment, technology | {{Not a member}} |
| **MTCR** | Missiles and unmanned delivery systems | Member since {{2016}} |
| **Australia Group** | {{Chemical and biological}} weapon-related exports | Member since {{2018}} |
| **Wassenaar Arrangement** | {{Conventional arms + dual-use}} goods and technology | Member since {{2017}} |

- All four → informal, voluntary groups of supplier countries; no treaty.
- NSG membership → access to advanced nuclear technology and trade. Does !!not!! make a country an NPT member.

> [!TRAP] Wassenaar is !!not!! an OECD body, and its aim is !!not!! the same as the Australia Group's.

> [!VERIFY] Founding years, member counts and secretariat details of the four regimes are thin in the source material — only the domain and India's status above are safe. India's joining years are from public records, not from the pack.

Missile types these rules cover → [Missiles](@prelims/science-tech/defence-technology#missiles)

## Chemical & Biological Weapons {#chemical-biological}

- **Chemical Weapons Convention** → bans development, production, stockpiling, transfer and use; stocks destroyed under verification.
- **OPCW** → implements the CWC; {{The Hague}}.
  - Monitors the chemical industry.
  - Helps and protects States Parties against chemical threats.
- **Biological Weapons Convention** → bans biological and toxin weapons.

> [!TRAP] OPCW is !!not!! a European Union body.

## Arms Control & Nuclear Security {#arms-control}

- **New START** → {{USA–Russia}} bilateral treaty cutting strategic nuclear arms.
- **Nuclear Security Summits** → !!not!! held under the UN.
- **International Panel on Fissile Materials** → independent experts; !!not!! an organ of the IAEA.

> [!DYN] Status of New START (suspension, expiry), treaty withdrawals, new entrants to any regime.
