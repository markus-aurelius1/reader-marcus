## What Kind of Body? {#classifier}

Ask this before anything else.

> [!FLOW] UN principal organ → specialised agency → UN programme / fund / office → inter-governmental body → treaty body → NGO / non-profit → temporary commission or initiative

- The usual question is "what kind of body is it", !!not!! "what does it do".
- {{Consultative status with ECOSOC}} does !!not!! make an NGO a UN agency.
- Private initiatives are often given a false UN / World Bank / IEA parent in the options.

UN's own agencies and programmes → [Agencies vs programmes](@un-system-law#agencies)

## Rights & Relief NGOs {#rights-relief}

| Body | What it is | Not |
|---|---|---|
| **Amnesty International** | Global {{human-rights}} movement; NGO; founded 1961, London | Not a UN agency |
| **Médecins Sans Frontières** (Doctors Without Borders) | Independent medical-humanitarian NGO — conflicts, epidemics, disasters; founded 1971, France | Not a WHO division, EU agency or UN agency |

## World Toilet Organization {#world-toilet-organization}

- NGO for sanitation awareness; founded {{2001}}; HQ {{Singapore}}.
- Runs → World Toilet Summit, World Toilet College.
- **World Toilet Day** (19 November) → later adopted by the UN.
- Not a UN agency, even with ECOSOC consultative status.

> [!VERIFY] The exam question on this body is recorded incomplete — it shows the topic is asked; its answer key is not used.

## Climate Group {#climate-group}

- **The Climate Group** → international {{non-profit}}; builds business and government networks for climate action.
- **EP100** → run by The Climate Group. !!Not!! by the International Energy Agency.
- **Under2 Coalition** → states and regions; The Climate Group is its {{secretariat}}.

Other climate initiatives → [Initiative → who runs it](@prelims/environment/international-conventions#initiatives)

## Ocean Bodies {#ocean-bodies}

| Body | Nature |
|---|---|
| **Global Ocean Commission** | Independent commission (2013–16) on high-seas governance; gives {{no licences}} |
| **International Seabed Authority** | Inter-governmental; created by {{UNCLOS}}; grants seabed exploration contracts in the Area |

Zones and the Area → [Law of the Sea](@un-system-law#unclos)

## Rapid Elimination {#elimination}

- Amnesty · MSF · World Toilet Organization · Climate Group → none is a UN specialised agency.
- "Global / World / International" in a name proves nothing about UN status.
- Licence, treaty, binding rule → look for an inter-governmental or treaty body.
- Campaign, network, summit, college → likely an NGO.

> [!DYN] Award winners, yearly rankings and reports of these bodies — current layer.
