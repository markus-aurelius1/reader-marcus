## G20 & G7 {#g20-g7}

- **G20** → born {{1999}}, after the Asian financial crisis, as a forum of {{finance ministers and central-bank governors}}; leaders' summits since {{2008}}.
  - Rotating presidency; Finance Track + Sherpa Track; {{no permanent secretariat or HQ}}.
  - Countries → Argentina, Australia, Brazil, Canada, China, France, Germany, India, Indonesia, Italy, Japan, Mexico, Russia, Saudi Arabia, South Africa, South Korea, Türkiye, UK, USA + the European Union.
- **G7** → informal group of seven advanced democracies; EU takes part; rotating presidency; no secretariat.

> [!TRAP] G20 did !!not!! start as a leaders' summit. · The G20 Common Framework is about debt — a separate thing from the grouping's identity.

Debt treatment → [Reports & debt governance](@prelims/economy/international-institutions#reports-and-debt)

> [!DYN] Presidency, troika, summit host, theme, logo, guest countries; African Union's seat in the G20 (since 2023). UPPCS has asked the G20 logo and the UP cities that hosted G20 meetings; also the Voice of Global South Summit.

## BRICS {#brics}

- BRIC foreign ministers first met → 2006.
- First summit → {{2009, Yekaterinburg}} (Russia).
- South Africa → invited 2010, joined {{2011}} → BRICS.
- **Fortaleza Declaration, 2014** → {{New Development Bank}} + {{Contingent Reserve Arrangement}}.
  - NDB → HQ {{Shanghai}}; infrastructure and sustainable-development loans.
  - CRA → short-term liquidity / balance-of-payments support.

> [!TRAP] First summit was !!not!! in Rio de Janeiro. · NDB is !!not!! an APEC body.

> [!DYN] Full-member list, partner countries, latest entrants, summit host and theme — expanded since 2024; always date-stamp.

NDB beside AIIB and ADB → [Regional banks](@prelims/economy/international-institutions#regional-banks)

## ASEAN & East Asia Summit {#asean-eas}

- **ASEAN** → {{1967, Bangkok Declaration}}; HQ {{Jakarta}}.
- ASEAN's free-trade partners → {{China, Japan, South Korea, India, Australia, New Zealand}}.
- **RCEP** → built around ASEAN and those partners; India stayed out.
- **East Asia Summit** → {{2005}}; ASEAN-led leaders' forum; India a member.

> [!DYN] ASEAN member count (Timor-Leste admitted in 2025) and the EAS count — sources disagree after the expansion; check before the exam.

Trade-agreement types → [Trade agreements](@prelims/economy/international-institutions#trade-agreements) · Roads and ports linking India to ASEAN → [Corridors](@bilateral-connectivity#corridors)

## NATO & Arctic Council {#nato-arctic}

- **NATO** → {{1949}}, North Atlantic (Washington) Treaty; HQ {{Brussels}}; {{12}} founders.
  - ==Article 5== → {{collective defence}}: attack on one = attack on all.
- **Arctic Council** → {{1996, Ottawa Declaration}}.
  - Eight members → {{Canada, Denmark, Finland, Iceland, Norway, Russia, Sweden, USA}}.
  - Deals with environment and sustainable development — !!not!! military security.

> [!DYN] NATO member count and latest entrants (Finland 2023, Sweden 2024) — questions now test membership country by country.

India's Arctic station → [Polar stations](@prelims/geography/world-regional#polar)

## Mekong–Ganga & Turkic States {#mgc-ots}

- **Mekong–Ganga Cooperation** → {{2000, Vientiane}}; six → India + {{Cambodia, Laos, Myanmar, Thailand, Vietnam}}.
  - Culture, commerce, connectivity. No permanent HQ.
- **Organization of Turkic States** → Turkic Council {{2009}}; renamed {{2021}}; Turkic-speaking states.

> [!TRAP] Bangladesh and China are !!not!! in Mekong–Ganga Cooperation.

> [!DYN] OTS members (Türkiye, Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan) and observers.

## European Union {#eu}

| Body | Role | Trap |
|---|---|---|
| **European Commission** | Proposes laws; runs policies and budget; enforces EU law | Not the heads of government |
| **European Council** | Heads of state / government → overall political direction | ≠ {{Council of the European Union}} |
| **European Parliament** | Directly elected every {{5}} years; makes law with the Council of the EU | Not an executive |
| **European Central Bank** | Central bank of the {{eurozone}}; {{Frankfurt}} | EU ≠ eurozone |
| **European Stability Mechanism** | Financial help to eurozone states in crisis; {{intergovernmental}}; Luxembourg | Not simply "an EU agency" |
| **Stability and Growth Pact** | Fiscal-discipline rules — deficit and debt | |

- Parliament → official seat {{Strasbourg}}; work in Brussels; staff in Luxembourg.
- **Digital Single Market Strategy** → EU.
- **Trade and Technology Council** → {{USA–EU}} forum.
- **Global Climate Change Alliance** → EU initiative.

> [!TRAP] There is no single "EU headquarters" fact — ask which institution.

> [!DYN] EU member count (27 after the UK left), eurozone and Schengen counts. Common non-members in options → UK, Norway, Switzerland, Iceland, Türkiye.
