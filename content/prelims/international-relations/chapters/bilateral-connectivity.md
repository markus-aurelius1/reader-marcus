## Policy Frames {#policy}

- **Look East Policy** → make India a player in East Asia + {{restore historical and cultural ties}} with South-East and East Asia.
  - "To fill the vacuum left by the end of the Cold War" → !!not!! accepted.
- **Act East Policy** → {{2014}}; successor to Look East; ASEAN and the Indo-Pacific.
- Logic → North-East India → Myanmar → ASEAN.
- **India–Africa Forum Summit** → structured India–Africa cooperation; first in 2008; {{2015 was the third}}.

> [!TRAP] The India–Africa Forum Summit was !!not!! started by Nehru in 1951.

ASEAN and India's status in it → [ASEAN & East Asia Summit](@groupings#asean-eas)

## Corridors {#corridors}

| Corridor | Route | India angle / trap |
|---|---|---|
| **Belt and Road (BRI)** | {{China}}-led; land belt + maritime road | India not part of it — CPEC runs through PoK |
| **BCIM corridor** | {{Kunming ↔ Kolkata}} (proposed) | !!Not!! Varanasi–Kunming |
| **Chabahar port** | {{Iran}} | Access to {{Afghanistan and Central Asia without Pakistan}} |
| **INSTC** | India → {{Iran}} → Caspian → Russia / Europe; multimodal | Founders → {{India, Iran, Russia}} |
| **Kaladan project** | Indian east coast → {{Sittwe}} → Kaladan river → Paletwa → road to {{Mizoram}} | India–Myanmar; sea–river–road |
| **IMT Trilateral Highway** | {{Moreh}} (India) → Myanmar → {{Mae Sot}} (Thailand) | !!Not!! Chiang Mai |
| **BBIN MVA** | Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Nepal — road movement | Not ASEAN |
| **Chennai–Vladivostok** | Sea corridor via Malacca and the South China Sea to the Russian Far East | |
| **Middle (Trans-Caspian) Corridor** | China → Central Asia → Caspian → Caucasus → Türkiye → Europe | Do not merge with INSTC |

- India–ASEAN connectivity → {{Kaladan + Trilateral Highway}}. Agartala–Akhaura is India–Bangladesh.
