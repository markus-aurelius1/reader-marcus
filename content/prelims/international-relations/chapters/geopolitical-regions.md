## Africa — Famine & Coups {#africa}

- **Sahel** → semi-arid belt across western and north-central Africa — a region, not a country; instability linked with military takeovers.
- Coups in recent years → {{Chad, Guinea, Mali, Sudan}}.
- Famine from war / ethnic conflict → {{Yemen, South Sudan}}; after long civil strife → {{Somalia}}.

> [!DYN] Reasons a country was "in the news" — Guinea (military takeover), Lebanon (economic depression), Tunisia (parliament suspended); who rules after a coup; ceasefire and front-line status. Geography stays, status changes.

## Chokepoints {#chokepoints}

| Route | Joins | What gets flipped |
|---|---|---|
| **Strait of Hormuz** | {{Persian Gulf ↔ Gulf of Oman}}; Iran north, Oman (Musandam) south | {{Bahrain, Qatar}} must cross it to reach the Indian Ocean; Syria, Egypt do not |
| **Bab-el-Mandeb** | {{Red Sea ↔ Gulf of Aden}}; Yemen · Djibouti / Eritrea | Its link with Suez traffic |
| **Strait of Malacca** | Andaman Sea ↔ South China Sea; Malay Peninsula · Sumatra | Confused with Kra |
| **Kra Isthmus** | Southern Thailand — a canal here is only {{proposed}}; would shorten the India–East Asia route | Not an existing canal; "deepening Malacca" is the wrong answer |
| **Suez Canal** | {{Mediterranean ↔ Red Sea}} | Atlantic / Indian Ocean are not its ends |
| **SUMED pipeline** | Egypt; {{Red Sea → Mediterranean}} | Carries Gulf oil towards Europe; a pipeline, not a canal |
