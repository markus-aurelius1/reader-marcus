## Six Principal Organs {#principal-organs}

1. General Assembly (UNGA)
2. Security Council (UNSC)
3. Economic and Social Council (ECOSOC)
4. Trusteeship Council — work suspended in {{1994}}; still exists in law
5. International Court of Justice (ICJ)
6. Secretariat

| Organ | Members | Term | Chosen by |
|---|---|---|---|
| **UNSC** | {{15}} = 5 permanent + 10 non-permanent | Non-permanent → {{2 years}}; five change each year | UNGA |
| **ECOSOC** | {{54}} | {{3 years}}, overlapping | UNGA |
| **ICJ** | {{15}} judges | {{9 years}} | UNGA + UNSC |
| **UNHRC** *(not a principal organ)* | {{47}} | 3 years; at most two terms in a row | UNGA |

## General Assembly {#unga}

- {{193}} members; regular session opens in {{September}}.
- Approves the UN budget and each member's share.
- **Observer status** → UNGA can grant it to {{non-member states}}; inter-governmental organisations can seek it; Permanent Observers may keep missions at UN headquarters.
- **Credentials Committee** → a committee of the {{General Assembly}}; {{9}} members; examines the credentials of member-state representatives and reports to the Assembly.

> [!TRAP] Credentials Committee is !!not!! a Security Council body. · It has !!no!! fixed March–June–September meeting cycle.

> [!DYN] Who sits on the Credentials Committee this year.

## Security Council {#unsc}

- P5 → China, France, Russia, UK, USA.
- A decision needs {{9}} affirmative votes.
- Substantive matters → no P5 member may vote against (veto). Procedural matters → veto does not apply.
- Job → international peace and security; can impose sanctions and authorise force.

> [!DYN] Current non-permanent members; India's past terms. UPPCS has asked which countries were elected in a given year.

## ECOSOC, Secretariat, ICJ & UNHRC {#other-organs}

- **ECOSOC** → UN's forum for economic, social and environmental coordination; functional commissions sit under it.
- **Secretariat** → administrative arm. Secretary-General → appointed by {{UNGA on UNSC's recommendation}}; 5 years, renewable.
- **ICJ** → principal judicial organ; {{The Hague}}; disputes between **states** + advisory opinions; no two judges of the same nationality.
- **UNHRC** → set up by UNGA in {{2006}}; replaced the Commission on Human Rights.

> [!DYN] UNHRC membership and India's terms.

## Agencies vs Programmes {#agencies}

- **Specialised agencies** → FAO, ICAO, IFAD, ILO, IMO, ITU, UNESCO, UNIDO, UN Tourism, UPU, WHO, WIPO, WMO.
- **Programmes / funds / offices** → UN-Habitat, UNHCR, UNRWA, UNFPA, UNCTAD, UNOPS, UNICEF, WFP.

> [!TRAP] UN-linked ≠ specialised agency.

## UNTOC vs UNCAC {#untoc-uncac}

| | UNTOC (Palermo Convention) | UNCAC |
|---|---|---|
| Against | {{Transnational organised crime}} | {{Corruption}} |
| Adopted | 2000 | 2003 |
| Key feature | Three protocols → trafficking in persons · {{smuggling of migrants}} · firearms | Prevention · criminalisation · cooperation · {{asset recovery}} |
| Secretariat | UNODC | UNODC |

- UNCAC → the only legally binding universal anti-corruption instrument.

> [!TRAP] Migrant-smuggling protocol → UNTOC, !!not!! UNCAC. · Asset recovery → UNCAC.

> [!VERIFY] UNCAC dates differ across coaching sources. Treaty record: adopted 31 October 2003, in force 14 December 2005 — recheck before quoting day and month.

## Declarations & Frameworks {#declarations}

| Instrument | Subject |
|---|---|
| **UDHR**, 1948 | Human rights charter — not a treaty |
| **Beijing Declaration & Platform for Action**, {{1995}} | {{Women's empowerment}} — Fourth World Conference on Women |
| **Alma-Ata Declaration**, {{1978}} | {{Primary health care}} — "Health for All" |

- International treaties need no State's consent to be made law in India → `Art. 253`.

## Law of the Sea {#unclos}

| Zone | Limit from baseline | Rights |
|---|---|---|
| **Territorial sea** | Up to {{12 nautical miles}} | Sovereignty; foreign ships have {{innocent passage}} |
| **Exclusive Economic Zone** | Up to {{200 nautical miles}} | Resource rights — not full sovereignty |

- **International Seabed Authority** → created under UNCLOS; HQ {{Kingston, Jamaica}}.
  - Regulates mining in "the Area" — seabed beyond national jurisdiction.
  - Area's resources → {{common heritage of mankind}}.
  - Grants exploration contracts.

> [!TRAP] Global Ocean Commission does !!not!! license seabed mining → [Ocean bodies](@ngos-other-bodies#ocean-bodies)
