### Impeachment — Art. 61
> [!FLOW] Ground: {{**violation of the Constitution**}} only → either House → {{14 days' notice signed by ≥ 1/4 of total membership}} → passed by {{≥ **2/3 of total membership**}} → other House investigates (President may appear) → also ≥ 2/3 of total membership → removed

- Nominated MPs take part; State legislatures have no role.

## President — Position & Legislative Powers

- President = constitutional (*de jure*) head; PM + CoM = real executive. Executive power still *vests* in the President.
- `Art. 74` → acts on CoM advice; may return it {{**once**}}; reconsidered advice binding; advice not inquired into by courts.
- `Art. 77` → executive action taken in the {{**name of the President**}}; President makes business rules.
- `Art. 79` → {{part of Parliament, but not a member of either House}}.
- `Art. 87` special address → {{first session after each general election + first session of each year}}.
- `Art. 85` → summons, prorogues, dissolves LS on advice.

## Ordinance — Article 123

- When → {{**both Houses not in session together**}} (one alone not sitting is enough) + need for immediate action.
- Force → same as an Act of Parliament; only within Parliament's competence.
- Laid before both Houses; lapses {{**6 weeks** after reassembly}} (later date if Houses reassemble separately); may be withdrawn / disapproved earlier.

| Can | Cannot |
|---|---|
| {{Amend / repeal a Central Act}} | {{Amend the Constitution}} |
| {{Operate retrospectively}} | {{Abridge Fundamental Rights}} |

- Repeated re-promulgation to bypass the legislature → unconstitutional.
- Exercised on CoM advice — not the President's personal law-making.

## Pardoning Power — Article 72

President may grant {{pardon, reprieve, respite, remission}} or {{suspend, remit, commute}} where:
- sentence by {{Court Martial}};
- offence against a {{Union law}};
- sentence of {{**death**}}.

- Exercised on {{advice of the Union CoM}} — not personal discretion.
- Not an appeal; President may examine the evidence afresh.
- {{**Limited judicial review**}} → arbitrariness, mala fides, non-application of mind.

Governor's Art. 161 contrast → [State Executive](@state-executive-legislature#governor)

## Prime Minister & Council of Ministers

- `Art. 75(1)` → PM appointed by President; ministers appointed {{on PM's advice}}.
- Need not be an MP when appointed → must become member of {{**either** House within 6 consecutive months}}.
- `Art. 75(1A)` → CoM incl. PM {{≤ **15%** of Lok Sabha strength}} (91st Amdt). `Art. 75(1B)` → defector cannot be minister.
- Ministers hold office during the President's pleasure; President administers oaths of office + secrecy.
- `Art. 75(3)` → collectively responsible to {{the **Lok Sabha**}} — not "Parliament".
- No-confidence motion → whole ministry goes; PM's death / resignation → CoM dissolves.
- Salaries → Parliament by law.

### Art. 78 — PM's duties
- Communicate CoM decisions on administration & legislation to the President.
- Furnish information the President calls for.
- If President requires → submit to CoM a matter decided by a single minister.

### Council of Ministers ≠ Cabinet

| Council of Ministers | Cabinet |
|---|---|
| Arts. 74–75 | Word appears only in {{`Art. 352(3)`}} |
| PM + Cabinet ministers + MoS + deputy ministers | {{PM + ministers of **Cabinet rank**}} |
| Collective responsibility attaches here; 15% cap | Real policy-making core; no separate cap |

> [!TRAP] Ministers of State are !!not!! Cabinet members. · The Constitution does not classify ministers into ranks.

## Cabinet Secretariat & PMO

- Cabinet Secretariat → {{directly under the **PM**}}; head = {{**Cabinet Secretary**}} (also ex-officio Chairman, Civil Services Board).
- Functions → secretarial help to Cabinet & its committees; agenda, records, follow-up; inter-ministerial coordination.
- Administers {{Allocation of Business Rules, 1961 + Transaction of Business Rules, 1961}} — framed under {{`Art. 77(3)`}}.

> [!TRAP] Allocating **financial resources** to ministries is !!not!! a Cabinet Secretariat function.

> [!UPPCS]
> - PM's Secretariat set up {{15 Aug 1947}}; renamed **PMO** in {{1977}}.
> - PMO headed by {{Principal Secretary to the PM}}.
> - PMO ≠ Cabinet Secretariat; Cabinet Secretary does not head the PMO.

> [!DYN] Cabinet Committees — number, composition, chairs — are not kept here.

## Attorney-General

- `Art. 76` → constitutional office; part of Union Executive; appointed by President.
- Qualification → {{qualified to be a **Supreme Court judge**}}.
- Duties → advise GoI; perform legal duties assigned by President.
- Right of audience in {{**all courts in India**}}.
- Tenure → {{pleasure of the President}}; no removal procedure / grounds in the Constitution.
- Remuneration → {{determined by the **President**}}.
- `Art. 88` → may speak in either House, joint sitting, committees where named — {{**no vote**}}.
- Need not be an MP; resigning with the government is convention, not law.

> [!TRAP] Solicitor-General is !!not!! a constitutional office.
