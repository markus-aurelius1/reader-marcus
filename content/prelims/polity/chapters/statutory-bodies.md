## Classification Key

| Category | Test |
|---|---|
| Constitutional | Source in the Constitution — an Article |
| Statutory | Created by an Act of Parliament / State Legislature |
| Executive (non-statutory) | Created by resolution / order / notification |
| Non-constitutional | Umbrella → {{may be statutory **or** executive}} |

> [!TRAP] Non-constitutional ≠ non-statutory.

## NITI Aayog, Planning Commission & NDC

### NITI Aayog
- Chairperson → {{Prime Minister}}. Policy think tank; cooperative federalism; coordinates {{SDGs}}.
- Governing Council → {{PM + CMs of States and UTs with legislatures + Lt Governors of other UTs}}.
- Vice-Chairperson → appointed by PM; part-time members max {{2}}; ex-officio Union Ministers max {{4}}; CEO → Secretary rank, fixed tenure.
- Regional Councils → specific issues, specified tenure.
- Attached → {{DMEO}} (monitoring & evaluation), Atal Innovation Mission.
- Not a renamed Planning Commission → the 1950 Resolution was superseded.

### Planning Commission
- 1950 executive resolution; advisory; PM = Chairman; no State partnership of the NITI kind.

### National Development Council
- 1952; PM chairs; Union Cabinet Ministers + all CMs + UT administrators + Planning Commission members.
- Considered and approved the Five-Year Plan → then placed before {{Parliament}}.
- {{**Not** an organ of the Planning Commission}}.

> [!UPPCS]
> - First NITI Vice-Chairman → {{Arvind Panagariya}}.
> - Planning Commission = "Economic Cabinet of India" → {{Ashok Chandra}}.

## Lokpal

- Chairperson + {{max **8** members}}; {{**50%** judicial}}; {{≥ 50% from SC / ST / OBC / minorities / women}}.
- Chairperson → {{CJI, or SC judge, or an eminent person}} — not "only a CJI".
- Not an MP / MLA; age {{≥ **45**}}; term {{**5 years or 70**}}.

### Selection Committee
- {{PM (Chair)}} · {{Speaker of Lok Sabha}} · {{LoP in Lok Sabha}} · {{CJI or an SC judge nominated by CJI}} · {{eminent jurist}}.
- !!Not!! a member → Chairman of Rajya Sabha.

### Jurisdiction
- Public servants {{in and outside India}}.
- PM (present / former), Union Ministers, MPs, Group A–D officials, specified bodies.
- Sitting PM → not exempt; excluded subjects {{international relations, security, public order, atomic energy, space}}; inquiry needs {{full bench + 2/3 approval}}; in camera.

## CVC & CBI

- **CVC** → statutory since {{2003}}; Central Vigilance Commissioner + {{max 2 Vigilance Commissioners}}; no constitutional Article.
- **CBI** → set up by executive resolution ({{1963}}); investigative powers under {{DSPE Act, 1946}}; under {{DoPT, Ministry of Personnel}}.
- CBI = India's {{National Central Bureau for INTERPOL}}.

> [!TRAP] "CBI is a statutory body" → false; "CBI has no statutory powers" → also false.

## Inter-State Council vs Zonal Councils

| | Inter-State Council | Zonal Councils |
|---|---|---|
| Basis | Art. 263 + Presidential Order, {{28 May 1990}} | States Reorganisation Act, 1956 |
| Number | One | {{**Five** — Northern, Central, Eastern, Western, Southern}} |
| Chair | {{Prime Minister}} | {{Union Home Minister}}; CMs vice-chair by rotation |
| Nature | Recommendatory | Advisory |
| Origin | Sarkaria Commission | Nehru's proposal |

- ISC members → CMs of States + UTs with legislatures; Administrators of other UTs; Governors of States under President's Rule; six Union Cabinet Ministers.
- ISC's order covers Art. 263(b)–(c) — it has {{not been given the dispute-inquiry role of 263(a)}}.
- NEC is separate from the five Zonal Councils.

Constitutional status of ISC → [Federalism](@federalism-emergency#inter-state-coordination)

> [!DYN] ISC Standing Committee (Home Minister + 4 Union Cabinet Ministers + 8 CMs) and Zonal Council State rosters change on reconstitution.
