## Union & its Territory — Articles 1–4

| Article | Content |
|---|---|
| `Art. 1` | "India, that is Bharat, shall be a {{**Union of States**}}"; territory = States + UTs + acquired territories |
| `Art. 2` | Parliament → {{**admit** into the Union / **establish** new States}} |
| `Art. 3` | Parliament → {{form new State; increase / diminish area; alter boundaries; alter name}} |
| `Art. 4` | Arts. 2/3 laws may amend {{First + Fourth Schedules}}; {{**not** deemed amendments under Art. 368}} |

### Article 3 procedure
> [!FLOW] {{Prior recommendation of **President**}} → Bill in **either House** → if area / boundary / name of a State is affected, President refers Bill to that State Legislature for its {{**views**}} within the specified period → Parliament passes by {{**simple majority**}}

- State Legislature's views → !!not!! binding; no consent, no veto.
- In Art. 3 "State" includes UT; in the *reference proviso* "State" excludes UT.
- Parliament may unite parts of States / UTs to form a State / UT.

> [!TRAP] Art. 2 (admit / establish — territory not already part of India's States) ≠ Art. 3 (reorganise existing States). · Power to create or rename a State → **Parliament**, not the State Legislature.

> [!EXC] Cession of Indian territory to a foreign State ≠ Art. 3 → needs a **constitutional amendment**.

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 1948 | {{**Dhar Commission**}} → preferred administrative convenience over language |
| 1948–49 | {{**JVP Committee** — Jawaharlal Nehru, Vallabhbhai Patel, Pattabhi Sitaramayya}} → rejected language as the basis |
| 1953 | {{**Andhra State**}} carved from Madras → first linguistic State |
| 1953 | States Reorganisation Commission → {{**Fazl Ali** (chair) + K.M. Panikkar + H.N. Kunzru}} |

> [!TRAP] Andhra **State** 1953 ≠ Andhra **Pradesh** 1956. · SRC members ≠ K.T. Shah / Pattabhi Sitaramayya.

## Citizenship — Constitution (Part II)

| Article | Recall |
|---|---|
| 5 | At commencement: domicile + born in India / parent born in India / 5 years' ordinary residence |
| 6 | Migrants {{**from Pakistan** to India}} |
| 7 | Migrants {{**to Pakistan** after 1 Mar 1947}}; return-permit exception |
| 8 | Persons of Indian origin residing {{outside India}} |
| 9 | {{Voluntary acquisition of foreign citizenship → not a citizen under 5 / 6 / 8}} |
| 10 | Continuance, subject to parliamentary law |
| 11 | {{**Parliament** regulates acquisition, termination and all other matters}} |

- India → {{**single citizenship**}}; "one citizenship + one domicile". Federal polity ≠ dual citizenship.
- `Art. 58` requires "a citizen of India" → a **naturalised** citizen can be President.

> [!TRAP] Citizenship = Part **II** (Arts. 5–11), not Part III. · No "natural-born" requirement for any office. · Aadhaar ≠ proof of citizenship / domicile.

## Citizenship Act, 1955

**Acquisition — 5** → {{Birth (s.3) · Descent (s.4) · Registration (s.5) · Naturalisation (s.6) · Incorporation of territory (s.7)}}

| Born in India | Citizen by birth if |
|---|---|
| 26 Jan 1950 – 30 Jun 1987 | {{Born in India — irrespective of parents}} |
| 1 Jul 1987 – 2 Dec 2004 | {{**Either** parent a citizen at birth}} |
| From 3 Dec 2004 | {{**Both** parents citizens, **or** one citizen + other **not an illegal migrant**}} |

- Exceptions → children of foreign diplomats / enemy aliens.

| By descent (born outside India) | Rule |
|---|---|
| 26 Jan 1950 – 9 Dec 1992 | {{**Father** a citizen at birth}} |
| From 10 Dec 1992 | {{**Either** parent a citizen}} |
| From 3 Dec 2004 | + birth registered at Indian consulate (statutory conditions) |

**Loss — 3**

| Mode | Core |
|---|---|
| Renunciation (s.8) | {{Voluntary declaration}} |
| Termination (s.9) | {{Voluntary acquisition of another country's citizenship}} |
| Deprivation (s.10) | {{Compulsory — Central Government, on specified grounds}} |

> [!TRAP] "Citizenship once granted can never be deprived" → !!false!!. · Post-2004 birth rule is !!not!! "both parents citizens only". · PIO is a legacy category merged into OCI; OCI is not citizenship.
