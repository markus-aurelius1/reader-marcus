## DPSP — Frame

- {{Part IV · Arts. 36–51}}; idea from the {{Irish Constitution}} (itself influenced by the Spanish).
- `Art. 36` → "State" = same as Part III.
- `Art. 37` → {{**not enforceable by any court**, yet **fundamental in the governance** of the country}}; State's duty to apply them in making laws.
- Purpose → social + economic democracy; ==welfare state → DPSP== (not FR / FD).
- Positive directions to the State; no individual remedy for non-implementation.
- Socialist / Gandhian / Liberal-intellectual → {{study classification — **not** in the Constitution}}.

> [!TRAP] Non-justiciable ≠ unimportant. · "Fundamental in governance" ≠ enforceable. · DPSP *guide* the State — they are !!not!! limitations on legislative / executive functions.

## Fundamental Rights ↔ DPSP

- No blanket "FR supreme" / "DPSP supreme" rule → {{**harmony and balance** between Parts III and IV = basic structure}} (*Minerva Mills*).
- `Art. 31C` → laws giving effect to {{Art. 39(b) / 39(c)}} shielded from {{Arts. 14 & 19}}; still open to judicial review.
- 42nd Amdt extended 31C to all DPSP → {{struck down in *Minerva Mills*}}.

## Fundamental Duties — Article 51A

- {{Part IV-A · Art. 51A}}; {{42nd Amdt, 1976}} → 10 duties on {{**Swaran Singh Committee**}} recommendation; source {{USSR}}.
- 11th duty (k) → {{86th Amdt, 2002}}.

| Clause | Duty |
|---|---|
| (a) | Abide by Constitution; respect ideals, institutions, {{National Flag & Anthem}} |
| (b) | Cherish noble ideals of the {{freedom struggle}} |
| (c) | Uphold {{sovereignty, unity, integrity}} |
| (d) | {{Defend the country; national service when called}} |
| (e) | Harmony & common brotherhood; renounce practices {{derogatory to dignity of women}} |
| (f) | Preserve heritage of {{composite culture}} |
| (g) | Protect {{natural environment — forests, lakes, rivers, wildlife; compassion for living creatures}} |
| (h) | {{Scientific temper, humanism, spirit of inquiry & reform}} |
| (i) | Safeguard {{public property; abjure violence}} |
| (j) | Strive for {{excellence}} |
| (k) | Parent / guardian → education for child / ward {{**6–14**}} |

- Apply to {{**citizens only**}}.
- Non-justiciable; Parliament may enforce related conduct by separate law (statutory sanction ≠ enforceable Art. 51A).
- Enjoyment of FRs is not conditional on performing duties.

> [!TRAP] !!Not!! Fundamental Duties: voting · paying taxes · protecting monuments (Art. 49 DPSP) · protecting weaker sections (Art. 46 DPSP) · promoting international peace (Art. 51 DPSP).
