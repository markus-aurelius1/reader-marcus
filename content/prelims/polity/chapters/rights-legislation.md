## Custody, Parole & Prisons

| Item | Fact | Trap |
|---|---|---|
| Police custody | Accused with police; police lock-up; police interrogate | ≠ judicial custody |
| Judicial custody | Under Magistrate / court; lodged in {{jail}} | not a police-station lock-up |
| Interrogation in judicial custody | Only with {{**court permission**}} | not a total bar |
| Parole | Conditional temporary release; {{**not an absolute right**}}; governed by {{State rules}} | "sufficient cause" ≠ entitlement |
| Prisons | {{**State List — Entry 4**}}; Prisons Act 1894 + State manuals | ≠ Union List |
| Model Prisons and Correctional Services Act, 2023 | {{Model for States to adopt}} | not automatically in force |

- Preventive ≠ punitive detention; constitutional safeguards → [Art. 22](@fundamental-rights#right-to-freedom-articles-19-22).
- Regular bail → post-arrest bail for a person in custody (not only "police custody").

> [!TRAP] POTA 2002 (repealed) allowed certain police confessions as evidence → the blanket claim "a confession to police can never be evidence" was wrong for that regime. Not present law.

## Consumer Protection

- Current law → {{**Consumer Protection Act, 2019**}} (replaced 1986 Act) → {{CCPA}} + District / State / National Commissions (statutory, quasi-judicial — not constitutional).
- Who may complain → consumer · recognised consumer association · governments / CCPA · consumers with same interest · {{**legal heir** of a deceased consumer}} · guardian of a minor.
- Legacy 2012 PYQ → food sample testing allowed; "no fee at all" false; legal heir may complain.
- **MRP** → {{Maximum Retail Price = **ceiling**, incl. taxes}}; sale **below** MRP is legal — source: Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, not the CPA.

> [!TRAP] MRP ≠ price the seller must charge.

> [!DYN] Pecuniary jurisdiction limits of consumer commissions change — not kept here.

## Right to Education

- `Art. 21A` → {{86th Amendment, **2002**}}; {{RTE Act, **2009**}}; both in force {{**1 April 2010**}}; age {{6–14}}.
- Teacher qualifications (s.23) → set by academic authority authorised by the Centre = {{**NCTE**}} — !!not!! the State Council.
- {{**TET**}} = essential qualification, held as per NCTE guidelines; passing TET ≠ right to appointment.
- Prohibited → capitation fee · screening procedure · physical punishment / mental harassment · denial of admission for want of age proof.

> [!TRAP] 86th Amendment (2002) ≠ RTE Act (2009).

## Disability Rights

| Anchor | Fact |
|---|---|
| Principal law | {{**Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016**}} — not 2018 |
| Benchmark disability | {{≥ **40%** of a specified disability}} |
| s.31 | Free education, {{**6–18** years}} |
| s.32 | {{≥ **5%** seats}} — govt / aided higher education |
| s.34 | {{≥ **4%** vacancies}} — government establishments |
| ss.40–42 | Accessibility; transport; ICT |

- **Sugamya Bharat** (Accessible India) → {{built environment + transport + ICT}}.
- **NDFDC** → under {{DEPwD, Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment}} — not Corporate Affairs.
- 2011 PYQ (free schooling till 18, preferential land allotment, ramps) reflects the older law.

> [!TRAP] 5% education ≠ 4% jobs. · RPwD 6–18 ≠ Art. 21A 6–14.

## IT Act & Flag Code

### Cyber reporting
- {{**CERT-In**}} → statutory, {{s.70B, IT Act 2000}}.
- Must report incidents → {{service providers, intermediaries, data centres, bodies corporate}} (and government organisations).

> [!DYN] Reporting deadlines / incident lists change.

### Flag Code of India, 2002
- Consolidates laws, conventions, instructions → {{**not a statute**}}. Statutes: Emblems and Names Act 1950; Prevention of Insults to National Honour Act 1971.
- Shape → rectangular; {{length : height = **3 : 2**}}.
- Standard sizes (mm) → 6300×4200 · 3600×2400 · 2700×1800 · 1800×1200 · 1350×900 · 900×600 · 450×300 · 225×150 · 150×100.

> [!TRAP] 600 × 400 mm has the right ratio but is !!not!! a listed standard size.

## Land Acquisition

- {{**RFCTLARR Act, 2013**}} — in force {{1 January 2014}}.
- Fair compensation + rehabilitation & resettlement; {{Social Impact Assessment}} + public hearing.
- Prior consent → private company {{**≥ 80%**}} of affected families · PPP {{**≥ 70%**}}.
