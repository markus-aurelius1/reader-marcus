## Composition & Constitutional Map

| Article | Payload |
|---|---|
| `Art. 79` | Parliament = {{**President + Rajya Sabha + Lok Sabha**}} |
| `Art. 80` | RS max {{**250** = 12 nominated + ≤ 238 States / UTs}}; nominated fields → {{literature, science, art, **social service**}}; elected by {{elected MLAs — PR-STV}}; seats in Fourth Schedule |
| `Art. 81` | LS max {{**550** = ≤ 530 States + ≤ 20 UTs}} |
| `Art. 83` | RS → continuing House; {{1/3 retire every 2 years}}; member's term {{6 years}} (RPA 1951). LS → 5 years unless dissolved |
| `Art. 85` | President summons; gap between sessions {{**< 6 months**}} |
| `Art. 98` | Separate secretariat for each House |
| `Art. 100` | Quorum = {{**1/10** of total membership}} |
| `Art. 101(4)` | Absent {{**60 days**}} without leave → House {{**may declare seat vacant**}} |

> [!TRAP] President is part of Parliament, not a member. · "Social service", !!not!! social sciences. · Constitution prescribes no "three sessions" or minimum sitting days. · 60 days' absence ≠ automatic disqualification.

## Presiding Officers

| Office | Rule |
|---|---|
| RS Chairman | Vice-President, ex officio — {{**not** an RS member}} |
| RS Deputy Chairman | Elected from RS members — {{**is** a member}} |
| Speaker / Deputy Speaker | Elected by LS from its members |
| Removal (Dy Chairman, Speaker, Dy Speaker) | {{Majority of **all the then members** = effective majority}}; {{14 days'}} notice |
| Speaker after dissolution | Continues till {{just before first meeting of the new LS}} |
| Speaker facing removal motion | {{Cannot preside; may speak; votes in first instance — no casting vote}} |
| Deputy Speaker's election | Date fixed by {{**Speaker**}} (LS Rule 8); opposition holding it = convention only |

> [!TRAP] Effective majority ≠ absolute majority.

## Lok Sabha vs Rajya Sabha

| Matter | Position |
|---|---|
| Ordinary Bill · Constitution Amendment Bill | Equal powers |
| Money Bill | LS dominant; RS only recommends |
| Demands for Grants | {{LS votes; RS only discusses}} |
| No-confidence motion | {{LS only}} |
| `Art. 249` — Parliament legislates on State List | {{RS resolution, **2/3 present and voting**}}; valid 1 year, renewable |
| `Art. 312` — new All-India Service | {{RS resolution, **2/3 present and voting**}} |

> [!TRAP] Arts. 249 / 312 → 2/3 present & voting, !!not!! 2/3 of total membership.

## Bills — Types & Procedure

| Type | Introduced in | RS power | Joint sitting |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ordinary | Either House | Amend / reject | Yes |
| **Money** — `Art. 110` | {{LS only; President's recommendation}} | {{Recommend only; return in **14 days**}} | {{No}} |
| **Financial Bill-I** — `Art. 117(1)` | {{LS only; President's recommendation}} | {{Amend / reject}} | {{Yes}} |
| **Financial Bill-II** — `Art. 117(3)` | {{Either House}} | Equal | Yes; President's recommendation needed {{before **consideration**}} |
| Constitution Amendment | Either House | Equal | {{No}} |

- Money Bill = **only** Art. 110(1) matters → tax; borrowing; custody of / withdrawal from CFI & Contingency Fund; appropriation; charged expenditure; receipts & audit; incidental.
- RS not returning in 14 days → deemed passed. LS may accept or reject RS recommendations.
- Speaker certifies; decision "final" (`Art. 110(3)`) — yet {{open to limited judicial review}}.
- Fin. Bill-I = Art. 110 matter **+** other matters. Fin. Bill-II = expenditure from CFI, no Art. 110 matter.
- ==All Money Bills are Financial Bills; not all Financial Bills are Money Bills.==

> [!TRAP] RS may **recommend** on a Money Bill; it cannot amend or reject. · Appropriation Bill is a Money Bill; "Finance Bill = Art. 110" is wrong shorthand — Art. 110 defines *Money Bill*.

## Joint Sitting & Lapse of Bills

### Joint sitting — Art. 108
- Triggers → other House {{rejects}} · {{final disagreement on amendments}} · {{**more than 6 months** elapse}}.
- President {{**may**}} summon; {{Speaker}} presides; {{simple majority of members present and voting}}.
- !!Not!! for Money Bills / Constitution Amendment Bills.

### On dissolution of Lok Sabha

| Bill | Fate |
|---|---|
| Pending in LS | {{Lapses}} |
| Passed by LS, pending in RS | {{Lapses}} |
| Pending in RS, not passed by LS | {{Does **not** lapse}} |
| Passed by both, awaiting assent | {{Does not lapse}} |
| Returned by President for reconsideration | {{Does not lapse}} |
| Joint sitting already notified | {{Does not lapse}} |

- Prorogation → {{no Bill lapses}}.

## Sessions & Devices

| Term | Meaning | By |
|---|---|---|
| Adjournment | Ends a sitting | {{Presiding officer}} |
| Adjournment *sine die* | Ends sitting without a date | Presiding officer |
| Prorogation | Ends a session | {{President}} |
| Dissolution | Ends the House — {{LS only}} | President |

- **Question Hour** → first hour. Starred → {{oral answer + supplementaries}}. Unstarred → {{written answer, **no** supplementaries}}.
- **Zero Hour** → after Question Hour, before listed business; {{not in the Rules of Procedure}} — an Indian convention.
- **Adjournment Motion** → definite matter of urgent public importance; {{LS only}}; element of censure.
- **No-confidence Motion** → LS only; {{not mentioned in the Constitution}}; flows from Art. 75(3).
- **Private Member** → any MP who is {{not a minister}} (elected or nominated).
- **Leader of Opposition** → statutory, {{1977 Act}}; leader of the largest opposition party recognised by Speaker / Chairman; {{the Act has **no 10% rule**}}; first recognised in LS in {{1969}}.
- **Office of profit** → `Art. 102(1)(a)`; exemptions by {{Parliament (Prevention of Disqualification) Act, 1959}}.

## Budget & Financial Procedure

> [!FLOW] `Art. 112` Annual Financial Statement laid before **both** Houses → general discussion → DRSC scrutiny → Demands for Grants + cut motions (**LS only**) → Appropriation Bill (`Art. 114`) → Finance Bill

- Expenditure {{charged on CFI}} → discussed, {{not voted}}.
- Demand for grant → only on {{President's recommendation}}; voted only by {{LS}}.
- Appropriation Act → sole authority to withdraw from CFI. Public Account withdrawals need no appropriation.

| Grant | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Supplementary / additional / excess (`Art. 115`) | Shortfall, new service, overspend |
| **Vote on Account** (`Art. 116`) | {{Advance grant for part of the year pending the full procedure}} |
| Vote of Credit | {{Unexpected demand of indefinite character}} |
| Exceptional Grant | {{Not part of the current service of any year}} |

> [!TRAP] Vote on Account ≠ Interim Budget (the latter is not a constitutional category). · AFS goes to both Houses; grants are voted by LS alone.
