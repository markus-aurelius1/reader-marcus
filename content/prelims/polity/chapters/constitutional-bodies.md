## Finance Commission

- `Art. 280` → {{President}} constitutes every {{**5th year** or earlier}}; {{**Chairman + 4** members}}.
- Qualifications → prescribed by {{**Parliament by law**}} — Finance Commission (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1951.
  - Chairman → experience in {{public affairs}}.
  - Members → HC judge / qualified to be one · government finance & accounts · financial & administrative experience · economics.

**Recommends**
- {{Vertical devolution}} — Union ↔ States share of the divisible pool.
- {{Horizontal devolution}} — among States.
- Principles of {{grants-in-aid}} (`Art. 275`).
- Measures to augment State Consolidated Funds for {{panchayats and municipalities}} (on SFC recommendations).
- Any matter referred by the President for sound finance.

- `Art. 281` → President lays report + {{explanatory memorandum on action taken}} before both Houses.
- Recommendations → {{advisory, not binding}}.
- Divisible pool = {{net proceeds}}; {{cesses and surcharges excluded}}. `Art. 279` → CAG certifies net proceeds.
- Income distance → poorer States get more (equity).

> [!TRAP] FC recommends — it does not distribute funds, and has no role in PSU finances. · Chairman + 4, not + 6. · "Always accepted" is not a rule.

## Election Commission of India

- `Art. 324` → rolls + elections to {{Parliament, State Legislatures, President, Vice-President}}.
- CEC + {{such number of ECs as the President fixes}} — no fixed size in the Constitution.
- Decides election schedule (not the Home Ministry); decides {{party recognition / symbol disputes}}.
- Does !!not!! → conduct local-body elections (SEC) · try election petitions ({{High Court}}).

| Article | Payload |

- Term → {{6 years or 65}}.
- Reports examined by the {{PAC}}.

## Public Service Commissions

| | UPSC | SPSC | Joint SPSC |
|---|---|---|---|
| Appointed by | {{President}} | {{Governor}} | {{President}} |
| Term | 6 years / {{65}} | 6 years / {{62}} | 6 years / {{62}} |
| Removed by | {{President}} | {{**President**}} | {{President}} |
| Report | President → Parliament | Governor → Legislature | |

- Number of members → not fixed by the Constitution.
- {{Half}} the members → at least {{10 years}} in government service.
- Joint SPSC → {{resolutions of the State legislatures → **Parliament** by law}}.
- `Art. 317` → misbehaviour: President removes after {{**Supreme Court inquiry**}}; direct removal for insolvency, paid outside employment, infirmity. Governor may only *suspend* an SPSC member.
- `Art. 320` → exams; advice on recruitment methods, promotions, transfers, discipline. Advice {{not binding}}.
- `Art. 321` → functions extendable by Parliament / State Legislature.
- `Art. 323` → annual report + memo on advice not accepted.

> [!TRAP] SPSC: appointed by Governor, removed by **President**. · UPSC does !!not!! supervise SPSCs.

> [!UPPCS] UPSC's functions flow from → Constitution + Parliamentary laws + executive rules / orders + conventions.
