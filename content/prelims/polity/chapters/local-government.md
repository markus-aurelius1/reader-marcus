### State Finance Commission
- Reviews panchayat finances → sharing of State taxes; taxes assignable; grants-in-aid; measures to improve finances.
- Governor lays recommendations + action-taken memo before the State Legislature.
- Same SFC covers municipalities (`Art. 243Y`).

### Compulsory vs left to States

| Compulsory | State's discretion |
|---|---|
| Gram Sabha | Its powers |
| Three tiers (≤ 20 lakh exception) | How the village chairperson is chosen |
| Direct election to all seats | Representation of MPs / MLAs |
| Indirect election of intermediate / district chairpersons | Backward-class reservation |
| SC/ST + women reservation | Devolution of functions (243G) |
| 5-year term; SEC; SFC | Taxation powers (243H) |

> [!TRAP] Gram Sabha ≠ all residents. · Women ≥ 1/3 — not 1/4. · Tenure counts from first meeting. · SEC conducts elections — it does !!not!! decide election disputes. · SFC ≠ Union Finance Commission; ECI ≠ local-body elections.

## DPC vs MPC

| | District Planning Committee | Metropolitan Planning Committee |
|---|---|---|
| Article | {{243ZD}} | {{243ZE}} |
| Amendment | {{**74th**}} | 74th |
| Task | Consolidate panchayat + municipality plans → draft district plan | Draft metropolitan development plan |
| Elected members | {{≥ **4/5**}} | {{≥ **2/3**}} |
| Elected by | Elected members of district panchayat + municipalities | Elected municipal members + panchayat chairpersons |
| Ratio | Rural : urban population | Municipalities : panchayats population |
| Sent to | State Government | State Government |

> [!TRAP] DPC is a 74th-Amendment body, !!not!! 73rd. · MPC plans — it is not the sole implementing agency.
