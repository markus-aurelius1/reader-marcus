## Federal Character

- `Art. 1` → "Union of States" → {{not the result of an agreement among States}}; {{no right to secede}}.

| Federal features | Unitary / centralising features |
|---|---|
| Dual polity | Strong Centre; residuary power with Parliament |
| Written + supreme Constitution | Single Constitution; {{single citizenship}} |
| Division of powers | Parliament can enter the State List |
| Rigidity of federal provisions (State ratification) | State boundaries alterable by Parliament |
| {{**Independent judiciary**}} | {{Integrated judiciary}} |
| Bicameralism | Unequal State representation in RS |
| | Governors appointed by President; All-India Services; Emergency provisions |

- Federalism = basic structure.

> [!TRAP] Independent judiciary + division of powers → federal. Single citizenship, emergency powers, strong Centre → !!not!! federal markers.

## Legislative Relations

- Part XI, Ch. I → Arts. 245–255. `Art. 246` + Seventh Schedule → three Lists ([entries](@parts-schedules#seventh-schedule-lists)).
- `Art. 248` → residuary legislation **and** taxation → {{Parliament}}.

### Parliament in the State field

| Route | Trigger | Fine print |
|---|---|---|
| `Art. 249` | {{**Rajya Sabha** resolution — 2/3 present and voting — national interest}} | Resolution {{1 year}}, renewable; law lapses {{6 months}} after resolution ends |
| `Art. 250` | {{National Emergency in operation}} | States still legislate; law lapses 6 months after Emergency |
| `Art. 252` | {{Resolutions of **2 or more** States}} | Applies to those + adopting States; {{only Parliament can amend / repeal}} |
| `Art. 253` | {{Treaty, agreement, convention, decision of an international body}} | {{**No State consent** needed}} |
| Arts. 356–357 | President's Rule | State legislative power exercised by / under Parliament |

- `Art. 254` repugnancy on Concurrent subject → Central law prevails; a State law {{reserved for and assented to by the President}} prevails {{in that State only}} — Parliament may still override.

> [!TRAP] Art. 249 → Rajya Sabha, not Lok Sabha; 2/3 present & voting ≠ Art. 368 special majority. · Art. 253 is not limited to "signed and ratified" treaties.

### Minor minerals — MMDR Act, 1957
- {{**State Governments**}} → make rules for quarry / mining leases and concessions of minor minerals (s.15); rules against illegal mining.
- {{**Central Government**}} → notifies what is a "minor mineral" (s.3(e)).

> [!TRAP] "States have no rule-making power over minor minerals" → !!false!!.

## National Emergency — Article 352

- Only on {{**written** decision of the Cabinet}}; whole or part of India; imminent danger suffices.
- "Internal disturbance" → replaced by {{"armed rebellion" — 44th Amdt}}.
- LS disapproval → notice by {{1/10 of LS members}} → sitting within {{14 days}} → if disapproved, President must revoke.
- If LS is dissolved → RS approves; new LS within 30 days of its first sitting.

### Effects
- Parliament may legislate on State List; State legislatures {{are **not** suspended}}.
- Union may direct States on any matter.
- President may modify revenue distribution (Arts. 268–279).
- LS / Assembly life extendable {{1 year at a time}}; not beyond 6 months after Emergency.
- `Art. 358` → {{Art. 19 suspended automatically — only for **war / external aggression**}}; protects only Emergency-related laws.
- `Art. 359` → President suspends {{right to move courts}} for specified FRs; {{**Arts. 20 & 21 excluded**}}.

> [!TRAP] Approval is not a Lok Sabha-only power. · Art. 359 suspends *enforcement*, not the rights themselves.

## President's Rule — Article 356

- `Art. 355` → Union's duty to protect States and ensure constitutional government.
- `Art. 356` → on {{Governor's report **or otherwise**}}.
- `Art. 365` → non-compliance with Union directions = constitutional failure.

| Consequence | Position |
|---|---|
| Council of Ministers | Dismissed; Governor administers for the President |
| Assembly | {{Suspended **or** dissolved — not automatic}} |
| Legislative power | {{By / under authority of Parliament}} |
| High Court | {{Powers cannot be assumed}} |
| Fundamental Rights | {{Unaffected}} |
| Local bodies | Not dissolved |
| Laws made during it | {{Continue until altered by a competent legislature}} |

- Beyond {{1 year}} → both needed: {{National Emergency in force}} **+** {{ECI certifies elections cannot be held}}.
- {{**S.R. Bommai (1994)**}} → proclamation judicially reviewable; majority tested on the floor.

## Financial Emergency — Article 360

- Union may direct States to observe canons of financial propriety.
- Reduce salaries of all in State service; of Union service incl. {{SC / HC judges}}.
- State Money Bills may be reserved for the President.
