## Fifth Schedule

- Scheduled Area declared / altered by → {{**President**}} (Governor consulted where specified).
- Declaration criteria (administrative, {{**not in the Constitution**}}) → tribal preponderance; compact area; viable unit; economic backwardness.
- {{**State executive power continues**}} in Scheduled Areas.
- Governor reports to {{**President** — annually or whenever required}}; Union may give directions to the State.
- Administrative units → {{district = largest; cluster of villages in a block = lowest}}.

### Tribes Advisory Council
- Mandatory in every State with Scheduled Areas; also in a State with STs but no Scheduled Area {{if President directs}}.
- {{**Not more than 20** members}}; as nearly as may be {{**3/4** = ST MLAs}}.
- Advises on ST welfare matters referred by the Governor.

### Governor's powers
- By notification → a Central / State Act shall not apply, or apply with modifications (may be retrospective).
- Regulations for peace and good government → {{restrict transfer of tribal land; regulate land allotment; regulate money-lending}}.
- Regulations may amend / repeal laws → need {{**President's assent**}}; TAC consulted.

> [!TRAP] Fifth Schedule does !!not!! → make the area a UT · create an autonomous local body · give Special Category status · end State administration. Its PYQ consequence → **tribal land cannot be alienated to non-tribals**. · TAC is ≤ 20, not "20". · Governor does not declare Scheduled Areas; CM does not report to the Home Ministry.

## Sixth Schedule

- Only {{Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram}} → Arunachal, Nagaland, Manipur, Sikkim !!not!! covered.
- Tribal areas → **autonomous districts**; different tribes within one → Governor creates **autonomous regions**.
- Governor may organise / reorganise districts — area, boundary, name.
- District Council → {{**≤ 30** members; **≤ 4** nominated by Governor; rest elected}} *(Assam-specific exceptions — don't universalise "26 + 4")*.
- Councils hold {{legislative + executive + judicial + fiscal}} powers → land, non-reserved forests, shifting cultivation, village administration, inheritance, marriage, social customs; laws need {{Governor's assent}}.
- May run schools, dispensaries, markets, roads; set up village councils / courts; levy specified taxes.
- Autonomous districts {{remain within the executive authority of the State}}.

## PESA, 1996

- Provisions of the Panchayats (Extension to the Scheduled Areas) Act, {{**1996**}} → extends {{**Part IX**}} to {{Fifth-Schedule Scheduled Areas}}, with modifications.
- State panchayat law must conform to → customary law, social & religious practices, traditional management of community resources.
- **Gram Sabha** → safeguards customs, identity, community resources, customary dispute resolution; approves plans & projects; selects beneficiaries; issues {{**certificate of utilisation of funds**}}.
- Reservation → STs {{≥ **1/2** of seats}}; {{**all chairpersons**}} at every level = ST.

| Requirement | Applies to |
|---|---|
| **Consultation** | {{Land acquisition for development projects; resettlement & rehabilitation}} |
| **Mandatory prior recommendation** | {{Prospecting licence / mining lease / auction concession for **minor minerals**}} |

Powers specifically endowed → {{ownership of **minor forest produce**}} · prohibition / regulation of intoxicants · prevent & restore alienated tribal land · village markets · control money-lending · control social-sector institutions · local plans incl. tribal sub-plan · minor water bodies.

- Higher panchayat cannot assume powers of a lower one / Gram Sabha.

> [!TRAP] PESA mining recommendation = !!minor!! minerals only. · Consultation ≠ mandatory recommendation. · PESA ≠ autonomous regions and ≠ Sixth-Schedule areas. · Funds: Gram Sabha *certifies* utilisation — not "permits spending".

> [!DYN] Number of States that have notified PESA Rules changes — not kept here.

## Scheduled Tribes & PVTGs

### Art. 342 — who is an ST?
- {{**President**}} specifies by public notification; for a State → {{after **consulting** the Governor}}.
- Later inclusion / exclusion → {{only **Parliament by law**}}.
- ST status is {{State / UT-specific}}.

> [!TRAP] Governor neither declares nor notifies STs.

### PVTGs
- Administrative category within STs — not a constitutional list.
- {{**75** groups in **18 States + 1 UT** (Andaman & Nicobar)}}.
- Criteria → {{pre-agricultural technology · stagnant / declining population · very low literacy · subsistence economy}}.
- PYQ examples → Irular, Konda Reddi.

> [!TRAP] 75, !!not!! 95.

## Forest Rights Act, 2006

- Scheduled Tribes and Other Traditional Forest Dwellers (Recognition of Forest Rights) Act, 2006.
- {{**Gram Sabha**}} initiates determination of individual / community forest rights.
- Nodal ministry → {{**Tribal Affairs**}} (not Environment).
- Bamboo = {{**minor forest produce**}}; FRA gives ownership / collection / disposal rights over MFP.
- "Critical Wildlife Habitat" is defined in FRA.
- Habitat rights of PVTGs are !!not!! declared by MoEFCC alone.

> [!TRAP] The 2017 Indian Forest Act amendment dropped bamboo from "tree" only for bamboo grown in **non-forest** areas — it created no forest-area right.
