## Preamble — Skeleton & Exact Words

- Immediate basis → {{**Objectives Resolution** — moved by Jawaharlal Nehru}}, adopted by the Constituent Assembly.
- "**We, the People of India**" → people = ultimate source of authority.

| Component | Content |
|---|---|
| Source of authority | People of India |
| Nature of State | Sovereign · Socialist · Secular · Democratic · Republic |
| Objectives | Justice · Liberty · Equality · Fraternity |
| Adoption date | {{**26 November 1949**}} |

### Exact content → 3–5–2–1

| Element | Exact words |
|---|---|
| **Justice — 3** | {{Social, Economic, Political}} |
| **Liberty — 5** | {{Thought, Expression, Belief, Faith, Worship}} |
| **Equality — 2** | {{Status, Opportunity}} |
| **Fraternity — 1** | Assuring dignity of the individual + unity and integrity of the Nation |

> [!TRAP] **Federal** → !!not!! in Preamble. · **Economic liberty** → !!not!! in Preamble (economic *justice* is). · Order matters: Justice → Liberty → Equality → Fraternity.

## Original (1950) vs Present Wording

| Point | 26 Jan 1950 | Present |
|---|---|---|
| State-description | {{**Sovereign Democratic Republic**}} | Sovereign **Socialist Secular** Democratic Republic |
| Fraternity clause | unity of the Nation | unity **and integrity** of the Nation |

- {{**42nd Amendment, 1976**}} → inserted **Socialist**, **Secular**, **Integrity** (effective 3 Jan 1977).
- Preamble amended {{**only once**}}.
- `15 Aug 1947 → 25 Jan 1950` Dominion → `26 Jan 1950` Constitution commenced; Sovereign Democratic Republic.

> [!TRAP] Sovereign / Democratic / Republic = **original** words, not 42nd additions. · 26 Nov 1949 (adoption) ≠ 26 Jan 1950 (commencement). · No "Socialist/Secular" in the 1949 text ≠ no socialist/secular features before 1976.

## Legal Status & Cases

| Proposition | Position |
|---|---|
| Part of the Constitution? | {{**Yes**}} |
| Independently enforceable / justiciable? | {{**No**}} |
| Grants substantive rights by itself? | No |
| Aid to interpretation? | **Yes** |
| Source of legislative power? | {{No}} |
| Independent prohibition on legislative power? | {{No}} |
| Amendable under `Art. 368`? | {{Yes — subject to basic structure}} |

| Case | Holding |
|---|---|
| **Berubari Union (1960)** | Key to framers' mind; {{**not part** of Constitution}} *(historical view)* |
| **Kesavananda Bharati (1973)** | {{**Is part**; amendable, but basic features cannot be destroyed}} |
| **LIC of India (1995)** | Reaffirmed — integral part |

> [!TRAP] Part of Constitution ≠ enforceable. · Non-justiciable ≠ legally irrelevant. · Interpretive aid ≠ source of power; it cannot override an express provision.

## Commencement & Part XXII

| Article | Content |
|---|---|
| `Art. 393` | Short title → {{"Constitution of India"}} |
| `Art. 394` | Specified provisions in force at once (26 Nov 1949); the rest on {{**26 January 1950**}} |
| `Art. 394A` | Authoritative text in Hindi |
| `Art. 395` | Repealed {{Indian Independence Act, 1947 + Government of India Act, 1935}} (and amending enactments, with stated exception) |
