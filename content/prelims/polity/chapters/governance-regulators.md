## Status Rules

| Label | Test |
|---|---|
| Statutory body | Constituted under an Act; powers from statute |
| Executive body | Resolution / order; no constituting statute |
| Autonomous society | Registered society — {{≠ statutory}} |
| "Regulator" | A function — says nothing about legal status |
| "Under Ministry X" | Administrative home — {{≠ source of powers}} |
| "Enforces Act X" | {{≠ created by Act X}} |

Constitutional vs statutory vs executive bodies → [Statutory Bodies](@statutory-bodies#classification-key)

- Parliamentary review of independent regulators → {{Department-Related Standing Committees + ad hoc committees}} — not Finance Commission / NITI / FSLRC.

## Aadhaar & UIDAI

- **UIDAI** → {{statutory — Aadhaar Act, 2016}}; under {{MeitY}}.
- Aadhaar → identity number for {{**residents**}}; {{**not proof of citizenship or domicile**}} (s.9).
- s.7 → may be required for subsidies / benefits funded from the {{Consolidated Fund of India or a State}}; alternate ID must be allowed until enrolment.
- Authentication → number + demographic / OTP / biometric matched against CIDR.
- Aadhaar {{can be deactivated}}.

> [!VERIFY] Aadhaar metadata-retention period and private-entity use — the 2020 PYQ reflected the law of that moment; current position not resolved in the source pack.

## Digital Governance

- **DPI** → open, interoperable infrastructure → {{Aadhaar (identity) · UPI (payments) · DigiLocker / API Setu (documents)}}.
- **DigiLocker** → Digital India; authentic digital documents; MeitY.
- Open public platforms (UPSC 2022) → {{Aarogya Setu, CoWIN, DigiLocker, DIKSHA}}.
- ICT in governance → lowers cost; raises citizen access, input, transparency — does !!not!! inherently increase red tape.
