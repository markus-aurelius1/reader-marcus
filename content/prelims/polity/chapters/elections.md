| Anchor | Fact |
|---|---|

| `Art. 326` | Adult suffrage — citizen, {{**18+**}}; voting age 21 → 18 by {{**61st Amdt, 1988**}} (in force 1989) |
| `Arts. 327–328` | Parliament (and, residually, State Legislature) makes election law |

### RPA 1950 vs RPA 1951

| RPA 1950 | RPA 1951 |
|---|---|
| {{Allocation of seats}} | {{Qualifications / disqualifications of members}} |
| {{Electoral rolls; voter qualification}} | {{Conduct of elections — nomination, poll, counting}} |
| {{Delimitation framework}} | Registration of political parties; election expenses |
| Election officers | Corrupt practices & offences; election disputes |

- Election petition (Parliament / State Legislature) → {{**High Court**}}; appeal → Supreme Court.

## Voting System & the Voter

- LS + Assembly → **FPTP**: single-member constituency; {{most valid votes wins — **plurality**}}.
- 50% + 1 is !!not!! required; vote share ≠ seat share.
- RPA 1950 → s.17 not in more than one constituency · s.18 not twice in one · s.19 {{18+ on qualifying date **and ordinarily resident**}}.
- {{Name on the electoral roll}} decides the right to vote — holding an EPIC is not enough.
- Reserved constituency ≠ separate electorate → only candidates are restricted; all electors vote.

### Right to vote — what kind of right?
- UPSC key (2017) → {{**Constitutional right**}} — never mark "Fundamental Right".
- Courts have variously called it constitutional and statutory; keep the keyed answer, hold the label loosely.

## Candidates & Disqualification

- LS candidate → citizen; {{**25** years}}; an **elector** for any parliamentary constituency — need not live in the one contested.
- Proposers (s.33) → recognised party candidate {{**1**}}; independent / unrecognised party {{**10**}}.
- May contest at most {{**2** constituencies}} (s.33(7)); cannot keep both seats.
- Conviction with sentence {{≥ **2 years**}} (s.8(3)) → disqualified from conviction + {{**6 years** after release}} — not for life.
- Old s.8(4) three-month shield for sitting members → struck down (*Lily Thomas*).

## Delimitation

- Fixing boundaries of territorial constituencies.
- {{**Four** Commissions}} → Acts of 1952, 1962, 1972, 2002 (set up 1952, 1963, 1973, 2002).
- Composition (2002) → {{retired / serving SC judge (Chair) + CEC or an EC + State Election Commissioner}}; associate MPs / MLAs {{have no vote}}.
- Orders → {{force of law; cannot be questioned in court}}; laid before LS / Assembly, which {{cannot modify}} them.
- {{**84th Amdt, 2001**}} + {{87th Amdt, 2003}} → seat totals frozen on {{1971 Census}} till the first census after {{2026}}; boundaries & SC/ST seats redrawn on {{2001 Census}}.

> [!TRAP] Delimitation Commission is !!not!! advisory. · 84th Amendment is 2001, not 2002.

## Reservation in Legislatures

- SC / ST → seats in proportion to population (Arts. 330, 332).
- Keep apart from local-body reservation (Arts. 243D / 243T) → [Local Government](@local-government#panchayats-part-ix).

> [!DYN] Women's reservation (106th Amendment, 2023) — one-third of seats in Lok Sabha and State Assemblies; commencement is tied to a future delimitation. Implementation timeline not frozen here.

## Parties & Election Reforms

> [!UPPCS]
> **National party — any one**
> - {{6% votes in 4+ States + 4 LS seats}}
> - {{2% of LS seats from at least 3 States}}
> - {{State party in 4+ States}}
>
> **State party — any one**
> - 6% votes + 2 Assembly seats · 6% votes + 1 LS seat · 3% of Assembly seats or 3 seats (whichever more) · 1 LS seat per 25 allotted · 8% votes
>
> - Symbols → Election Symbols (Reservation and Allotment) Order, {{1968}}.
> - Party chronology → {{TDP 1982 → BSP 1984 → SP 1992 → AITC 1998}}.
> - National Voters' Day → {{25 January}}.
