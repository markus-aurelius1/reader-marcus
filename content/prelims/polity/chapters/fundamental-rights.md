## Part III — Architecture

- Part III = {{Arts. 12–35}}; presently {{**6** categories}} (originally 7 — property removed by the {{44th Amdt}}).
- Justiciable · qualified, not absolute · amendable **subject to basic structure**.

| Article | Content |
|---|---|
| `Art. 12` | "State" → Union Govt + Parliament; State Govts + Legislatures; local authorities; other authorities in India / under GoI control |
| `Art. 13` | Inconsistent law void {{**to the extent of inconsistency**}}. "Law" includes {{ordinance, order, bye-law, rule, regulation, notification, **custom / usage having force of law**}} |
| `Art. 13(4)` | Amendment under Art. 368 is not "law" here — yet still open to basic-structure review |

### Who can claim?

| Available to | Articles |
|---|---|
| **Any person** | {{14, 20, 21, 25}} (and most others) |
| **Citizens only** | {{15, 16, 19, 29, 30}} |
| Enforceable against **private persons** too | {{15(2), 17, 23, 24}} |

- An ordinance has the force of law but cannot abridge FRs.

> [!UPPCS] "Fundamental Rights — a pledge to our people and a pact with the civilised world" → {{Dr S. Radhakrishnan}}.

## Right to Equality — Articles 14–18

| Art. | Payload |
|---|---|
| **14** | Equality before law (negative, British) + equal protection of laws (positive, US). {{Class legislation barred; **reasonable classification** allowed}} → intelligible differentia + rational nexus. Arbitrariness / unguided discretion violates Art. 14 |
| **15** | No discrimination against citizens **only** on {{religion, race, caste, sex, place of birth}}. 15(3) women & children · 15(4) SEBC/SC/ST · 15(5) admissions incl. private institutions, {{**except minority institutions**}} · 15(6) EWS |
| **16** | Public employment. 16(3) residence requirement → only by {{**Parliament**}} · 16(4) backward classes · 16(4A) {{promotion — SC/ST}} · 16(4B) carry-forward · 16(6) EWS |
| **17** | {{Untouchability abolished}} — any form, punishable offence |
| **18** | No titles except {{military / academic distinctions}} |

> [!TRAP] Art. 17 belongs to Right to **Equality**, !!not!! Right against Exploitation. · Art. 335 mentions efficiency of administration but does not define it.

- National awards (Bharat Ratna, Padma) ≠ titles — cannot be used as prefix / suffix.
- Padma Awards instituted {{1954}}; suspended {{1978–79 and 1993–97}}; Bharat Ratna capped at {{**3** a year}}.

## Right to Freedom — Articles 19–22

### Article 19 — six freedoms (citizens)

| Freedom | Restriction grounds |
|---|---|
| Speech & expression | {{Sovereignty & integrity; security of State; friendly relations with foreign States; public order; decency / morality; contempt of court; defamation; incitement to offence}} |
| Peaceful assembly **without arms** | {{Sovereignty & integrity; public order}} |
| Association / union / co-operative society | {{Sovereignty & integrity; public order; morality}} |
| Movement · Residence | {{General public; interests of Scheduled Tribes}} |
| Profession / trade / business | General public; professional qualifications; State monopoly |

> [!TRAP] Property is !!not!! an Art. 19 freedom — 19(1)(f) omitted by the 44th Amdt.

### Articles 20–22

| Art. | Payload |
|---|---|
| **20** | Only three: {{no **ex-post-facto** criminal law · no **double jeopardy** (prosecuted *and* punished) · no compelled **self-incrimination**}} |
| **21** | No deprivation of life / personal liberty except by procedure established by law → *Maneka*: fair, just, reasonable ([due process](@political-theory#rule-of-law-and-due-process)) |
| **21A** | Free + compulsory education, {{**6–14** years}} — {{86th Amdt, 2002}} |
| **22** | Ordinary arrest: grounds informed + lawyer of choice + magistrate within {{**24 hours** excluding journey}} |

**Art. 21 — durable judicial expansion**: dignity · {{privacy — *K.S. Puttaswamy* (9 judges, 2017)}} · marry a person of one's choice · healthy environment · livelihood · shelter / health · free legal aid · speedy & fair trial · travel abroad.

- Privacy is not absolute → test: {{legality → legitimate aim → proportionality}}.

> [!TRAP] Art. 20 has no "torture" clause. · *Puttaswamy* 2017 is the privacy case — the Aadhaar merits judgment came later. · Education as a right (*Unni Krishnan*) was till age 14, not "secondary level".

### Preventive detention — Art. 22(3)–(7)
- Art. 22(1)–(2) safeguards do **not** apply to {{enemy aliens and preventive detainees}}.
- Detainee → grounds communicated "as soon as may be" + earliest opportunity of representation; facts against public interest may be withheld.
- Beyond {{**3 months**}} → Advisory Board needed.

> [!TRAP] The 44th Amdt's reduction to **2 months** has !!never been notified!! — 3 months stands.

## Exploitation, Religion & Minorities — Articles 23–30

| Art. | Payload |
|---|---|
| **23** | Trafficking, *begar*, forced labour barred; compulsory public service allowed without discrimination |
| **24** | No child {{below **14**}} in {{factory, mine or hazardous employment}} |
| **25** | Conscience + profess, practise, propagate — subject to {{public order, morality, health and other FRs}}; secular activity regulable |
| **26** | Denominations → institutions, manage religious affairs, property — subject to public order, morality, health |
| **27** | No tax {{specifically appropriated}} for promoting a particular religion |
| **28** | Wholly State-maintained → no religious instruction; aided / recognised → none without consent |
| **29(1)** | {{**Any section of citizens**}} with distinct language, script, culture → conserve it |
| **29(2)** | No denial of admission to State-maintained / aided institution on religion, race, caste, language |
| **30** | {{**Religious or linguistic minorities**}} → establish & administer educational institutions |

> [!TRAP] Right against Exploitation = Arts. 23–24 only. · Art. 29(1) is !!not!! minority-only. · The Constitution does not define "minority".

## Property & Articles 31A–31C

- `Art. 31` omitted (44th Amdt) → {{`Art. 300A`: "no person shall be deprived of his property save by authority of law"}}.
- 300A = {{constitutional / legal right, **not** a Fundamental Right}}; available to {{any person}}; no Art. 32 petition on it alone.
- **31A** → estate-acquisition etc. laws immune from Arts. 14 & 19 (State law needs President's assent).
- **31B** → Ninth Schedule; [no blanket immunity](@parts-schedules#ninth-schedule).
- **31C** → operative only for laws implementing {{**Art. 39(b), (c)**}}; 42nd Amdt extension to all DPSP struck in *Minerva Mills*.

## Article 32 & Writs

- `Art. 32` is {{itself a Fundamental Right}}; SC issues directions, orders, five writs.
- Parliament may empower any other court to issue them (without derogating SC's power).

| Writ | Literally | Use | Trap |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Habeas Corpus** | {{have the body}} | Release from unlawful detention | Lies against {{public **or private** detention}}; locus relaxed |
| **Mandamus** | {{we command}} | Compel a public / legal duty | Not for a purely private duty; but {{a private body performing a **public duty** is reachable}} |
| **Prohibition** | to forbid | Higher court stops lower court / tribunal exceeding jurisdiction | {{**Preventive** — before proceedings end; only judicial / quasi-judicial bodies}} |
| **Certiorari** | {{to be certified / informed}} | Quash order of inferior court / tribunal | {{**Corrective / curative**}} |
| **Quo Warranto** | {{by what authority}} | Test claim to a public office | Petitioner need not show personal injury; office must be public & substantive |

| | SC — Art. 32 | HC — Art. 226 |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | {{FRs only}} | {{FRs **and "any other purpose"**}} → wider |
| Nature | Itself an FR | Constitutional power |
| Territory | All India | Where cause of action arises wholly / partly — even if the authority sits outside |

> [!TRAP] Prohibition **stops**; Certiorari **quashes**. · Prohibition lies against a lower court — not a government officer or legislature.

## Articles 33–35 & Emergency

- `Art. 33` → {{**Parliament**}} may restrict FRs of armed forces, police, intelligence personnel.
- `Art. 34` → indemnity for acts during martial law. `Art. 35` → laws under 16(3), 32(3), 33, 34 → only Parliament.
- `Art. 358` → Art. 19 suspended automatically only when Emergency is on {{**war / external aggression**}} — not armed rebellion.
- `Art. 359` → President may suspend the *right to move courts*; {{**Arts. 20 & 21 can never be included**}}.

### UDHR cue
- UDHR 1948 covers education, equal access to public service, adequate standard of living incl. food.
- Reflected in India across Preamble + FRs + DPSP + FDs — not every UDHR right is an enforceable FR.
