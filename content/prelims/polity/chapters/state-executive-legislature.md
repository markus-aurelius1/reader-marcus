## Governor

- **Removal** → no procedure or grounds in the Constitution; pleasure of President.
- *B.P. Singhal* → removal cannot be {{arbitrary, whimsical, capricious}}; open to {{limited judicial review}}.
- Vacancy / contingency → President decides (Art. 160); HC Chief Justice is !!not!! an automatic successor.
- Constitutional head + federal link — not an "agent" or employee of the Centre.

### Discretion — Art. 163
- Constitution expressly recognises discretion.
- PYQ examples → {{report recommending **President's Rule**}} · {{**reserving** a Bill for the President}} · inviting a leader when majority is unclear (majority tested on the floor).
- !!Not!! discretionary → appointing ministers (CM's advice); making business rules (Art. 166).

### Bills — Arts. 200 / 201
> [!FLOW] Bill passed → Governor: **assent** · **withhold** · **return** (not Money Bills) · **reserve for President** → if returned and repassed → Governor {{**shall not** withhold assent}}

- Must reserve → Bill endangering the {{position of the High Court}}.
- President acts only on a Bill the Governor has reserved — {{cannot reserve a State Bill suo motu}}.

### Ordinance — Art. 213
- Only when the legislature (both Houses, if bicameral) is not in session.
- Prior instructions of President needed in three specified cases (where the Bill itself would need them).

### Clemency — Art. 161
- Offences against laws within the State's executive power.
- Court-martial sentences → {{President only}}.
- Death sentences → Governor's power is not excluded where the offence is within the State field; overlaps with the President's.

### Immunity — Art. 361
- Not answerable to any court for official acts.
- {{No criminal proceedings}} instituted or continued during term; no arrest.
- Civil suit for personal acts → {{**2 months'** written notice}}.
- Does not protect the Government itself; ends with office.

### Sarkaria Commission on Governors
1. {{Eminent in some walk of life}}
2. {{From **outside** the State}}
3. {{Detached from local politics}}
4. {{Not recently active in politics}}

- CM should be consulted on the appointment.

> [!TRAP] "Governor for each State" ≠ a separate person for each. · 5-year term ≠ security of tenure. · Art. 155 = appointment; 156 = term / pleasure. · Governor as university Chancellor comes from State law, !!not!! the Constitution.

## State Council of Ministers & Chief Secretary

- CM appointed by Governor; ministers on CM's advice.
- Collectively responsible to the {{**Legislative Assembly**}} — not the Council.
- `Art. 164(1A)` (91st Amdt) → ministers incl. CM {{≤ **15%** of Assembly; minimum **12**}}.
- Non-legislator minister → out after {{6 consecutive months}}.

### Chief Secretary
- Administrative head; {{**not** a constitutional office}}.
- "Appointed by the Governor" → {{false}} (chosen by CM / State executive). "Fixed tenure" → {{false}}.
- 2nd ARC's fixed 2-year tenure proposal was not accepted.

## Presiding Officers & Procedure

- Speaker, Deputy Speaker elected by Assembly; Chairman, Deputy Chairman elected by {{the Council itself}}.
- Removal → {{majority of all the then members}} + 14 days' notice.
- Speaker continues after dissolution till {{just before the new Assembly's first meeting}}.
- Speaker certifies Money Bills — final.

| Matter | Rule |
|---|---|
| Ordinary Bill — Council's first delay | {{**3 months**}} |
| After Assembly repasses | {{**1 month**}} |
| Maximum delay | {{**4 months** — not 6}} |
| Deadlock | {{**No joint sitting**}} |
| Bill starting in Council, rejected by Assembly | {{Dies}} |
| Money Bill | Assembly only; Council recommends within {{14 days}} |

- `Art. 176` → Governor's special address → {{first session after each general election + first session of each year}}.
- `Art. 208` → each House makes its own rules; falling back on Lok Sabha practice where rules are silent is convention, not Art. 208 text.
- `Art. 194` → no court proceedings for anything said or any vote given in the House / committee.
