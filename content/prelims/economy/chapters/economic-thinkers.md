- Optimum population → the size that maximises per-capita output.

## Gresham's Law {#greshams-law}

- "{{Bad money drives out good}}."
- Two monies at a fixed legal rate → the overvalued ("bad") circulates; the undervalued ("good") is hoarded, exported or melted.
- Holds under an **imposed** exchange ratio — not a free-market rule.

Legal tender → [Money](@money-rbi-monetary-policy#money-and-legal-tender)
