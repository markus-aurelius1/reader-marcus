## Money Market vs Capital Market {#money-vs-capital-market}

| | Money market | Capital market |
|---|---|---|
| Horizon | {{≤ 1 year}} | > 1 year + equity |
| Purpose | Liquidity, working capital | Investment, capital formation |
| Instruments | {{Call / notice money, T-bills, CP, CD, repo / TREPS}} | {{Dated G-Secs, corporate bonds, debentures, shares}} |

- First cut on any instrument → ==maturity · issuer · claim · regulator · tradability==.

> [!TRAP] Money market is not "usually unsecured" — repo / TREPS are secured. · Government bond market = capital market; T-bill market = money market.

## Money-Market Instruments {#money-market-instruments}

| Instrument | Core |
|---|---|
| **Call money** | {{Overnight, unsecured, interbank}} |
| Notice / term money | Longer short-term interbank |
| **Commercial Paper** | {{Unsecured promissory note}} of corporates / financial entities |
| **Certificate of Deposit** | {{Negotiable deposit instrument issued by banks / FIs}} |
| **Treasury Bill** | {{Central Government; issued at discount, no coupon}}; 91 / 182 / 364 days |
| **Repo / TREPS** | Collateralised borrowing against securities |

- **CBLO** → money-market instrument; legacy name → now {{Triparty Repo — TREPS}}.
- States → issue {{**SDLs**}}, not T-bills.

> [!TRAP] CD is not an RBI loan to companies. · T-bills are not issued by State Governments.

## Government Securities & Yields {#government-securities}

- **G-Sec** → tradable debt of Central / State Government. Centre → T-bills + dated securities. States → SDLs.
- RBI manages the auction; {{Government is the debtor}}.
- Retail access → RBI Retail Direct. **NDS-OM** → {{RBI's order-matching platform — not a depository}}.
- {{Price ↑ → yield ↓}}.
- Yield drivers → policy rate, inflation, government borrowing, liquidity, global yields / Fed.
- Insurers, pension funds, retail investors, FPIs → can hold G-Secs and corporate bonds.
- US Treasury → sovereign debt.

Government borrowing and debt accounting → [Budget](@budget-fiscal-policy#funds-and-public-debt) · RBI's OMO → [Monetary policy](@money-rbi-monetary-policy#omo-sterilisation-transmission)

## Debt vs Equity {#debt-vs-equity}

| | Bond / debenture | Equity share | Preference share |
|---|---|---|---|
| Holder | {{Lender / creditor}} | {{Owner}} | Preferential owner |
| Return | Interest (coupon) | Dividend — not guaranteed | Preferential dividend |
| On liquidation | {{Paid before shareholders}} | Residual, last | Before equity |
| Risk | Lower | Higher | In between |

- Bonds / debentures → secured **or** unsecured; convertible **or** non-convertible.
- Preference shares → need not convert.

> [!TRAP] "Bonds are always security-backed" → wrong. · "Debentures / preference shares must convert into equity" → wrong.

## Special Bonds {#special-bonds}

| Bond | Core | Trap |
|---|---|---|
| **Zero-coupon** | No coupon; sold at discount | ≠ interest-paying bond |
| **Convertible** | Converts to equity → {{lower coupon}}; equity upside | Not guaranteed inflation cover |
| **Inflation-Indexed** | Principal / coupon linked to inflation → hedge | {{No blanket tax exemption}} |
| **Masala** | {{Rupee-denominated, issued offshore}}; currency risk on the investor | IFC popularised it; IFC not required |
| **Sustainability** | Funds {{environmental + social}} projects | ≠ green-only bond |
| **Sovereign Gold Bond** | Government debt in grams of gold; issued via RBI; interest + gold-linked redemption | No physical gold |

- **Gold Monetisation Scheme** → idle gold into the formal system.
- SGB + GMS → {{mobilise idle gold + cut gold imports}}. Not FDI in jewellery.

Masala bonds, ECB → [External Sector](@external-sector#external-debt)

## Market Structure {#market-structure}

- **Primary** → fresh issue (IPO, FPO, rights) → issuer gets funds. **Secondary** → existing securities trade.
- BSE / NSE → equity, derivatives **and debt** segments.
- Depositories → {{**NSDL, CDSL**}}. Depository Participant = investor's access point.
- Demat account ≠ trading account ≠ bank account.
- **Beta** → {{sensitivity of a security to the market}}; > 1 = more volatile than the benchmark.
- **Venture capital** → long-term risk capital for early-stage ventures.

> [!UPPCS] SENSEX → BSE; {{30}} companies. NIFTY 50 → NSE.

> [!DYN] Index constituents, market-cap ranks, F&O volumes, FPI investment limits (a 2020 Budget limit was once asked).

## Regulators {#regulators}

| Regulator | Domain |
|---|---|
| **SEBI** | Stock exchanges, brokers, mutual funds, AIFs, listed-company disclosure, {{credit-rating agencies}}, securities derivatives |
| **RBI** | Banks, monetary policy, G-Sec and money markets, forex, payment systems, NBFCs |
| **IRDAI** | Insurance |
| **PFRDA** | Pensions / NPS |

- **Credit rating** → opinion on default risk, {{not a guarantee}}. ICRA → public limited company. Brickwork → Indian CRA. Lower rating → higher yield.
- **BRSR** → listed-entity ESG disclosure → {{SEBI}}, not RBI.
- **L.C. Gupta Committee** → {{SEBI — derivatives roadmap}}.

## Funds & Trusts {#funds-and-trusts}

| Vehicle | Core |
|---|---|
| Mutual fund | Pooled; investors hold units; NAV |
| **ETF** | Fund units {{traded on the exchange intraday}} |
| **AIF** | Privately pooled vehicle under SEBI |
| **InvIT** | Trust holding infrastructure assets |
| **REIT** | Trust holding income-generating real estate |

- AIF → Cat I (venture capital, infrastructure) · Cat II (PE, debt) · Cat III ({{hedge funds}}).
- InvIT → recognised as a borrower under SARFAESI.

> [!TRAP] Bonds and stocks are securities, not AIFs. · ETF ≠ every passive mutual fund. · A physical capital asset is not a financial instrument; ETFs and currency swaps are.

> [!DYN] InvIT / REIT distribution tax rules are amendment-sensitive — the 2023 statement "interest exempt, dividend taxable" was false as on that date.

## Derivatives & P-Notes {#derivatives-and-p-notes}

| Contract | Core |
|---|---|
| Forward | Customised, OTC |
| Futures | Standardised, exchange-traded, margined |
| **Option** | Buyer has {{the right, not the obligation}}; pays premium |

> [!TRAP] P-notes are regulated, !!not!! banned.
