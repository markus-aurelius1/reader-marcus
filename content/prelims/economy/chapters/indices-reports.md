## Component Traps {#component-traps}

### GHI
- Four indicators → {{undernourishment · child wasting · child stunting · under-five mortality}}.
- Wasting = low weight-for-height. Stunting = low height-for-age.

### PQLI
- {{Morris D. Morris}}.
- Components → {{infant mortality · life expectancy at age one · basic literacy}}.
- Income is !!not!! a component.

### HCI
- World Bank **Human Capital Index** → survival, schooling, health. ≠ UNDP HDI.
