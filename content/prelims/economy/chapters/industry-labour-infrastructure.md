## Industrial Policy {#industrial-policy}

### New Industrial Policy, 1991
- Industrial licensing → {{abolished except a short notified list}}.
- Public-sector reservation → sharply cut.
- MRTP asset limits on large firms → removed.
- Foreign investment and technology → liberalised.

> [!TRAP] 1991 = delicensing + competition + private / foreign entry — not the end of all regulation.

Reform logic → [1991 & LPG](@planning-reform#lpg)

### SEZ
- {{`SEZ Act, 2005`}} + Rules 2006 → export / investment zones; simpler approvals and customs.
- Board of Approval (Centre) + Development Commissioner (zone).

## CSR {#csr}

- {{`Companies Act, 2013` — s.135}}.
- Applies if, in the preceding FY, **any one** → net worth ≥ {{₹500 crore}} · turnover ≥ {{₹1,000 crore}} · net profit ≥ {{₹5 crore}}.
- Spend ≥ {{**2%** of average net profit of the 3 preceding FYs}}.
- Not CSR → normal course of business · activities only for employees and their families · political contributions.

> [!TRAP] Not "Companies Act 2014"; not "1%".

## Labour Law {#labour-law}

- {{Four Labour Codes}} → in force {{21 Nov 2025}}; replaced {{29}} central laws.

| Code | Field | Subsumes |
|---|---|---|
| **Wages, 2019** | Wages, minimum wage, bonus, equal pay | {{Payment of Wages · Minimum Wages · Payment of Bonus · Equal Remuneration}} |
| **Industrial Relations, 2020** | Unions, standing orders, disputes | Trade Unions · Standing Orders · Industrial Disputes |
| **Social Security, 2020** | EPF, ESI, gratuity, maternity, unorganised workers | EPF · ESI · Maternity Benefit · Gratuity |
| **OSHWC, 2020** | Safety, factories, mines, contract and migrant labour | 13 laws |

- Maternity benefit → {{26 weeks}} (fewer than two surviving children); {{12 weeks}} (two or more).
- ILO → {{**C138** — minimum age}}; {{**C182** — worst forms of child labour}}. India ratified both in {{2017}}.

> [!TRAP] "Casual worker" label alone does not decide EPF cover — no "all" or "none". · Older questions quote the repealed Acts; current answer = the Codes.

> [!UPPCS] Contract Labour Act is !!not!! one of the four laws merged into the Code on Wages.
