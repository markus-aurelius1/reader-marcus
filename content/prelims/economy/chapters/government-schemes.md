## Scheme Architecture {#scheme-architecture}

| Type | Rule | Trap |
|---|---|---|
| **Central Sector** | {{Funded and implemented by the Union}} | "Central scheme" ≠ Central Sector |
| **Centrally Sponsored** | Union part-funds; {{States implement}}; sharing varies | No universal 60 : 40 |
| **DBT** | Benefit straight to the beneficiary's account | A delivery channel, not a scheme type |
| **Statutory guarantee** | Entitlement from an Act | Stronger than a discretionary scheme |

==Scheme → objective → target group → ministry → one exclusion → famous trap.==

## Rural Employment & Livelihoods {#rural-employment}

| | MGNREGA, 2005 | VB–G RAM G Act, 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Status | {{Repealed 1 July 2026}} | {{In force from 1 July 2026}} |
| Guarantee | 100 days a year | {{**125 days**}} |
| Who | Rural household adults volunteering for unskilled manual work | Same |
| Nature | Statutory, demand-driven | Statutory, demand-driven; unemployment allowance |
| Funding | Historical | {{60 : 40}}; 90 : 10 for NE / Himalayan |

- Entitlement → not limited to BPL or to any caste.
- **DAY-NRLM** → {{Ministry of Rural Development}}; SHGs and federations → credit, livelihoods.
- **DDU-GKY** → rural-youth skilling and placement.

> [!TRAP] MGNREGA is no longer the current law — older questions test it as it stood. · NRLM does not hand out free inputs or set up industries.
