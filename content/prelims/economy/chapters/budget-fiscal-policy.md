## Budget Accounts {#budget-accounts}

| Item | Test | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Revenue receipt** | {{Creates no liability, reduces no asset}} | Taxes, fees, dividends, {{interest received}} |
| **Capital receipt** | {{Creates a liability **or** reduces an asset}} | Borrowing, recovery of loans, disinvestment |
| **Revenue expenditure** | No asset created, no liability reduced | Salaries, pensions, interest payments, subsidies, most grants |
| **Capital expenditure** | {{Creates an asset or reduces a liability}} | Infrastructure, equity purchase, loans given, repayment of principal |

- Non-debt capital receipts → {{recovery of loans + disinvestment}}.
- Plan / Non-Plan classification → legacy; dropped from {{2017–18}}.

> [!TRAP] Interest **received** on loans = revenue receipt. · How a firm finances itself (debt / equity) is not "capital expenditure". · Borrowing = capital receipt.

Budget procedure, grants, Money Bill → [Polity: Budget & Financial Procedure](@prelims/polity/parliament#budget-and-financial-procedure)

## Deficits {#deficits}

| Deficit | Formula |
|---|---|
| **Revenue Deficit** | {{Revenue expenditure − revenue receipts}} |
| **Fiscal Deficit** | {{Total expenditure − revenue receipts − non-debt capital receipts}} = net borrowing need |
| **Primary Deficit** | {{Fiscal deficit − interest payments}} |
| Effective Revenue Deficit | Revenue deficit − grants for creation of capital assets |

- Worked anchor → revenue expenditure 80,000, revenue receipts 60,000 → RD = {{20,000}}. Borrowing 10,000, interest 6,000 → PD = {{4,000}}.
- Cutting the deficit → trim revenue expenditure / rationalise subsidies; raise revenue.

> [!TRAP] RD ≠ FD ≠ PD. · The traditional "budget deficit" is no longer the headline measure — it was not literally abolished.

## Fiscal Policy {#fiscal-policy}

| Stance | Action | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Expansionary** | G ↑ and / or T ↓ | Aggregate demand ↑ — against recession; inflation risk |
| **Contractionary** | G ↓ and / or T ↑ | Aggregate demand ↓ — disinflation |
| Counter-cyclical | Restrain in boom, support in slump | Smooths the cycle |
| Pro-cyclical | Moves with the cycle | Amplifies swings |

- **Fiscal stimulus** → deliberate expansionary action.
- {{**Crowding out**}} → government borrowing ↑ → interest rates ↑ / loanable funds absorbed → {{private investment ↓}}.
- **Deficit financing** → borrowing from the public, from abroad, or from the central bank.
- ==Financing by new money creation → most inflationary== → [Inflation: causes](@inflation#causes).
- Fiscal answer to inflation → cut duties / taxes on essentials, restrain spending → [Inflation: control](@inflation#control).

> [!TRAP] Expansionary fiscal policy ≠ monetary easing — different authority, different tools → [RBI tools](@money-rbi-monetary-policy#policy-tools).

## FRBM {#frbm}

- {{`FRBM Act, 2003`}} → fiscal discipline, transparency, medium-term fiscal management.
- Statements laid with the Budget → {{**Macro-Economic Framework Statement**}} + medium-term fiscal policy / fiscal-strategy statements.
- **N.K. Singh Committee** (FRBM review) → debt as the anchor + escape clause; recommended general-government debt {{60% of GDP — Centre 40% + States 20%}}.

> [!TRAP] "FD must always be 3%" and "60% debt" are a historical statute target and a committee recommendation — not timeless current law. Current framework = debt glide path.

## Funds & Public Debt {#funds-and-public-debt}

| Fund | Article | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **Consolidated Fund of India** | {{`Art. 266`}} | All revenues, loans raised, loan recoveries; withdrawal needs appropriation |
| **Public Account** | {{`Art. 266`}} | Money held in trust (PF, small savings); no appropriation vote |
| **Contingency Fund** | {{`Art. 267`}} | Advances for unforeseen expenditure pending authorisation |

- Union borrowing → {{`Art. 292`}}. State borrowing → {{`Art. 293`}}.
- **Public debt** = internal + external. Government liabilities = public debt + other liabilities (Public Account).
- **Off-budget borrowing** → by public entities, serviced by government, outside the headline deficit → transparency risk.

G-Sec instruments and trading → [Financial Markets](@financial-markets#government-securities)

## Disinvestment {#disinvestment}

- Proceeds → {{non-debt capital receipt}}.
- Government can sell equity and keep management control.

> [!TRAP] Disinvestment ≠ privatisation. · Proceeds are not earmarked for repaying external debt.

- Horizontal criteria reward need, equity and tax effort; weights change with each Commission.
