## Poverty Concepts {#poverty-concepts}

| Concept | Meaning | Cue |
|---|---|---|
| **Absolute** | Below a fixed minimum-needs standard | Normative threshold |
| **Relative** | Deprivation relative to the prevailing / median standard | Inequality dimension |
| **Human** | Deprivation in capabilities and services beyond income | Health, education, shelter, dignity |
| **Multidimensional** | Simultaneous weighted deprivations | Not an income line |
| **Social exclusion** | Shut out of normal participation / access | Cause and consequence of poverty |
| **Vulnerability** | Higher risk of falling into poverty after a shock | Risk, not current status |

- {{**Headcount Ratio** = persons below the poverty line ÷ population}}.
- India measured poverty on {{consumption expenditure}} — income is volatile and under-reported.
- State poverty lines differ because {{price levels differ}}.

> [!TRAP] Absolute ≠ relative. · Poverty ≠ inequality.

Growth without development → [Growth vs Development](@national-income-growth#growth-vs-development)

## Inequality & Inclusive Growth {#inequality-and-inclusive-growth}

- **Lorenz curve** → cumulative population share vs cumulative income share; farther from the line of equality = more unequal.
- **Gini coefficient** → {{0 = perfect equality; 1 = perfect inequality}}.
- **Kuznets hypothesis** → inequality first rises, then falls with development → {{inverted U}}. A hypothesis, not a law.

### Inclusive growth
- Broad-based growth → excluded groups **participate in and benefit from** it.
- Pace **and** pattern both matter → productive employment, access to markets and resources, health / education / skills, regional and social inclusion.
- Instruments → SHGs, MSMEs, access to education and public services.

> [!TRAP] Inclusive growth ≠ redistribution after growth. · Strengthening capital markets alone is not inclusive growth.

## Employment Indicators {#employment-indicators}

| Indicator | Formula |
|---|---|
| Labour force | Employed + unemployed (seeking / available) |
| **LFPR** | {{Labour force ÷ population × 100}} |
| **WPR** | {{Employed ÷ population × 100}} |
| **Unemployment Rate** | {{Unemployed ÷ **labour force** × 100}} |
| **Employment elasticity** | {{%Δ employment ÷ %Δ output}}; low + high growth → jobless growth |

| Status | Reference period |
|---|---|
| **Usual Status (PS + SS)** | {{365 days}}; principal + subsidiary activity |
| **Current Weekly Status** | {{7 days}}; captures seasonal change |

- **PLFS** → {{NSO, MoSPI}}; annual + higher-frequency urban estimates.
- No universal "working age" → state the survey's cohort (PLFS reports 15+, 15–59).
- **Labour Bureau** → data on industrial disputes, closures, retrenchment, lay-offs.

> [!TRAP] UR's denominator is the labour force, not the population.

> [!VERIFY] UPPCS 2019 item on female LFPR — option boundaries unclear in the record; evidence of demand only.

> [!DYN] Current LFPR, WPR, unemployment rate.

## Unemployment Types {#unemployment-types}

| Type | Cue |
|---|---|
| **Disguised** | More workers than needed → {{marginal productivity ≈ zero}}; removing some leaves output unchanged |
| **Seasonal** | No work in the off-season |
| **Frictional** | Search / matching gap between jobs |
| **Structural** | Skills or location mismatch |
| **Cyclical** | Recession cuts demand |
| Open | Willing and available, no work |
| Educated | Qualified, no suitable job |
| Voluntary | Declines available work |

> [!UPPCS] "Culture of poverty", older poverty surveys and book–author pairs recur in UPPCS — the pack records the demand, not the answers.
