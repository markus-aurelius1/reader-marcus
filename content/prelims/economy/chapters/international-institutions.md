| Arm | Client / function | Trap |
|---|---|---|
| **IBRD** | Loans, guarantees to {{middle-income and creditworthy low-income governments}}; born for post-war reconstruction | {{Not the concessional window}} |
| **IDA** | {{Poorest countries — grants, concessional credits}} | ≠ IBRD |

- **Global Infrastructure Facility** → World Bank; prepares and structures complex infrastructure PPPs.

## IMF {#imf}

- Mandate → monetary cooperation, surveillance, temporary BoP financing.
- **Quota** → in {{SDRs}}; decides subscription, votes, SDR allocation, access. Paid {{25% in reserve assets + 75% in own currency}}.
- **Reserve Tranche** (old "gold tranche") → member's liquid claim on the IMF; part of its reserves; {{drawing it is not IMF credit}}.

| SDR | |
|---|---|
| Nature | {{Reserve asset and unit of account — not a currency, not a claim on the IMF}} |
| Claim | On freely usable currencies of members |
| Basket | {{USD, EUR, CNY, JPY, GBP}} |
| Allocation vs holdings | Interest arises when the two differ |

| Facility | Window | For |
|---|---|---|
| **RFI** | General Resources Account | {{Any member}} with urgent BoP need |
| **RCF** | PRGT — {{concessional}} | {{Low-income members}} |

- **IMFC** → ministerial advisory body of the IMF; World Bank attends as observer.
- International liquidity → availability of hard / reserve currencies.

> [!TRAP] The 25% reserve portion is not "the five SDR-basket currencies". · SDR **allocation** ≠ SDR **holdings**.

> [!DYN] SDR basket weights; quota and vote shares.

## WTO Agreements {#wto-agreements}

- WTO → {{1995}}, Uruguay Round / Marrakesh Agreement; successor to GATT.
- **MFN** → no discrimination among trading partners. **National Treatment** → no discrimination against imports once inside.

| Agreement | Domain | Key |
|---|---|---|
| GATT 1994 | Goods | Tariff / non-tariff discipline |
| **GATS** | Services | {{4 modes of supply}} |
| **TRIPS** | Intellectual property | Minimum standards |
| **TRIMs** | {{Investment measures affecting trade in **goods** only}} | Bars local-content / trade-balancing rules |
| **AoA** | Agriculture | Market access · domestic support · export competition |
| **SPS** | Food safety, plant / animal health | Science-based |
| **TFA** | Customs procedures | {{Bali 2013}}; in force {{2017}}; India ratified 2016 |

- **Anti-dumping** → export price below normal value + injury. India → {{**DGTR** investigates; Finance Ministry levies}}.
- **Countervailing duty** → offsets foreign subsidy.

> [!TRAP] TRIMs is not a general code on foreign investment. · MFN does not prohibit FTAs — recognised exception. · DGFT is not the anti-dumping authority.

## AoA Boxes & Peace Clause {#aoa-boxes}

| Box | Nature | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| **Green** | {{Minimal distortion}} — research, decoupled support | No cap |
| **Amber** | {{Trade-distorting}} — price / product support | De minimis {{5% developed · 10% developing}} |
| **Blue** | {{Production-limiting}} direct payments | No cap |

- **Peace Clause** → interim, conditional cover for developing countries' {{public stockholding for food security}}.

> [!TRAP] Peace Clause is not blanket immunity. · 10% de minimis is not a universal subsidy ceiling.

## TRIPS, GI & Patents {#trips-gi-patents}

- {{**GI is an intellectual-property right**}} → links quality / reputation to origin.
- {{`GI of Goods (Registration and Protection) Act, 1999`}} → TRIPS obligation; valid {{10 years}}, renewable; Registry at {{Chennai}}.
- Patents Act **s.3(j)** → no patent on {{plants, animals, seeds, varieties, essentially biological processes}} (microorganisms excepted).
- Plant varieties → {{sui generis law}}, not patents.
- **National IPR Policy 2016** → [Industry](@industry-labour-infrastructure#ipr-and-make-in-india).

> [!TRAP] IPAB existed when asked in 2019 → abolished by the 2021 tribunal reforms; appellate work moved to the courts.

## Trade Agreements {#trade-agreements}

| Level | Adds |
|---|---|
| **PTA** | Lower tariffs on selected goods |
| **FTA** | Tariffs removed among members; own external tariff |
| **Customs Union** | {{+ common external tariff}} |
| **Common Market** | {{+ free movement of labour and capital}} |
| **Economic Union** | + harmonised economic policy |

- **Rules of Origin** → stop {{trade deflection}} through a low-tariff member. Certificate of Origin proves it.
- CECA / CEPA / ECTA → agreement labels, not a legal hierarchy.
- **BTIA** → {{India–EU}}.
- TPP → trade pact; not all Pacific Rim states, not a security alliance.
- US–EU Trade and Technology Council → coordination forum.

> [!DYN] ASEAN FTA partners, India–Australia ECTA details, new FTAs — read each list as of its year.
