## Five-Year Plans {#five-year-plans}

| Plan | Period | Identifier |
|---|---|---|
| **1st** | 1951–56 | {{**Harrod–Domar**}}; agriculture, irrigation, power |
| **2nd** | 1956–61 | {{**P.C. Mahalanobis**}}; rapid industrialisation — heavy, basic and capital-goods industries; socialist pattern |
| **3rd** | 1961–66 | Self-reliant, self-generating economy; hit by wars and drought |
| **Plan Holiday** | {{1966–69}} | {{Three Annual Plans}} |
| **4th** | 1969–74 | Growth with stability + self-reliance; {{correction of concentration of wealth and economic power}} |
| **5th** | 1974–79 | Removal of poverty + self-reliance; {{terminated 1978}} by the Janata government |
| **Rolling Plan** | {{1978–80}} | Janata government; revised every year |
| **6th** | 1980–85 | Poverty alleviation + modernisation |
| **7th** | 1985–90 | {{Food, work and productivity}} |
| **Annual Plans** | {{1990–92}} | 8th Plan deferred |
| **8th** | 1992–97 | Post-1991; {{human development}}, employment; indicative planning |
| **9th** | 1997–2002 | {{Growth with social justice and equity}} |
| **10th** | 2002–07 | Faster growth + monitorable targets |
| **11th** | 2007–12 | {{Towards Faster and More Inclusive Growth}} |
| **12th** | 2012–17 | {{Faster, More Inclusive and Sustainable Growth}} — last Plan |

> [!FLOW] 2nd (Mahalanobis) → 3rd → **Plan Holiday 1966–69** → 4th → 5th (cut short 1978) → **Rolling Plan 1978–80** → 6th → 7th → **Annual Plans 1990–92** → 8th

> [!TRAP] "Financial sector made an integral part of the Plan for the first time in the 5th Plan" → false. · Capital-goods thrust = 2nd; concentration of wealth = 4th.

## 1991 Crisis & Reform Logic {#crisis-1991}

- Trigger → fiscal + {{balance-of-payments crisis}}, low reserves → [BoP](@external-sector#bop-accounts).

| Track | Horizon | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **Stabilisation** | Short-run, demand side | Fiscal restraint, inflation and BoP management |
| **Structural adjustment** | Long-run, supply side | Industrial deregulation, trade and investment reform, financial / tax / public-sector reform |

## LPG {#lpg}

| | Meaning | Instruments |
|---|---|---|
| **Liberalisation** | Fewer state controls, licences, entry barriers | Industrial delicensing, fewer quotas |
| **Privatisation** | Larger private role in ownership / management | Disinvestment, dereservation |
| **Globalisation** | Integration with world trade, capital, technology | Lower import barriers, FDI / FPI opening, exchange-rate reform |

- After 1991 ↑ → {{India's share in world trade, FDI inflows, forex reserves}}.
- New Industrial Policy 1991 → [Industry](@industry-labour-infrastructure#industrial-policy).
- Planning did not end in 1991 → Plans ran to the 12th, increasingly **indicative**.

> [!TRAP] Agriculture's share in GDP did !!not!! "increase enormously" after 1991. · Disinvestment ≠ full privatisation. · LPG is a mnemonic, not the full reform list.
