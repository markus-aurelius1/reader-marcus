## Bank Balance Sheet {#bank-balance-sheet}

| Item | Bank's books |
|---|---|
| Customer deposits | {{**Liability**}} |
| Borrowings, capital, reserves | Liability side |
| Loans and advances | {{**Asset**}} |
| Investments (G-Secs) | Asset |
| Cash, balances with RBI | Asset |
| Money at call and short notice | {{Asset}} |

- {{**Interest Coverage Ratio** = earnings available for interest ÷ interest expense}} → higher = stronger debt-servicing ability.
- **Scheduled bank** → listed in the {{Second Schedule, RBI Act}}. Scheduled ≠ public sector.
- Teaser / sub-prime loans → low initial rate, later reset → default risk.
- **Core Banking Solution** → networked branches; customer of the bank, not of a branch.

## Bank vs NBFC {#bank-vs-nbfc}

| Feature | Bank | NBFC |
|---|---|---|
| Lending / investment | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Demand deposits** | ✓ | {{✗}} |
| Public deposits | ✓ | Only authorised deposit-taking NBFCs — {{non-demand deposits}} |
| Payment system / cheques on itself | ✓ | {{✗}} |
| DICGC cover | ✓ | {{✗}} |
| CRR / SLR as banks | ✓ | ✗ — separate prudential rules |
| LAF access | Eligible banks / PDs | {{✗}} |
| RBI registration | Licence | Required — with statutory exemptions |

- NBFC test → {{**50–50** — financial assets and financial income}} as principal business.
- RBI uses **scale-based regulation**.
- Entities regulated by SEBI / IRDAI / PFRDA / States → exempt from RBI NBFC registration. HFCs → RBI.
- **IREDA** → government company with NBFC status.

> [!TRAP] "Small NBFCs are regulated by State Governments" → wrong. · An NBFC is not a bank because it lends.

> [!VERIFY] UPSC 2024 item on NBFC / FPI / stock exchanges — the stored question text is malformed. Rely only on the three independently checked facts: ordinary NBFCs have no bank-style LAF access · FPIs can invest in G-Secs · stock exchanges run debt segments.

## Priority Sector & Deposit Insurance {#psl-and-dicgc}

### PSL
- RBI-directed credit → agriculture · MSME · export credit · education · housing · social infrastructure · renewable energy · weaker sections.
- **PSLC** → trades {{the achievement, not the loan or its risk}}.
- PSL loans → not automatically concessional.
- Shortfall → parked in funds such as RIDF.

### DICGC
- {{Wholly owned subsidiary of RBI}}.
- Covers commercial + cooperative bank deposits; premium paid by {{the bank}}.
- Limit → {{per depositor per bank}}.

> [!DYN] DICGC cover ceiling in rupees; PSL sub-targets.

## Regulation & PSB Governance {#regulation-and-governance}

- RBI powers → RBI Act + Banking Regulation Act → licensing, branch expansion, prudential norms, inspection, amalgamation.
- PSBs → Government + RBI share control; RBI's powers are narrower than over private banks.
- **Foreign-bank WOS** → minimum capital ₹5 billion; board → {{≥ 50% Indian nationals / NRIs / PIOs, with ≥ 1/3 Indian nationals resident in India}}.
- **Banks Board Bureau** → historical; recommended PSB top appointments. {{RBI Governor was not its chair}}.
- {{**FSIB** — from 1 July 2022}} → replaced BBB; recommends whole-time directors and chairpersons of public-sector financial institutions.

> [!TRAP] "50% of WOS directors must be Indian nationals" → too simple. · "Government capital infusion in PSBs rose steadily for a decade" → false in the 2018 question; SBI associate-bank merger had happened.

## Basel & Capital {#basel}

| Norm | Core |
|---|---|
| Basel I | Capital against {{credit risk}}; risk-weighted assets |
| Basel II | 3 pillars → {{minimum capital · supervisory review · market discipline}} |
| Basel III | Better capital quality + buffers + leverage ratio + {{LCR and NSFR}} |

- {{**CAR / CRAR** = capital ÷ risk-weighted assets}} → absorbs unexpected loss; protects depositors.
- Capital conservation buffer · countercyclical buffer (built in booms).
- **LCR** → liquid assets for 30-day stress. **NSFR** → stable long-term funding.

> [!TRAP] SLR is an Indian statutory requirement — not a Basel norm.

## NPAs & Resolution {#npa-and-resolution}

- **NPA** → interest / principal overdue beyond the RBI-prescribed period.
- Gross NPA → before provisions. Net NPA → after eligible provisions.
- **AQR** → RBI clean-up, {{2015}}.
- **SARFAESI** → secured creditor enforces security {{without a court decree}}; {{agricultural land excluded}}.
- **ARC** → buys stressed assets; issues security receipts.
- **IBC** → time-bound; initiated by {{financial creditor / operational creditor / corporate applicant}}.
- **Inter-Creditor Agreement** → lenders act together on a stressed borrower.
- S4A, SDR, 5:25 → historical restructuring schemes.
- Bad bank → NARCL + IDRCL.

> [!TRAP] `Gross NPA = Net NPA + provisions` is not a universal identity. · Shareholders do not initiate IBC.
