## Tax Concepts {#tax-concepts}

| Type | Cue |
|---|---|
| **Progressive** | {{Burden rises with income}} |
| **Proportional** | Constant rate |
| **Regressive** | {{Burden falls, proportionately, as income rises}} |
| **Direct** | Incidence and burden on the same person |
| **Indirect** | {{Burden shifted through price}} |

- **Incidence** → economic question; statutory payer ≠ final bearer.
- **Tax-to-GDP** → moves with growth, policy, compliance, structure. No single cause.
- {{**Buoyancy** = %Δ tax revenue ÷ %Δ GDP}} → includes policy changes.
- {{**Elasticity**}} → response of revenue to the base, {{with tax policy held constant}}.

> [!TRAP] Buoyancy ≠ elasticity.

## VAT, ITC & GST {#gst}

> [!FLOW] Tax on value added at each stage → **Input Tax Credit** on tax already paid → no tax-on-tax (cascading)

- GST → {{destination / consumption based}}.

| Supply | Tax |
|---|---|
| Within a State | {{CGST + SGST / UTGST}} |
| Inter-State / import | {{IGST}} → apportioned later |
| Exports, SEZ supplies | {{Zero-rated}} |

- GST advantages → single market, no cascading, wider base.

### Constitutional anchor
- {{**101st Amendment**}} → GST.
- {{**GST Council — `Art. 279A`**}} → decisions by ≥ {{3/4}} of weighted votes; Union {{1/3}}, States {{2/3}}.

Amendments needing State ratification (incl. `Art. 279A`) → [Polity: Three Routes of Change](@prelims/polity/amendments-basic-structure#three-routes-of-change)

> [!TRAP] IGST is !!not!! a fixed 50 : 50 Centre–State split. · GST did not subsume "all indirect taxes except customs" — customs continues; items outside GST and State levies remain.

> [!DYN] GST rates, exempt-product lists, composition thresholds. GST Compensation Cess → original scheme is historical; its structure changed in 2026.

## Cess vs Surcharge {#cess-vs-surcharge}

| | Surcharge | Cess |
|---|---|---|
| Basis | {{`Art. 271`}} — tax on tax, for Union purposes | Levy for a **specific purpose** |
| Divisible pool | Excluded | {{Excluded (`Art. 270`)}} |

> [!TRAP] `Art. 271` = surcharge only, not "cess + surcharge".

> [!DYN] Agriculture Infrastructure and Development Cess (Budget 2021) and other current cesses.

## Direct Tax Law {#direct-tax-law}

- {{`Income-tax Act, 2025`}} → governs Tax Year 2026–27 onward (from 1 Apr 2026).
- `Income-tax Act, 1961` → earlier years, pending proceedings, older questions.

### Agricultural income
- Statutory definition → not every rural activity qualifies.
- {{Poultry, wool rearing}} → not agricultural income.
- Rural agricultural land meeting the statutory conditions → not a "capital asset".
- Specified tribal incomes → statutory income-tax exemption.

> [!UPPCS] Direct Tax Code → reform vocabulary / history; never the governing law. · Local-body taxes → State law, State Finance Commission → [Polity: Local Government](@prelims/polity/local-government#panchayats-part-ix).

> [!DYN] Income-tax slabs and corporate tax rates.

## International Tax & Anti-Avoidance {#international-tax}

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **BEPS** | {{Profit shifted to low / no-tax jurisdictions with little real activity}} |
| **Transfer pricing** | Prices between associated enterprises → {{arm's-length principle}} |
| **GAAR** | Rule against impermissible avoidance arrangements |
| **Indirect transfer** | Sale of an offshore entity deriving value substantially from Indian assets → taxable in India |

- Tax **planning** → lawful. Tax **evasion** → unlawful concealment. **Avoidance** → within the wording, countered by GAAR / specific rules.
