## MSP & FRP {#msp}

> [!FLOW] CACP recommends → Government (CCEA) approves → procurement → stocking and distribution

- **CACP** (Commission for Agricultural Costs & Prices) → {{recommends}} only. Looks at cost, demand–supply, domestic and world prices, inter-crop parity, terms of trade.
- MSP → one price for the {{whole country}}, not State-wise.
- MSP crops → {{22}}:

| Group | Crops |
|---|---|
| **Kharif (14)** | Paddy, jowar, bajra, maize, ragi · tur, moong, urad · groundnut, soyabean, sunflower, sesamum, {{nigerseed}} · cotton |
| **Rabi (6)** | Wheat, barley · gram, masur · rapeseed–mustard, safflower |
| **Commercial (2)** | {{Jute, copra}} |

- **Sugarcane** → {{FRP}} (Fair and Remunerative Price), not MSP. Approved by {{CCEA}}; paid by sugar mills; linked to sugar recovery. Replaced the Statutory Minimum Price from 2009–10.
- **SAP** (State Advised Price) → announced by some States, above FRP.

> [!TRAP] CACP does !!not!! fix or approve prices. · MSP is !!not!! a ceiling — market price can go above it. · Tea, coffee, rubber, spices are !!not!! MSP crops. · Sugarcane is not one of the 22.

> [!DYN] MSP and FRP rupee values, bonuses and SAP levels change every season. UPPCS has asked the current paddy MSP — refresh before the exam.

## Procurement & FCI {#procurement}

- Purpose → price support to farmers + stocks for the {{NFSA / PDS}} and buffer.
- **Wheat, paddy** → Central Pool; bought by FCI + State agencies; must meet {{Fair Average Quality}} norms.
- **Coarse grains** → as per approved State / Central plans.
- **Pulses, oilseeds, copra** → separate price-support schemes, with triggers and limits.
- **Cotton, jute** → specialised agencies.
- ==FCI economic cost== = {{acquisition cost (MSP + bonus)}} + {{procurement incidentals}} + {{distribution cost}}.
- Rice / grain price in the market is moved by → MSP · government buying · stocking and release · consumer (PDS) subsidy.

> [!TRAP] MSP announced ≠ unlimited purchase of every crop in every State / UT. Procurement differs by crop, agency, quality and scheme.

> [!DYN] Procurement ceilings, "100% procurement" announcements, issue prices and free-grain schemes → current layer.

Stockholding at the WTO → [AoA boxes & Peace Clause](@prelims/economy/international-institutions#aoa-boxes)

## Farm Credit & KCC {#credit}

| Institution | Role |
|---|---|
| **Commercial banks** | Largest direct lenders to agriculture |
| **RRBs** | Direct rural credit |
| **NABARD** | Apex — {{refinance, development, supervision}}; no retail loans to households |
| **PACS** | Village-level short-term cooperative credit |
| **DCCB** | District cooperative bank → {{funds PACS}} |
| **State Cooperative Bank** | Top of the short-term cooperative structure |
| **Land Development Banks** | Long-term loans; a direct lender |

- **Kisan Credit Card** → from {{1998–99}}; model scheme by {{NABARD}}; single-window credit.
  - Short-term limit covers → cultivation · {{post-harvest}} expenses · produce-marketing loan · {{household consumption}} · working capital for farm assets and allied activities.
  - Tractor, combine, big assets → {{investment (term) credit}}, not the short-term limit.
  - Open to tenant farmers, oral lessees, sharecroppers, SHGs / JLGs.

> [!TRAP] DCCBs do !!not!! give more short-term farm credit than commercial banks + RRBs.

> [!UPPCS] {{RRB 1975 → NABARD 1982 → SHG–Bank Linkage 1992 → KCC 1998}}.

Cooperative tiers and bank types → [Differentiated & cooperative banks](@prelims/economy/banking-financial-inclusion#differentiated-and-cooperative-banks) · Farm lending targets → [Priority sector](@prelims/economy/banking-financial-inclusion#psl-and-dicgc)

## Investment vs Subsidy {#investment-subsidy}

| Public investment | Not public investment |
|---|---|
| {{Computerising PACS}} | Fixing MSP |
| Social-capital development | {{Free electricity}} |
| Government {{cold storage}} | Farm-loan waiver |

- Investment → builds lasting assets or capacity. Spending public money alone does not make it investment.
- **Direct support** → income transfer to the farmer.
- **Indirect / input subsidy** → cheaper fertiliser, power, irrigation.
- WTO Agreement on Agriculture → sorts support by how trade-distorting it is (boxes) — !!not!! "direct allowed, indirect banned".

> [!VERIFY] One UPPCS question on direct vs indirect farm subsidies carries an internally inconsistent key ("all subsidies in India are indirect"). Use it only as a sign that the topic is asked.

## Fertiliser Pricing & Soil Health Card {#fertiliser}

| Fertiliser | Price system |
|---|---|
| **Urea** | Government-fixed MRP; {{administered}} |
| **P & K** (phosphatic, potassic) | {{Nutrient Based Subsidy}} since 2010 — fixed subsidy per kg of nutrient; companies set MRP, government monitors |

- Natural gas → {{ammonia}} → urea.
- Sulphur for phosphatic fertiliser → by-product of {{oil refineries}}.
- **Soil Health Card** → soil-test report + nutrient advice → checks overuse of fertiliser.

> [!TRAP] "Retail prices of chemical fertilisers are market-driven" → !!false!!. · Soil Health Card is !!not!! for expanding irrigation or assessing bank loans.

> [!DYN] Subsidy rates per nutrient and MRPs are revised often.

## Seeds & Farm Organisation {#seeds-farm-organisation}

- **Seed Replacement Rate** → share of area sown with {{certified / quality seed}} instead of farm-saved seed.
  - Real constraint → demand–supply gap of quality seed in {{low-value, high-volume}} crops.
  - A National Seeds Policy exists; the private sector does supply vegetable and horticultural seed.
- **Seed Village** → farmers trained to produce quality seed → available {{locally, on time, at low cost}}. Not a village reserved for certified seed; not a rule to use only own seed.
- **Small Farmer Large Field** → marginal farmers form a group and {{synchronise selected operations}}. Not contract farming; not handing land to a company.
- **PMFBY** → farmer's premium is !!not!! a flat 2% for all crops and seasons; named {{post-harvest}} losses are covered.
- **Intensive millets promotion** → cluster demonstrations of production, post-harvest and value-addition technology; poor, small, marginal and tribal farmers.

> [!DYN] Premium rates, notified crops and scheme names change — keep the design, refresh the numbers.

## Food Security & NFSA {#food-security}

- Food security = {{access}}, not production alone. Surplus output can sit beside hunger.
- Four dimensions → {{availability, access, utilisation, stability}}. Accountability is !!not!! one.
- **Amartya Sen** → {{entitlement approach}}: famine can occur without a fall in food supply.
- **National Food Security Act** → {{2013}}.
  - Covers up to {{75% rural + 50% urban}} population.
  - Not limited to BPL households.
  - Ration card → eldest woman, {{18 or above}}, is head of household.
  - Nutrition support for pregnant women, mothers and children.

> [!TRAP] NFSA is !!not!! a 2010 Act.
