## Sort the Question First {#distinctions}

Five things a GM question can swap → **trait** · **gene / source** · **technique** · **regulator** · **biosafety concern**.

| Tool | What it does on the farm |
|---|---|
| **Transgenic (GM) crop** | Gene introduced or altered by biotechnology |
| **Genome sequencing** | Finds markers for disease / drought tolerance → faster breeding; shows host–pathogen links |
| **RNA interference (RNAi)** | {{Silences}} a chosen gene → virus-resistant crops |
| **CRISPR–Cas9** | Cas9 = "molecular scissors" → {{targeted gene editing}} |

- Ways to make a transgenic crop that have been accepted → {{cytoplasmic male sterility}}, {{gene silencing}}.

> [!TRAP] Budding and grafting are !!not!! transgenesis. · Gene editing ≠ inserting a foreign Bt gene.

The molecular side → [RNAi, CRISPR & gene drive](@prelims/science-tech/genetics-biotechnology#gene-editing) · [Genomics tools](@prelims/science-tech/genetics-biotechnology#genomics-tools)

## Bt Crops {#bt-crops}

- **Bt** = {{Bacillus thuringiensis}} — a soil **bacterium**; its Cry proteins kill insects.
- **Bt brinjal** → aim: {{pest (insect) resistance}} — fruit-and-shoot borer.
- **Bt cotton** →

| Event | Genes |
|---|---|
| **Bollgard I** | {{cry1Ac}} |
| **Bollgard II** | {{cry1Ac + cry2Ab}} (stacked) |

- Target → bollworms. Refuge planting slows resistance.

> [!TRAP] Bt gene source is a bacterium, !!not!! a fungus. · Bt brinjal is !!not!! a terminator-seed technology. · Health and biodiversity worries are biosafety questions, not proven harm.

## Golden Rice {#golden-rice}

- Rice engineered to make {{beta-carotene}} (provitamin A) in the grain → against {{Vitamin A}} deficiency.
- A nutrition trait — !!not!! pest resistance or herbicide tolerance.

Vitamins → [Vitamins](@prelims/science-tech/health-diseases-nutrition#vitamins)

## GM Mustard {#gm-mustard}

- **DMH-11** → hybrid of Indian mustard (*Brassica juncea*).

| Gene | Job |
|---|---|
| **barnase** | {{Male sterility}} in the female parent line |
| **barstar** | {{Restores fertility}} in the hybrid |
| **bar** | Herbicide-tolerance {{marker}} used to select the plants |

- Why needed → mustard is mostly self-pollinating; the system forces cross-pollination → hybrid seed.

> [!VERIFY] The exam question on GM mustard is recorded incomplete; its answer key is not used. The gene roles above are from official ICAR / GEAC material.

## Biosafety & Regulation {#regulation}

- Legal base → {{Environment (Protection) Act, 1986}} + Rules of 1989.
- **GEAC** → under {{MoEFCC}}; appraises field trials and environmental release of GM organisms.
- **NBA** (National Biodiversity Authority) → access and benefit-sharing; guards traditional genetic resources against {{biopiracy}}. Does !!not!! supervise GM crop research.
- Import of GM food → needs approval of the competent authority.
- Biosafety tests look at → food / feed safety, allergy, toxicity, gene flow, weediness, non-target organisms, resistance build-up.

GEAC's parent law → [Environment (Protection) Act](@prelims/environment/environmental-laws#epa) · NBA → [Biological Diversity Act](@prelims/environment/environmental-laws#biodiversity-act) · Biosafety treaty → [CBD family](@prelims/environment/international-conventions#cbd-family) · Lab view of GM crops → [GM crops](@prelims/science-tech/genetics-biotechnology#gm-crops)

> [!DYN] Which crop or event is approved, on trial or on hold changes — never memorise approval status as permanent.
