## Farming Systems {#farming-systems}

| System | Mark |
|---|---|
| **Mixed farming** | Crops {{+ livestock}} on the same farm |
| **Intensive subsistence** | Small fragmented holdings, much labour → high output per hectare, {{low output per person}} |
| **Extensive commercial grain** | Large mechanised farms, few people → low output per hectare, {{high output per person}}; export |
| **Plantation** | Large estate, capital and management heavy, processing attached, export — tea, coffee, rubber |
| **Shifting cultivation** | Cut, burn, crop briefly, move on, leave fallow — {{jhum}} in NE India |
| **Nomadic herding** | Mobile, subsistence |
| **Commercial ranching** | Fixed, scientific, market-oriented |

- **Small Farmer Large Field** → a farm-organisation model, not a farming system → [Farm organisation](@agricultural-economics#seeds-farm-organisation)

## Conservation Agriculture & Zero Tillage {#conservation-agriculture}

- Three principles → {{minimum soil disturbance}} · {{permanent soil cover}} · {{crop rotation / diversification}}.
- **Zero tillage** → seed placed without prior ploughing.
  - Wheat sown straight into the previous crop's residue → {{no residue burning}}.
  - Rice can be direct-seeded → no nursery, no transplanting.
  - Builds soil organic carbon → carbon sequestration.
  - Holds soil moisture.
- Water-saving practices accepted in the exam → {{zero / reduced tillage}} + {{keeping crop residue on the field}}.

> [!TRAP] "Avoiding plantation crops" is !!not!! a principle of conservation agriculture. · Gypsum before irrigation is !!not!! a water-conservation practice.

Carbon held in soil → [Carbon sinks](@prelims/environment/climate-change#carbon-sinks) · Bunds, terraces, mulching → [Conservation methods](@soil-conservation#conservation-methods)

## Micro-irrigation & Fertigation {#micro-irrigation}

- **Drip** → small, frequent doses near the root → less evaporation, fewer {{weeds}}, less nutrient loss; lower {{soil salinity}}.
- Micro-irrigation → !!not!! the only way to irrigate drylands; can ease pressure on groundwater.
- **Fertigation** → soluble fertiliser fed through irrigation water (mostly drip).
  - Can control the {{alkalinity}} of irrigation water.
  - Better nutrient timing and uptake.
  - Less {{leaching}}.
- **Tensiometer** → measures {{soil-water tension}} → tells when to irrigate.

> [!TRAP] Rock phosphate and every phosphatic fertiliser are !!not!! suitable for fertigation — low solubility. · "Drip reduces soil erosion" was not accepted in the drip question.

## System of Rice Intensification {#sri}

- Core → {{alternate wetting and drying}}, fewer and younger seedlings, wider spacing, aerated root zone; no continuous flooding.
- Outcomes → less {{seed}} · less {{methane}} · less {{electricity}} (pumping).
- Why → less standing water → less anaerobic decay.

## Sugarcane Technology {#sugarcane}

- **Sustainable Sugarcane Initiative (SSI)** → nursery-raised {{bud-chip}} settlings, wide spacing, drip, intercropping.
  - Low seed cost ✓ · drip works well ✓ · more intercropping ✓.
  - "No chemical fertiliser at all" → !!not!! a feature.
- **Bud-chip settlings** → large saving in {{seed material}}.
- Tissue-culture settlings → can be used.
- Single-bud setts planted directly → do !!not!! germinate better than multi-bud setts in bad weather.

## Fertilisers & Soil Inputs {#inputs}

- Excess nitrogen fertiliser → {{soil acidity}} + {{nitrate leaching}} into groundwater. Does !!not!! multiply nitrogen-fixing microbes.
- **Neem-coated urea** → slows release / nitrification → less nitrogen lost, better use efficiency. Still urea — not nitrogen-free.
- **Biochar** → carbon-rich solid from biomass {{pyrolysis}}.
  - Holds water · houses useful microbes incl. nitrogen fixers · usable in growing media, even {{vertical farming}}.
- **Legumes in rotation** → biological nitrogen, break pest and disease cycles.
- **Azolla** → water fern with the cyanobacterium {{Anabaena}} → biofertiliser for flooded rice.
- **Bio-decomposer** → microbes turn stubble into manure in the field.

Microbial inputs in full → [Biofertilisers](@prelims/environment/bioenergy-bio-inputs#biofertilisers) · Price and subsidy of fertiliser → [Fertiliser pricing](@agricultural-economics#fertiliser)

> [!DYN] Bio-decomposer roll-out details and named climate-smart programmes are current layer — keep the practice, not the scheme trivia.

## Organic Farming & Permaculture {#organic}

- **Organic farming** → biological inputs, ecological pest and nutrient management; no synthetic inputs. Not automatically high-yield.
- **NPOP** (National Programme for Organic Production) → certification for production and export; secretariat → {{APEDA}}.
- **Sikkim** → {{India's first fully organic State}}.
- **Permaculture** vs chemical farming →
  - discourages {{monoculture}};
  - mulching is central;
  - chemical farming can raise soil salinity;
  - permaculture is possible in semi-arid regions too.

> [!VERIFY] An older paper called Sikkim the first fully organic state "in the world". Keep the safer wording: India's first.

## Emissions from Farming {#emissions}

- Flooded paddy → {{methane}}; rice also gives {{nitrous oxide}}.
- Burning crop residue → CO, methane, ozone-forming gases, SO₂.
- Agricultural soils → nitrogen oxides. Cattle → {{ammonia}}. Poultry → reactive nitrogen compounds.

The gases compared → [Greenhouse science](@prelims/environment/climate-change#climate-science) · As air pollution → [Industry → emissions](@prelims/environment/pollution#industry-emissions)

## Livestock & Fisheries {#allied}

- **Kharai camel** → {{Gujarat}} (Kutch); swims in seawater, grazes {{mangroves}}. !!Can!! be domesticated.
- **Rashtriya Gokul Mission** → {{indigenous}} cattle and buffalo breeds; genetic improvement, artificial insemination; helps small, marginal and landless keepers.
- **Recirculating Aquaculture System (RAS)** → water treated and reused.
  - Biofilter → {{ammonia → nitrite → nitrate}} (nitrification).
  - Solids and uneaten feed → removed in a separate stage.
  - Biofilter does !!not!! add phosphorus.
- **FAO Blue Transformation / Four Betters** → better {{production, nutrition, environment, life}}.

Mangroves → [Mangroves & blue carbon](@prelims/environment/marine-ecology#mangroves-blue-carbon)
