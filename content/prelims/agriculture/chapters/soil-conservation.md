## Soil Health {#soil-health}

- Soil = mineral matter + {{organic matter}} + water + air + living organisms.
- **Organic matter / humus** →
  - binds particles → stable structure;
  - {{raises}} water-holding capacity;
  - supplies nutrients and feeds soil life.
- Soil takes part in nutrient cycles, including the {{sulphur}} cycle.
- Soil organisms → add organic matter, improve air and water movement.

> [!TRAP] "High organic matter drastically reduces water-holding capacity" → !!false!!. · "Soil plays no role in the sulphur cycle" → !!false!!.

> [!VERIFY] Earthworms loosen and aerate soil and speed up decomposition. One UPPCS key marks "earthworms improve soil fertility" as wrong — the key is suspect; do not learn it as a fact.

## Soil Erosion {#erosion}

- Erosion = soil detached and carried off by water or wind; fertile topsoil goes first.

> [!FLOW] Splash (raindrop) → sheet (thin even layer) → rill (small channels) → gully (deep channels) → ravines / badlands

- **Chambal ravines** → {{gully}} erosion.
- Causes → {{deforestation}}, overgrazing, bare or down-slope ploughing, concentrated runoff, wind over dry bare soil.

> [!TRAP] Terrace cultivation is a control, !!not!! a cause. · "Tropical climate" alone is not a cause.

## Conservation Methods {#conservation-methods}

| Method | How it works | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Contour ploughing** | Furrows run {{along the contour}} → block downslope flow | Slopes |
| **Contour bunding** | Earth / stone / plant barriers along the contour → {{slow runoff, more infiltration}} | Sloping, hilly land |
| **Terracing** | Slope cut into {{bench-like steps}} → shorter slope, slower water | Steep slopes |
| **Mulching / residue cover** | Shields soil from raindrops; cuts evaporation | Any field |
| **Shelterbelts / windbreaks** | Tree rows cut {{wind}} speed | Dry, windy tracts |
| **Strip / intercropping** | Alternate strips keep cover, break flow | Slopes, drylands |
| **Check dams / gully plugging** | Slow concentrated flow, trap silt | Gullies |
| **Afforestation** | Roots bind, canopy and litter shield | Catchments |
| **Controlled grazing** | Keeps plant cover | Pastures |

> [!TRAP] Contour bunding !!slows!! water — it does not ease its flow. · Bunding (barrier) ≠ terracing (reshaped slope). · Contour bunding is for slopes, !!not!! desert margins, floodplains or scrubland.

Tillage and residue practices → [Conservation agriculture](@farming-practices#conservation-agriculture)

## Salinity & Sodicity {#salinity}

> [!FLOW] Irrigation water carries salts → water evaporates, salts stay → poor drainage / high water table → capillary rise → salts build up at the surface

- Worst in {{arid and semi-arid}} areas with faulty irrigation.
- Effects → plants cannot take up water, poor germination, lower yield.

| | Cause | Effect on soil |
|---|---|---|
| **Salinity** | Excess {{soluble salts}} | Osmotic stress on plants |
| **Sodicity / alkalinity** | High exchangeable {{sodium}} | Clay disperses → poor structure, {{low permeability}} |

- Cure → drainage + leaching. Sodic soil → calcium amendment such as {{gypsum}}.

> [!TRAP] Gypsum is !!not!! a cure for every saline soil. · Exam papers use "salinisation" loosely for both; impermeable soil is strictly a sodium problem.

## Watershed Management {#watershed}

- **Watershed / catchment** → land area whose runoff drains to a {{common outlet}}.
- **Integrated watershed management** → conserve and use land, surface water, groundwater and vegetation together, with the community.
- Works → contour and vegetative barriers, check dams, percolation tanks, rainwater harvesting, afforestation, pasture.
- Results → less {{runoff and soil loss}} · more {{recharge}} · moisture kept · vegetation regrows.

> [!TRAP] Linking perennial rivers with seasonal ones is !!not!! watershed development.

> [!UPPCS] **Hariyali** → Central watershed-development programme for rural water conservation, run through local bodies.
