## Land-Use Terms {#land-use}

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Net Sown Area (NSA)** | Area sown with crops / orchards; land sown more than once counted {{once}} |
| **Gross Cropped Area (GCA)** | Total cropped area; each sowing counted {{every time}} |
| **Current fallow** | Left uncultivated for {{one agricultural year or less}} |
| **Fallow other than current** | Uncultivated {{more than 1, up to 5}} years |
| **Culturable wasteland** | Cultivable, but left uncultivated {{5 years or more}} |

- ==Cropping intensity== = {{(GCA ÷ NSA) × 100}}. Above 100 → part of the NSA is cropped more than once.
- Total cultivable land = {{NSA + fallows + culturable wasteland}}.

> [!TRAP] Culturable wasteland ≠ barren / non-culturable land. · NSA can never exceed GCA.

## Irrigated vs Rainfed Farming {#irrigated-rainfed}

| Type | Logic |
|---|---|
| **Protective irrigation** | Limited water spread over a {{larger area}} → protects crops from moisture stress |
| **Productive irrigation** | {{More water per unit area}} → maximum productivity |
| **Dryland farming** | Rainfall broadly {{below 75 cm}}; hardy crops — ragi, bajra, moong, gram, guar; moisture conservation and rainwater harvesting central |
| **Wetland farming** | Rain exceeds crop need in the rainy season; flood and erosion risk; rice, jute, sugarcane, aquaculture |

- Irrigation lets a crop grow outside its natural rainfall zone → rainfall limits are not absolute.

Rain that decides all this → [Monsoon](@prelims/geography/india-climate-soils-vegetation#monsoon) · Water under the fields → [Groundwater](@prelims/geography/india-drainage#groundwater)

## Regional Pattern {#regional-pattern}

- North and interior India → clear {{Kharif – Rabi – Zaid}} seasons.
- South India → the same tropical crop can be grown more than once a year where moisture allows.
- India's tropical-monsoon setting → crops possible round the year → wide crop diversity.
- Dated land-area ranks between countries → never carry without a year.

### India vs China

- Arable area → {{China more than India}}.
- Share of area irrigated → {{India higher}}.
- Productivity per hectare → India is !!not!! higher than China.
