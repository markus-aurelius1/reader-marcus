## Seasons {#seasons}

| Season | Window | Crops |
|---|---|---|
| **Kharif** | Sown with the SW monsoon; harvest Sep–Oct / Nov | Rice, cotton, jute, maize, jowar, bajra, {{tur / arhar}}, groundnut |
| **Rabi** | Sown Oct–Nov; harvest Mar–Apr | Wheat, {{gram}}, rapeseed–mustard, barley |
| **Zaid** | Short summer between rabi and kharif | Watermelon, muskmelon, cucumber, vegetables, fodder; needs irrigation |

> [!EXC] A crop is not tied to one season everywhere.
> - **Black gram** → kharif and rabi.
> - **Jowar** → kharif in the north; both seasons in parts of the south.
> - **Groundnut** → mostly kharif; rabi in the south.
> - **Rice** → several crops a year in the south and West Bengal.
> - Sugarcane → planted across regional calendars.

## Cereals & Millets {#cereals-millets}

| Crop | Climate / water | Soil / region | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rice** | Warm, humid; over {{100 cm}} rain or irrigation | Water-holding alluvial / clayey; humid east, deltas, irrigated north-west | Kharif in the north |
| **Wheat** | Cool growing season; warm, dry, bright ripening | Well-drained loam; Indo-Gangetic plain, central India | {{Rabi}}; mostly irrigated; rainfed in highlands / Malwa |
| **Maize** | Warm; moderate rain; dislikes waterlogging | Well-drained loam; widespread | Food + fodder; starch; oil → biodiesel; alcoholic drinks |
| **Bajra** | Hottest, driest major millet | Light sandy soils; west and north-west | = {{pearl millet}}; kharif |
| **Jowar** | Semi-arid; drought tolerant | Central and south India | = sorghum; food + fodder |
| **Ragi** | Milder and moister than bajra | Red / black / sandy loam; south and hills | = {{finger millet}} |

> [!TRAP] Ragi = finger millet. Bajra = pearl millet. Never the other way.

> [!UPPCS]
> - West Bengal rice → three crops: {{aus, aman, boro}}.
> - Rice's western limit without irrigation → roughly the {{100 cm}} rainfall line.

## Pulses & Oilseeds {#pulses-oilseeds}

| Crop | Season | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Gram** | {{Rabi}} | Dryland pulse |
| **Tur / arhar** (pigeon pea) | Mainly {{kharif}} | A legume → fixes nitrogen like the others |
| **Black gram** | Kharif + rabi | |
| **Groundnut** | Largely kharif, rainfed | Light, well-drained soils; oilseed **and** member of the {{pea / legume family}} |
| **Rapeseed–mustard** | Rabi | Cool season; frost-sensitive; north-west and central India |
| **Niger** | Kharif | Hardy, rainfed; central and eastern tribal belts; edible oil; an {{MSP}} crop |

- Pulses → low water need; mostly rainfed drylands of central India, the Deccan, the north-west.
- Legumes → groundnut, horse gram, soybean, gram, pea, green gram, black gram, arhar. Root nodules hold {{Rhizobium}} → nitrogen fixation.
- **Cowpea, green gram** → pulse + fodder + green manure. Not every pulse is a green-manure crop.

> [!TRAP] "All pulses except arhar fix nitrogen" → !!wrong!!. Arhar is a legume and fixes nitrogen.

Botany of the family → [Legumes & nitrogen](@prelims/environment/plants#legumes) · Niger and price support → [MSP](@agricultural-economics#msp)

## Fibre & Sugar Crops {#fibre-sugar}

| Crop | Climate / water | Soil / region | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cotton** | Subtropical; about {{210 frost-free days}}; roughly {{50–100 cm}} rain | Light, well-drained, moisture-holding; black (regur) belt — not only there | Mainly {{kharif}}; frost kills it |
| **Jute** | Hot, humid; heavy rain | Alluvial floodplain / delta; West Bengal core | "Golden fibre"; {{retting}} needs plenty of water |
| **Sugarcane** | Hot, humid, long duration; ==water-intensive== | Fertile loam / alluvium; also black soil | Sugar, gur, khandsari, {{molasses}} |

- 50–75 cm for cotton → a common textbook band, not a hard limit.

> [!UPPCS] Jute → sowing and harvest windows vary with variety and region. In assertion–reason items, judge the origin claim and the season claim separately.

Black soil itself → [Soils](@prelims/geography/india-climate-soils-vegetation#soils) · Cane-planting methods → [Sugarcane technology](@farming-practices#sugarcane) · Cane price → [FRP](@agricultural-economics#msp)

## Plantation Crops & Spices {#plantation-spices}

| Crop | Needs | Region |
|---|---|---|
| **Tea** | Humid, heavy rain, good drainage; frost and standing water harmful; {{acidic}}, humus-rich slopes | Assam, West Bengal, Tamil Nadu, Kerala core — also grown in Andhra Pradesh, Himachal Pradesh, Tripura |
| **Coffee** | Warm, humid; {{shade-loving}}; frost-sensitive; well-drained loam | Western Ghats → {{Karnataka, Kerala, Tamil Nadu}}; Baba Budan hills tradition |
| **Rubber** | Hot, humid; rain spread through the year; lateritic / loamy soil | {{Kerala}} core |
| **Turmeric** | Warm, humid; fertile, well-drained loam | Maharashtra, Telangana, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu; spice + dye + medicine |

> [!TRAP] Coffee is !!not!! confined to the Nilgiris — it is a Western Ghats crop.

> [!UPPCS] Kerala → {{rubber}}; also the classic home of {{cardamom and pepper}}.

## Origin, Uses & By-products {#uses}

- **New World origin** → {{tobacco, cocoa, rubber}}.
- **Biomass gasification feed** → coconut shell, groundnut shell, {{rice husk}}.
- **Guar (cluster bean)** → vegetable and fodder; ==guar gum== thickens the fluid used in {{hydraulic fracturing}} for shale gas.
- **Maize** → starch, oil / biodiesel, alcoholic beverages.

How the feed becomes gas → [Biomass gasification](@prelims/environment/bioenergy-bio-inputs#gasification) · Fracking → [Unconventional gas](@prelims/geography/india-minerals-energy-industry#hydrocarbons)
