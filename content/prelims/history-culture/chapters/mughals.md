## Chronology & Battles {#chronology-battles}

| Ruler | Sequence |
|---|---|
| **Babur** | {{Panipat I 1526 → Khanwa 1527 → Chanderi 1528 → Ghaghra 1529}} |
| **Humayun** | {{Chausa 1539 → Kannauj (Bilgram) 1540}} → exile → restored 1555 |
| **Sur** | Sher Shah 1540–45 → Islam Shah |
| **Akbar** | Panipat II 1556; Haldighati 1576; Gujarat, Bengal, Deccan |
| **Jahangir** | 1605–27 |
| **Shah Jahan** | 1628–58 |
| **Aurangzeb** | {{Dharmat → Samugarh → Khajwa → Deorai}}; Bijapur {{1686}}; Golconda {{1687}} |

- Babur → Timurid; memoir in {{Chagatai Turkic}} — *Baburnama*.
- Court and administrative language → {{Persian}}.
- **Bairam Khan** → regent of young Akbar; Khan-i-Khanan.

> [!TRAP] Babur did !!not!! bring the arch-and-dome or gunpowder to India for the first time. · Turki was not the court language throughout.

## Sur Administration {#sur-administration}

- Roads, sarais → Sadak-i-Azam (Grand Trunk).
- Silver {{rupiya}} standardised; copper `dam`.
- Revenue → measurement; {{`patta`}} (demand statement) + {{`qabuliyat`}} (acceptance).
- A bridge to Akbar's system — not its sole inventor.

## Mansabdari {#mansabdari}

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `Mansab` | Rank in imperial service |
| {{`Zat`}} | Personal rank, pay |
| {{`Sawar`}} | Cavalry obligation |
| `Dagh` · `Chehra` | Horse branding · descriptive roll |
| {{`Do-aspa`, `Sih-aspa`}} | Jahangir — more troopers without a higher zat |
| `Naqdi` | Paid in cash |

- Not hereditary; transfers frequent.

## Jagirdar vs Zamindar {#jagirdar-vs-zamindar}

| | Jagirdar | Zamindar |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | {{Imperial assignee of revenue}} | {{Local hereditary intermediary}} |
| Tenure | Transferable | Hereditary |
| Basis | Mansab, service | Custom, clan, locality |
| Land | No ownership | Own land — {{`milkiyat`}} |
| Role | Collects assigned revenue | Revenue, retainers, local power |

> [!TRAP] "Jagirdars had judicial and police powers, zamindars only revenue" and its reverse are both overstatements.

|---|---|

|---|---|
| {{Polaj}} | Cultivated every year |

- Jagir crisis → jama above hasil; too many mansabdars.

## Agrarian Society {#agrarian-society}

- {{`Khud-kashta`}} → resident cultivator. · {{`Pahi-kashta`}} → from another village.
- {{`Jins-i-kamil`}} → cash crops.
- Shah Nahr canal → Shah Jahan, Punjab.

## Akbar — Institutions {#akbar-institutions}

- {{**Ibadat Khana**}}, Fatehpur Sikri → religious debate, opened to all faiths.
- {{`Sulh-i-kul`}} → universal peace.
- Maktab Khana → translations from Sanskrit.
- Abul Fazl → *Akbarnama*, *Ain-i-Akbari*. · Badauni → critic.
- {{Nizamuddin Panipati}} → Persian *Yogavasistha*.
- Kavindracharya Saraswati → Sanskrit scholar at Shah Jahan's court.

## Court Culture {#court-culture}

- Painting → Jahangir's portraits and albums → [Miniatures](@painting-crafts-textiles#miniatures).
- Music → Tansen, Tappa → [Hindustani forms](@performing-arts#hindustani-forms).
- Monuments → [Mughal architecture](@architecture-sculpture#mughal-architecture).
- *Kitab-i-Nauras* → {{Ibrahim Adil Shah II}}, Bijapur — not a Mughal.

## Marathas {#marathas}

- **Shivaji** → coronation {{1674}}; {{Ashtapradhan}}.
- {{`Chauth`}} → one-fourth. · {{`Sardeshmukhi`}} → additional 10%.
- {{`Deshmukh`}} → hereditary local chief, brought under control.
- Peshwa dominant in the 18th c.
