## Mauryan Spine & Sources {#mauryan-spine}

- **Chandragupta Maurya** → Kautilya tradition; treaty with {{Seleucus I Nicator}}; {{Megasthenes}} at court.
- **Bindusara** → Greek "Amitrochates".
- **Ashoka** → Kalinga war in the {{8th regnal year}} → Rock Edict {{XIII}}.
- **Brihadratha** → last Maurya; killed by {{Pushyamitra Shunga}}.

| Source | Caution |
|---|---|
| *Arthashastra* | Layered text — not every passage is Chandragupta-era |
| *Indica* | Survives only in later quotations |
| Ashokan inscriptions | Outrank later legends |
| Puranas, Buddhist / Jaina traditions | Dynastic lists, legends |

> [!TRAP] Named marriage to a Seleucid princess, "99 brothers killed", Chandashoka → later legend, not inscriptional fact.

## Dhamma {#dhamma}

- Ethical and social policy for a plural empire → !!not!! state Buddhism.
- Values → respect for elders; gifts to Brahmanas and shramanas; humane treatment of servants; restraint in killing; tolerance.
- Ashoka personally Buddhist → doctrine → [Buddhism](@buddhism-jainism#buddhist-doctrine).

## Kanganahalli {#kanganahalli}

- Buddhist stupa site, {{Karnataka}} (Sannati).
- Relief labelled {{"Ranyo Asoko"}} → rare named portrait of Ashoka.
