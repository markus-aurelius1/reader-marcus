## Ghadar {#ghadar}

- Indians abroad; HQ {{San Francisco}} — Yugantar Ashram.
- {{Sohan Singh Bhakna}} first president; {{Har Dayal}} organiser.
- Rash Behari Bose → linked to the 1915 plan.

> [!TRAP] Barindra Ghosh (Bengal) and Jogesh Chatterjee (HRA) are not Ghadar men.

## HRA → HSRA {#hra-hsra}

- HRA → after NCM was called off; *Bandi Jiwan* — Sanyal.
- HSRA → "Socialist" added at Feroz Shah Kotla.
- Bismil → {{Shahjahanpur}}; hanged at Gorakhpur.

> [!VERIFY] "Hindustan Socialist Democratic Union" in one UPPCS item is not a standard name — learn HRA 1924 and HSRA 1928.

## Chittagong {#chittagong}

- Surya Sen; Kalpana Dutta, Pritilata Waddedar.

> [!TRAP] Pritilata died at Pahartali in 1932, not in the 1930 raid.
