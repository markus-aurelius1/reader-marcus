## Sangam Society {#sangam-society}

| Tinai | Landscape | Life | Deity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kurinji | Hills | Hunting | Murugan |
| Mullai | Pasture | Herding | Mayon |
| Marutam | Farmland | Wet cultivation | Indra |
| Neytal | Coast | Fishing, salt | Varunan |
| Palai | Dry zone | Raiding | Korravai |

- Groups → Arasar, Anthanar, Vanigar, {{Vellalar}} → not a mechanical four-varna order.
- `Uzhavar` → ploughmen · `Adimai` → dependent / slave.
- {{`Natukal`}} → hero stones.
- {{**Vattakirutal**}} → defeated king's ritual fast unto death.
- `Puram` → war, fame · `Akam` → love.
- Women poets → Avvaiyar.

## Sangam Economy {#sangam-economy}

- Rice, pepper, cotton textiles, pearls, salt, ivory, gems.
- Port customs → major royal revenue.
- Arikamedu → Mediterranean amphorae; Roman coins and `Yavana` references.
- Port list → [Ports](@historical-geography#ports).

> [!TRAP] Roman coins ≠ proof of direct state-to-state trade.

## Chola Administration {#chola-administration}

- Mandalam → Valanadu → Nadu → village.

| Body | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `Ur` | {{General village assembly}} |
| `Sabha` / `Mahasabha` | Assembly of {{brahmadeya}} villages |
| `Nagaram` | {{Merchant town body}} |
| `Nadu` | Territorial unit + assembly |

- **Uttaramerur** inscriptions → {{`kudavolai`}} lot system; qualifications for committees.

> [!TRAP] Structured by landholding and status → not a universal democracy.

Modern local bodies → [Polity: Panchayats](@prelims/polity/local-government#committees-and-history)
