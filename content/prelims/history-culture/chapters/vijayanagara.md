## Dynasties {#dynasties}

| Dynasty | Phase | Rulers |
|---|---|---|
| **Sangama** | 14th–15th c. | Harihara I, Bukka I, Devaraya I, Devaraya II |
| **Saluva** | Late 15th c. | Saluva Narasimha |
| **Tuluva** | 16th c. | Vira Narasimha; {{Krishnadevaraya 1509–29}}; Achyuta Raya |
| **Aravidu** | After 1565 | Tirumala |

- Founded by {{Harihara and Bukka}} on the Tungabhadra.
- {{**Talikota, 1565**}} → Rama Raya killed; capital sacked → the state continued under the Aravidus.

> [!TRAP] 1565 ≠ end of Vijayanagara.

## Rulers — PYQ Anchors {#rulers}

| Ruler | Retain |
|---|---|
| **Harihara I** | New city; sacral kingship |
| **Devaraya I** | {{Dam on the Tungabhadra}} + canal to the capital |
| **Devaraya II** | Cavalry, archers strengthened |
| **Krishnadevaraya** | {{Raichur, 1520}}; Portuguese allowed a fort at {{Bhatkal}}; *Amuktamalyada* (Telugu) |

> [!TRAP] Devaraya I's water came from the Tungabhadra, !!not!! the Kaveri. · Devaraya II was !!not!! a contemporary of Vasco da Gama (1498).

## Administration & Economy {#administration-economy}

- {{**Amara-nayaka**}} → military chief with revenue assignment ({{`amaram`}}) → kept troops, horses; sent tribute.
- {{**Ayagar**}} → village functionaries paid by land shares.
- Imported {{war horses}} → west coast, Portuguese.
- Coin → [Fanam](@society-economy-terms#coins-credit).
- Madras site → granted to the English by a Chandragiri representative → [English factories](@europeans-british-expansion#english-factories).

## Hampi {#hampi}

- River → {{Tungabhadra}} — not Malaprabha.
- Kamalapuram tank, Hiriya canal; fort walls enclosed farmland.
- Mahanavami Dibba, Lotus Mahal, Hazara Rama.
- Kalyana mandapa → [Vijayanagara temples](@architecture-sculpture#south-indian-temples).
