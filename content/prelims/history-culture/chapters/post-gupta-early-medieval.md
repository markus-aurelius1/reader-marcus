## Chronological Frame {#chronology-frame}

| Block | Powers |
|---|---|
| c. 550–750 | Later Guptas, Maukharis, Pushyabhutis, Maitrakas, Gauda; Chalukya–Pallava |
| c. 750–1000 | {{Pala – Pratihara – Rashtrakuta}} |
| c. 950–1200 | Chandela, Paramara, Solanki, Chauhan, Gahadavala, Western Chalukya, Chola |
| Late 11th c. onward | {{Hoysala, Kakatiya, Yadava}} |

> [!TRAP] Hoysala, Gahadavala, Kakatiya, Yadava are !!not!! early-8th-century kingdoms. UPSC shifts dynasties by 1–3 centuries.

> [!FLOW] Mahendravarman I (Pallava) → Gopala (Pala) → Mihira Bhoja (Pratihara) → Parantaka I (Chola)

## Harsha {#harsha}

- c. {{606–647}}; Thanesar → {{Kannauj}}.
- East → Shashanka of Gauda. · South → checked by {{Pulakeshin II}} near the Narmada.
- {{Banabhatta}} → *Harshacharita*. · {{Xuanzang}} → visited.
- Patron of Buddhism **and** Brahmanical institutions → not an exclusive convert.

## Chalukya – Pallava {#chalukya-pallava}

- **Pulakeshin II** → Badami Chalukya; {{Aihole prashasti by **Ravikirti**}}.
- Narasimhavarman I → defeated Pulakeshin II, took Vatapi → [Pallavas](@sangam-south-india#pallavas).

> [!TRAP] Aihole inscription → Ravikirti, !!not!! Banabhatta.

## Tripartite Struggle {#tripartite-struggle}

| Power | Base | Rulers | Anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pala** | Bengal–Bihar | Gopala, Dharmapala, Devapala | Gopala {{chosen to end disorder}}; {{Vikramashila}}, Somapura; Vajrayana |
| **Pratihara** | West → Kannauj | Nagabhata I, II; {{Mihira Bhoja}} | Checked Arab expansion |
| **Rashtrakuta** | Deccan; {{Manyakheta}} | Dantidurga, Dhruva, Govinda III, Amoghavarsha | *Kavirajamarga*; Kailasa, Ellora — Krishna I |

- Prize → {{Kannauj}}. Not one continuous war under the same kings.
- Odantapuri–Gopala → later tradition only.
