## Stupa {#stupa}

- `Anda` → dome. · {{`Harmika`}} → railing at the top. · `Yashti` + `Chhatra` → mast, umbrellas.
- `Vedika` → railing. · `Torana` → gateway.
- Functions → relic, votive, commemorative.
- Early art → {{aniconic}} — empty throne, Bodhi tree, footprints, wheel.
- Bharhut, Sanchi → narrative reliefs, donor inscriptions.
- **Amaravati** → {{pale limestone}}; crowded dynamic reliefs; {{ayaka platforms and pillars}} at the cardinal points.
- **Nagarjunakonda** → Andhra; Sri Lankan links.

> [!TRAP] Amaravati stone is limestone, !!not!! marble. · "Only a relic store" is too narrow.

Doctrine and sects → [Buddhism](@buddhism-jainism#buddhist-schools)
