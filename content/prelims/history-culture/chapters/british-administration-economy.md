## Economic Impact {#economic-impact}

| Mechanism | Effect |
|---|---|
| Machine goods + tariffs + lost patronage | {{Deindustrialisation}} |
| Revenue and export demand | {{Commercialisation}} — cotton, indigo, opium, jute, tea |
| Railways with guaranteed returns | Markets linked; risk on Indian revenues |
| Rigid revenue, debt | Famine vulnerability |

- Indigo → revolt 1859–60; later decline due to {{synthetic dyes}}.
- Famine as loss of access to food → [Economy: Amartya Sen](@prelims/economy/economic-thinkers#thinkers-and-books).

## Drain of Wealth {#drain-of-wealth}

- Critics → {{Dadabhai Naoroji, R. C. Dutt, G. Subramania Iyer}}.
- Drain → surplus sent abroad {{without equivalent return}}.
- {{**Home Charges**}} → India Office costs, pensions, salaries, interest on debt, stores bought in Britain.
- Export surplus = the channel of remittance, not capital formation → [Economy: BoP accounts](@prelims/economy/external-sector#bop-accounts).

## Civil Services {#civil-services}

- Cornwallis → professional covenanted service.
- 1833 → no bar by religion or birth — principle only.
- {{**1853**}} → open competition; Macaulay Committee 1854.
- {{Satyendranath Tagore}} → first Indian in the ICS, {{1863}}.
- {{Aitchison}} 1886 → Imperial, Provincial, Subordinate.
- {{Lee Commission}} 1923–24 → PSC set up {{1926}} → [Polity: PSCs](@prelims/polity/constitutional-bodies#public-service-commissions).
