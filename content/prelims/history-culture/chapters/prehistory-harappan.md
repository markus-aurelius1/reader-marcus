## Periodisation {#periodisation}

| Phase | Diagnostic | Sites to place |
|---|---|---|
| **Palaeolithic** | Hunter-gatherers; chipped stone — handaxe / cleaver → flakes → blades | Soan valley, Narmada valley, Hunsgi, Attirampakkam, Bhimbetka |
| **Mesolithic** | {{Microliths}}; broader subsistence; early domestication at some sites; rock art | {{Bagor}}, Adamgarh, Langhnaj, Sarai Nahar Rai, Mahadaha, Bhimbetka |
| **Neolithic** | Food production; domestication; ground / polished stone; villages | Mehrgarh, Burzahom / Gufkral, Koldihwa–Mahagara, Chirand, Daojali Hading, southern ash-mound sites |
| **Chalcolithic** | Copper + stone; village farming cultures, non-urban | Ahar / Banas–Gilund, Kayatha, Malwa / Navdatoli, {{Jorwe–Inamgaon}}, Daimabad |
| **Mature Harappan** | Cities, standard weights / bricks / script, craft and trade networks | {{c. 2600–1900 BCE}} — regional Late Harappan phases continue after |

- Inamgaon → {{Chalcolithic, Maharashtra}} — !!not!! Harappan.
- Rock paintings of Bhimbetka → [Murals](@painting-crafts-textiles#murals).

> [!UPPCS]
> - Bhimbetka → {{V. S. Wakankar}}.
> - Bagor → {{Mesolithic}} — early animal domestication.

## Town Planning & Technology {#town-planning-technology}

- Planned streets, drains, standard bricks → ratio {{1 : 2 : 4}} common, not universal.
- Upper / lower town at major sites, but layouts vary by region.
- Standard {{cubical stone weights}} + scales → regulated exchange.
- Copper / bronze; gold, silver, lead known → {{iron absent}} in the mature phase.
- Spindle whorls → spinning, weaving; early {{cotton}}.
- Script → short texts, usually right-to-left, {{undeciphered}}. Language unknown — Proto-Dravidian is a hypothesis only.
- Dancing Girl → bronze, lost-wax → [Sculpture](@architecture-sculpture#sculpture).

## Economy & Trade {#economy-trade}

- Crops → wheat, barley, pulses, sesame; millets in Gujarat; cotton; rice at some sites / late contexts.
- Crafts → beads, shell, metal, pottery, faience.
- Exchange → Afghanistan / Central Asia, Iran, Oman ({{Magan}}), Bahrain ({{Dilmun}}), Mesopotamia.
- Mesopotamian texts → {{**Meluhha**}} = Harappan region (widely accepted).

> [!TRAP] Seals + weights + standardisation show organised trade — they do !!not!! prove a centralised "merchant government".

## Religion & Society — Evidence Limits {#religion-society}

| Label in older books | Exam-safe reading |
|---|---|
| "Mother Goddess", "Proto-Shiva / Pashupati" | {{Interpretations}} of figurines and a seal — not established facts |
| "Great Granary" | Conventional label; function questioned |
| "Priest-King", merchant oligarchy, single empire | Political form {{unresolved}}; authority and coordination are evident |
| Temples | No structure securely identified as a temple |
| Caste, matriarchy | Not archaeologically established; house sizes show social differentiation |
| Horse "unknown" | Horse {{not characteristic; evidence disputed / limited}} |

- Burials vary by region; grave goods occur.

## Harappan vs Early Rigvedic {#harappan-vs-rigvedic}

| | Harappan | Early Rigvedic |
|---|---|---|
| Settlement | Urban centres | Pastoral / tribal; no such cities |
| Writing | Script, undeciphered | Oral tradition |
| Metal | Copper / bronze; no iron | `ayas`; iron is Later Vedic |
| Wealth | Weights, long-distance trade | Cattle central |
| Horse | Not characteristic | Horse and chariot prominent |
