## Vedic Corpus — Order {#vedic-corpus}

- **Shruti** → transmitted orally through recensions ({{shakhas}}).
- Earliest layer → Rigveda {{family books 2–7}}.
- Later layer → Rigveda books 1, 8, 9, 10 → other Samhitas → Brahmanas → Aranyakas → early Upanishads.

> [!FLOW] Mantra portions → Brahmanas → Aranyakas → early Upanishads (composition overlapped)

> [!TRAP] Brahmana (text genre) ≠ Brahmana (varna).

## Four Vedas {#four-vedas}

| Veda | Character | Priest / anchor |
|---|---|---|
| **Rigveda** | Hymns; 10 mandalas; oldest | {{Hotri}}; Mandala 9 → Soma |
| **Samaveda** | Chants, largely from the Rigveda | {{Udgatri}} |
| **Yajurveda** | Sacrificial formulae + procedure | {{Adhvaryu}}; Shukla (White) and Krishna (Black) |
| **Atharvaveda** | Domestic / royal rites, healing, charms, speculation | {{Brahman}} priest |

- {{**Vajasaneyi Samhita**}} = Shukla Yajurveda → recensions Madhyandina, Kanva.
- Krishna Yajurveda → Taittiriya, Maitrayani, Kathaka.
- Vedatrayi → Rig–Sama–Yajur; Atharva integrated later.
- `kshetra-patni` → tested against four texts; keyed to {{Atharvaveda}}.

## Brahmana → Aranyaka → Upanishad {#brahmana-aranyaka-upanishad}

| Veda | Brahmana |
|---|---|
| Rig | Aitareya, Kaushitaki |
| Sama | Panchavimsha / Tandya |
| Shukla Yajur | {{Shatapatha}} |
| Atharva | {{Gopatha}} |

- **Aranyakas** → "forest texts"; symbolism of ritual.
- **Upanishads** → dialogues, arguments, parables → atman, brahman, karma, rebirth, liberation.

| Upanishad | Veda | Anchor |

| **Brihadaranyaka** | Shukla Yajur | {{Yajnavalkya}} debates; Gargi, Maitreyi |
| **Chandogya** | Sama | Uddalaka Aruni–Shvetaketu; {{tat tvam asi}} |
| **Mundaka** | Atharva | {{**Satyameva Jayate**}}; higher / lower knowledge |
| Isha | Shukla Yajur | |
| Kena | Sama | |

> [!TRAP] Upanishads **do** use parables and dialogues, and are much earlier than the Puranas. · Brihadaranyaka and Chandogya are "among the oldest" — no single "oldest".

## Early Vedic Religion {#vedic-religion}

- Nature deities + {{yajna}} → no temple / image cult.
- Indra (war, rain) · Agni (fire, intermediary) · Soma · {{Varuna — guardian of rta}} · Surya / Savitri · Ushas · Ashvins · Maruts.
- Henotheism → the deity addressed is praised as supreme in that hymn.
- Later Vedic → elaborate ritual; Prajapati, Vishnu, Rudra rise.

> [!TRAP] Do not project later temple worship or Bhakti onto Rigvedic religion.

## Rta & Dharma {#rta-dharma}

- **Rta** → {{cosmic, natural and moral order}} — wider than ritual.
- **Dharma** → duties, obligations, right conduct toward self and others.

## Vedic Society — Text Evidence {#vedic-society}

- Rigvedic → {{pastoral emphasis}}, cattle = wealth; agriculture known.
- Rigveda carries vocabulary for wells / water-lifting and draught animals → not "purely pastoral".
- Later Vedic → settled agriculture in the western / middle Ganga plain; territorial polities.
- `Sabha`, `Samiti` → assemblies; distinction varies by passage.
- Ratnin {{**Bhagadugha**}} → collector of the king's share.
- **Nadi-sukta** (Rigveda) → river evidence → [Ancient rivers](@historical-geography#ancient-rivers).

## Six Astika Darshanas {#six-darshanas}

| School | Founder / text | Core | Liberation hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Samkhya** | Kapila (traditional); {{Ishvarakrishna's *Samkhyakarika*}} = earliest extant classic | Dualism — {{purusha + prakriti}}; 25 tattvas | Discriminative knowledge; accepts karma and rebirth |
| **Yoga** | {{Patanjali — *Yoga Sutra*}} | Samkhya categories + Ishvara; stilling of the mind | Eight limbs → yama … samadhi |
| **Nyaya** | {{Akshapada Gautama — *Nyaya Sutra*}} | Logic, realism | 4 pramanas → perception, inference, comparison, testimony |
| **Vaisheshika** | {{Kanada — *Vaisheshika Sutra*}} | Categories (padarthas); atomism | Knowledge of reality; theism is a later layer |
| **Purva Mimamsa** | {{Jaimini — *Mimamsa Sutra*}} | Vedic exegesis; ritual duty | Performance of duty; `apurva` |
| **Vedanta** | {{Badarayana — *Brahma Sutra*}} | Upanishadic Brahman / atman | Sub-schools differ → next section |

> [!TRAP] Not among the six → Lokayata / Charvaka, Buddhism, Jainism, Ajivika, Kapalika. · Patanjali's eight limbs ≠ Kundalini–chakra system. · Badarayana = author / compiler of the Brahma Sutra, not a "founder".

## Vedanta Schools {#vedanta-schools}

| Thinker | School | Distinction |
|---|---|---|
| **Shankara** | {{Advaita}} | Non-dual Brahman; knowledge central |
| **Ramanuja** | {{Vishishtadvaita}} | Qualified non-dualism; souls and world real; {{bhakti / prapatti}} leads to liberation |
| **Madhva** | {{Dvaita}} | God, souls, matter distinct; Vishnu supreme |
| **Nimbarka** | {{Dvaitadvaita}} (Bhedabheda) | Difference and non-difference; Radha–Krishna |
| **Vallabha** | {{Shuddhadvaita}} | World a real manifestation; grace — {{Pushtimarga}} |

Saints and movements → [Bhakti](@bhakti-sufi#bhakti-core)

## Heterodox Schools {#heterodox-schools}

- **Charvaka / Lokayata** → materialist; perception privileged; rejects Vedic authority.
- **Ajivika** → {{Makkhali Gosala}}; {{niyati — determinism}}; cave gifts → [Barabar](@architecture-sculpture#rock-cut).
- Buddhism, Jainism → [Doctrine](@buddhism-jainism#shramana-setting).

> [!UPPCS]
> - Vajasaneyi = {{Shukla Yajurveda}}.
> - Nyaya → {{Gautama}}.
> - Rigvedic pastoral → Later Vedic agrarian.
