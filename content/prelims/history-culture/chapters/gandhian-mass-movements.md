## Rowlatt & Jallianwala {#rowlatt-jallianwala}

- Rowlatt Act, 1919 → detention without trial safeguards; Sidney Rowlatt's committee.
- Gandhi → {{Satyagraha Sabha}}; Home Rule networks.
- Amritsar, 13 April 1919 → after the arrest of {{Kitchlew and Satyapal}}; Dyer.
- Tagore → renounced knighthood. · {{C. Sankaran Nair}} → resigned from the Viceroy's Council.

- Calcutta special session, Sep 1920 → approved. · Nagpur, Dec 1920 → ratified; Congress reorganised.
