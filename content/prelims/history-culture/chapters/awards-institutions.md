## Civilian Awards {#civilian-awards}

| Award | Retain |
|---|---|
| **Bharat Ratna** | {{1954}}; any field; PM recommends to the President; {{max 3 a year}}; no money |
| **Padma Vibhushan** | Exceptional and distinguished service |
| **Padma Bhushan** | Distinguished service of a high order |
| **Padma Shri** | Distinguished service |

- Padma awards not given → {{1978–79 and 1993–97}}.

> [!TRAP] "Suspended only once" → false. · No rule that one person must hold all four.

## Article 18 {#article-18}

- National awards ≠ titles → cannot be used as prefix or suffix.
- Constitutional rule → [Polity: Right to Equality](@prelims/polity/fundamental-rights#right-to-equality-articles-14-18).

## Gandhi Peace Prize {#gandhi-peace-prize}

- Instituted {{1995}}; Government of India.
- Jury → {{PM (chair) + CJI + Leader of Opposition in Lok Sabha + two eminent persons}}.

> [!TRAP] The President is !!not!! on the jury.

## Science & Literature Awards {#science-literature-awards}

- **Bhatnagar Prize** → {{CSIR}}; seven disciplines; age limit {{45}}.
- **Jnanpith** → {{Bharatiya Jnanpith}}; first {{1965 — G. Sankara Kurup}}.

> [!TRAP] Jnanpith ≠ Sahitya Akademi Award.

> [!DYN] Recipients of the year — Padma, Jnanpith, Sahitya Akademi, Booker, film and sports awards. UPPCS asks the last 12–18 months.
