## INC & Moderates {#inc-moderates}

- Hume's Indian National Union → INC.
- First session → {{Dec 1885, Bombay}}; {{72}} delegates; {{W. C. Bonnerjee}}.
- Method → petitions, resolutions, councils, opinion in Britain.
- Lasting work → economic critique → [Drain of Wealth](@british-administration-economy#drain-of-wealth).
- Social reform kept on a separate platform → [Indian Social Conference](@socio-religious-reform#western-india).
- Dufferin → Congress a {{"microscopic minority"}}.

## Moderates vs Extremists {#moderates-vs-extremists}

| | Moderates | Extremists |
|---|---|---|
| Method | Petition, councils | Swadeshi, boycott, passive resistance |
| Base | Educated middle class | Wider public |
| Names | Naoroji, Gokhale, Mehta, Banerjea | {{Tilak, Bipin Chandra Pal, Lajpat Rai, Aurobindo}} |

> [!TRAP] The divide was about scope and pace of mass action, not violence vs non-violence.

## Partition of Bengal & Swadeshi {#swadeshi}

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 19 Jul 1905 | Partition announced — Curzon |
| {{7 Aug 1905}} | Boycott resolution, Calcutta Town Hall |
| {{16 Oct 1905}} | Partition in force |
| {{1911}} | Annulled; capital {{Calcutta → Delhi}} |

| Axis | Content |
|---|---|
| Economic | Boycott; swadeshi enterprise |
| Educational | {{National Council of Education, 1906}} |
| Political | Samitis, passive resistance |
| Cultural | Songs, art, science |

- P. C. Ray → Bengal Chemical.
- {{S. G. Deuskar}} → *Desher Katha*.
- {{V. O. Chidambaram Pillai}} → {{Swadeshi Steam Navigation Company}}, Tuticorin, 1906.
- Decline by 1908 → repression, split, weak organisation, little peasant reach.

> [!TRAP] Tagore gave up his knighthood in 1919, not 1905.

> [!DYN] 7 August is observed as National Handloom Day — the static fact is the 1905 launch.
