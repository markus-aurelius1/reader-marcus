## Hindustani vs Carnatic {#hindustani-vs-carnatic}

| | Hindustani | Carnatic |
|---|---|---|
| Classification | {{Thaat — Bhatkhande}} | {{Melakarta — Venkatamakhin}} |
| Forms | Dhrupad, Khayal, Thumri, Tappa, Tarana | Kriti, Varnam, Ragam–Tanam–Pallavi, Tillana |
| Drum | Tabla; pakhawaj | Mridangam |

- {{Dheera Shankarabharanam}} (Carnatic) ≈ {{Bilawal}} (Hindustani).

> [!TRAP] Bhatkhande codified **Hindustani** music.

## Hindustani Forms {#hindustani-forms}

| Form | Retain |
|---|---|
| **Dhrupad** | Oldest major form; devotional; long alap; {{pakhawaj}}; Man Singh Tomar |
| **Khayal** | {{Sadarang, Adarang}} — Muhammad Shah |
| **Thumri** | Lucknow, Banaras |
| **Tappa** | Punjabi camel-drivers' songs; quick ornate phrases |
| **Dhamar** | Holi |
| **Tarana** | Syllables; fast |

- **Tansen** → earlier at the court of {{Raja Ramchandra of Rewa}}; Akbar gave the title {{"Mian"}}.
- Sufi sama → [Sufi vocabulary](@bhakti-sufi#sufi-vocabulary).

> [!TRAP] Akbar did !!not!! give the name "Tansen". · Dhrupad did not originate in Mughal-era Rajput courts. · Khayal and Tarana "invented by Amir Khusrau" → tradition only.

> [!VERIFY] Tappa → keyed to Muhammad Shah's court; refinement also credited to {{Shori Mian}} of Awadh — no single founder.

## Carnatic {#carnatic}

- Trinity → {{Tyagaraja, Muthuswami Dikshitar, Syama Sastri}}.
- **Tyagaraja** → mostly {{Rama}}; Pancharatna kritis.
- **Annamacharya** (15th c.) → {{Venkateswara}} — not a contemporary of Tyagaraja.
- **Purandaradasa** → father of Carnatic pedagogy.
