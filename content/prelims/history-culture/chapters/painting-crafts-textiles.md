## Murals & Rock Painting {#murals}

| Site | State | Retain |
|---|---|---|
| **Bhimbetka** | MP | Prehistoric rock shelters; {{V. S. Wakankar, 1957}} |
| **Ajanta** | Maharashtra | Buddhist; Jatakas; {{Padmapani, Vajrapani — Cave 1}}; Vakataka patronage |
| **Bagh** | MP | Buddhist; {{*Hallisalasya* — folk-dance scene}} |
| Badami | Karnataka | Chalukya fragments |
| **Sittanavasal** | Tamil Nadu | Jain; lotus pond |
| **Lepakshi** | Andhra Pradesh | Vijayanagara; Virabhadra temple |

- Ajanta technique → on dry plaster — tempera, not true wet fresco.

> [!TRAP] Lepakshi murals are religious narratives — not "secular", not "without primary colours".

## Miniatures — Early & Mughal {#miniatures}

- **Pala** → Buddhist palm-leaf manuscripts.
- **Western Indian / Jain** → Gujarat–Rajasthan; palm leaf → paper.

| Reign | Retain |
|---|---|
| Humayun | {{Mir Sayyid Ali, Abd al-Samad}} |
| Akbar | Illustrated manuscripts; {{*Hamzanama*}}; Daswanth, Basawan |
| Jahangir | {{Portraits, albums (`muraqqa`), flora and fauna}}; {{Ustad Mansur}}, Abu'l Hasan, Bishandas |
| Shah Jahan | Formal, opulent |
| Aurangzeb | Less imperial patronage → artists move to regional courts |

> [!TRAP] European influence is visible from Akbar and Jahangir, not only Shah Jahan. · Jahangir = connoisseur and patron, not "a great painter". · "All arts declined under Aurangzeb" is false.
