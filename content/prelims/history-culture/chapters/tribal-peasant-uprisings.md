## Tribal Resistance — Causes {#tribal-causes}

- Common factor → {{disruption of the old agrarian and social order}}.
- Loss of customary land and forest rights.
- {{`Dikus`}} → moneylenders, traders, zamindars, officials.
- Debt, land alienation, forced labour, forest laws.
- Missionaries mattered in places — not the universal cause.

Protection today → [Polity: Fifth Schedule](@prelims/polity/scheduled-tribal-areas#fifth-schedule) · [Forest Rights Act](@prelims/polity/scheduled-tribal-areas#forest-rights-act-2006)

## Revolt of 1857 {#revolt-1857}

- Spark → Enfield cartridge. · Causes → annexations, Awadh, sepoy grievances, dispossessed elites.
- {{29 Mar}} Barrackpore — Mangal Pandey → {{10 May}} Meerut → Delhi.

| Centre | Leader |
|---|---|
| Delhi | Bahadur Shah II; {{Bakht Khan}} |
| Kanpur | {{Nana Saheb}}, Tatya Tope |
| Lucknow | {{Begum Hazrat Mahal}} |
| Bareilly | {{Khan Bahadur Khan}} |
| Jhansi | Rani Lakshmibai |
| Jagdishpur | {{Kunwar Singh}} |
| Faizabad | {{Maulvi Ahmadullah Shah}} |

- Failure → limited spread, no common programme, weak coordination.
- Notable Hindu–Muslim cooperation in several centres.
- 1858 → Crown rule → [Company to Crown](@constitutional-development#company-to-crown).
