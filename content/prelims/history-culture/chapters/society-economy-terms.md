## Mitakshara vs Dayabhaga {#mitakshara-dayabhaga}

| | Mitakshara | Dayabhaga |
|---|---|---|
| Area | Most of India | {{Bengal, Assam}} |
| Right in ancestral property | {{By birth}} | {{On the father's death}} |
| Father's control | Son has interest in his lifetime | Father keeps fuller control |

> [!TRAP] Options reverse the timing of inheritance.

- *Arthashastra* → slavery and legal status appear as supporting statements.

## Science & Place Value {#science-place-value}

- Decimal place-value system → developed over time; inscriptions date it.
- Early epigraphic use → {{Mankani plates, Gujarat, c. 595–96 CE}}.
- General inscriptional use by the 9th c.; Sanskrit inscriptions in Southeast Asia by the 7th c.
- Science questions → chronology of capability (surgery, sine, geometry) → keep dated claims only.

> - `Bhagadugha` → {{revenue collector}} (Later Vedic).
