## Communal Award → Poona Pact {#communal-award-poona}

| | Communal Award | Poona Pact |
|---|---|---|
| Date | {{16 Aug 1932}} | {{24 Sep 1932}} |
| By | Ramsay MacDonald | Ambedkar; Malaviya |
| Depressed Classes | {{Separate electorate}} | {{Joint electorate + reserved seats}} |
| Provincial seats | 71 | {{148}} |
| Centre | | {{18%}} of general seats |

- Gandhi's fast → Yeravada, 20 Sep 1932 → against the separate electorate.
- Gandhi did not sign the pact.
- The Award covered several communities; the Depressed Classes clause is the tested one.

Reserved seats today → [Polity: Reservation in legislatures](@prelims/polity/elections#reservation-in-legislatures)

## Negotiation Ladder, 1944–45 {#negotiation-ladder}

| Proposal | Core |
|---|---|
| **C. R. Formula**, 1944 | {{C. Rajagopalachari}}; League backs independence; {{plebiscite of the whole population}} in Muslim-majority areas after the war; common defence, commerce, communications |
| **Desai–Liaquat**, 1945 | Parity in an interim government; never adopted |
| **Wavell Plan — Simla**, 1945 | Parity of caste Hindus and Muslims; League claimed all Muslim seats |

- Jinnah → wanted the two-nation claim accepted; only Muslims to vote.

> [!FLOW] Cripps 1942 → C. R. Formula 1944 → Wavell Plan 1945 → Simla 1945 → Cabinet Mission 1946

## Red Fort Trials {#red-fort-trials}

- {{Red Fort, Delhi}}, Nov 1945.
- {{Shah Nawaz Khan, Prem Kumar Sehgal, Gurbaksh Singh Dhillon}}.
- Defence → {{Bhulabhai Desai}}, Sapru, Katju, Nehru, Asaf Ali.
- Sentence → transportation for life; remitted by {{Auchinleck}}.
- Then → Rashid Ali agitation, Feb 1946 → RIN revolt.

> [!TRAP] Lakshmi Sahgal is not one of the Red Fort three. · Rash Behari Bose ≠ Rashid Ali.
