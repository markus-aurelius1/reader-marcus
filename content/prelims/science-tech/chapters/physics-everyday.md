## Heat, Pressure & Everyday Mechanics {#heat-pressure}

- **Pressure cooker** → higher pressure → {{higher boiling point}} → faster cooking. Pressure is set by the {{weight on the lid and the vent hole}}, not the flame.
- **Evaporation cools** → fast molecules leave. Slow in humid air → sweat does not dry → discomfort.
- **Water** → densest at {{4°C}}; ice is lighter → lakes freeze from the {{top}}, water below stays liquid.
- **Thermos flask** → vacuum stops conduction and convection; silvered walls stop radiation.
- **Ball bearings** → replace sliding friction with {{rolling friction}} (smaller).
- **Capillarity** → liquid rises in narrow tubes (surface tension) → lamp wick, blotting paper, water rising in soil.
- **Charged soap bubble** → expands (like charges repel).

> [!TRAP] Drinking through a straw is !!not!! capillarity — it is air-pressure difference. · Water rising in tall trees needs transpiration pull, not capillarity alone.

## Sound & Light {#sound-light}

- **Sound** → needs a medium. Speed → {{solid > liquid > gas}}.
- Loudness unit → {{decibel}} (logarithmic). Rustling leaves ≈ 20 dB.
- **Light** → travels in vacuum. Refraction → bending as speed changes.
- **Total internal reflection** → denser to rarer medium, beyond the critical angle → {{optical fibre}}, mirage, sparkle of diamond.
- **Rainbow** → {{refraction + dispersion + internal reflection}} in raindrops.
- **Twinkling of stars** → atmospheric refraction.
- **Blue sky, red sunset** → scattering (short wavelengths scatter more).

## Electromagnetic Spectrum {#em-spectrum}

> [!FLOW] Increasing frequency and energy → {{Radio → Microwave → Infrared → Visible → Ultraviolet → X-ray → Gamma}}. Wavelength decreases the same way.

- All travel at the speed of light in vacuum.
- Visible light ≈ 380–700 nm.
- **Semiconductors** → {{silicon, germanium}}; conductivity between conductor and insulator.
- **Crookes glass** → absorbs ultraviolet; used in spectacles.

## Forces & Particles {#forces-particles}

| Force | Strength | Acts on | Carrier |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Strong nuclear** | {{Strongest}} | Quarks; holds the nucleus | Gluon |
| **Electromagnetic** | | Charged particles | Photon |
| **Weak nuclear** | | Radioactive beta decay | W, Z bosons |
| **Gravity** | {{Weakest}}; infinite range | Mass | Graviton (not found) |

- **Neutrino** → {{no charge}}, almost no mass, passes through matter.
- **Higgs boson** → linked to the field that gives particles {{mass}}; found at CERN (2012). Not a force carrier.
- **Antimatter** → same mass, opposite charge; matter + antimatter → energy.
- **IceCube** → neutrino detector buried in {{Antarctic ice}}; also looks for dark matter.
- **Radioactivity** → comes from the nucleus. **Hydrogen bomb** → {{fusion}}. Atom bomb → fission.

Reactors and fuel → [Nuclear energy](@energy-technologies#nuclear)

## Gravity & Orbits {#gravity-orbits}

- A satellite is in {{continuous free fall}}; gravity bends its path around Earth.
- Orbital speed falls as the orbit gets higher.
- Escape velocity does not depend on the mass of the object.
