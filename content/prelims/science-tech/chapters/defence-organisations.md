## Chief of Defence Staff {#cds}

- Four-star officer; also **Secretary, Department of Military Affairs**.
- {{Permanent Chairman, Chiefs of Staff Committee}}.
- {{Principal Military Adviser}} to the Defence Minister on tri-service matters.
- Member, Defence Acquisition Council; adviser to the Nuclear Command Authority.
- Promotes jointness → procurement, training, logistics, theatre commands.

> [!TRAP] The CDS does !!not!! command the three Service Chiefs.

## Home Guards {#home-guards}

- Voluntary force; raised under {{State / UT Home Guards Acts}} — not a Central Act.
- Auxiliary to the {{police}}; helps in disasters, elections, essential services.
- **Border Wing Home Guards** → in some border States; assist the BSF.

Police and public order as State subjects → [Seventh Schedule](@prelims/polity/parts-schedules#seventh-schedule-lists)
