## Genetic Basics {#genetic-basics}

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **DNA** | Double helix; bases A, T, G, C |
| **RNA** | Single strand; U instead of T |
| **Gene** | Working segment of DNA |
| **Genome** | All the genetic material of an organism |
| **Chromosome** | DNA + protein |
| **Mutation** | Change in the DNA sequence |
| **Genotype · Phenotype** | Genetic make-up · visible trait |

> [!FLOW] DNA → (transcription) → mRNA → (translation) → protein. Retroviruses go backwards: RNA → DNA.

- Genetic code → 64 codons = {{61}} for 20 amino acids + {{3}} stop codons.
- DNA and RNA contain nitrogen; carbohydrates do not.

> [!UPPCS] **Mendel** → law of segregation + independent assortment. · ABO blood group → codominance, three alleles. · **Down** {{trisomy 21}} · **Turner** XO · **Klinefelter** XXY. · Mitochondrial DNA → normally from the mother.

## Recombinant DNA & PCR {#rdna}

| Tool | Job |
|---|---|
| **Restriction enzyme** | {{Cuts}} DNA at a set sequence |
| **Ligase** | {{Joins}} DNA |
| **Plasmid** | Small circular DNA of bacteria → carrier (vector) |
| **PCR** | Makes many copies of a DNA piece |

- Genes can be moved {{across species}} — even microbe to plant or animal.
- Products → human insulin, vaccines, GM crops.

## GM Crops {#gm-crops}

- Aims → pest resistance, herbicide tolerance, drought tolerance, more nutrition, longer shelf life.
- **Bt crops** → gene from the soil bacterium {{Bacillus thuringiensis}} makes a protein that kills bollworm-type pests. **Bt brinjal** → pest resistance.
- **Bollgard I and II** → Bt cotton.
- **Golden Rice** → {{Vitamin A}} (beta-carotene).
- GM mustard → uses gene technology for male sterility to make hybrids.

On the farm → [Agriculture](@prelims/agriculture) · Who approves GM crops → [GEAC](@prelims/environment/environmental-laws#epa) · Biosafety treaty → [Cartagena Protocol](@prelims/environment/international-conventions#cbd-family)

> [!DYN] Approval and court status of GM mustard and Bt brinjal is a current-layer item.

## RNAi, CRISPR & Gene Drive {#gene-editing}

| Tool | Does |
|---|---|
| **RNA interference** | {{Silences}} a gene by destroying its mRNA |
| **CRISPR-Cas9** | Guide RNA finds the spot; Cas9 {{cuts}} DNA — "molecular scissors" |
| **Gene drive** | Forces a gene to pass to almost all offspring |

- RNAi uses → gene-silencing medicines, cancer therapy, virus-resistant crops. !!Not!! hormone replacement.
- CRISPR → from a bacterial defence system.
- Editing embryos or sperm / egg (germline) → changes are {{inherited}}.

## Stem Cells, Cloning, Three-Parent Baby {#stem-cells-cloning}

- **Stem cells** → renew themselves + turn into other cells. Found in all multicellular life, not only mammals. Uses → drug testing, therapies.
- **iPSC** → adult cell reprogrammed to a stem cell.
- **Cloning (SCNT)** → nucleus of a body cell put into an egg whose nucleus is removed → Dolly the sheep.
- **Mitochondrial replacement** → child gets nuclear DNA from parents + mitochondria from a {{donor woman}} → avoids mitochondrial disease.

| Method | When |
|---|---|
| **Maternal spindle transfer** | {{Before}} fertilisation |
| **Pronuclear transfer** | {{After}} fertilisation |

## Genomics Tools {#genomics-tools}

| Tool | Tells |
|---|---|
| **Genome sequencing** | Full DNA sequence → markers, faster breeding, host–pathogen study |
| **Transcriptome** | All {{RNA being expressed}} in a cell |
| **DNA barcoding** | Which {{species}} — even look-alikes or processed food |
| **DNA fingerprinting** | Which {{individual}} |
| **Microsatellites** | Short repeats → relationships between populations |
| **Metagenomics** | DNA of all organisms in a sample, without culturing |
| **Aerial metagenomics** | DNA collected from {{air}} |

> [!TRAP] DNA mapping cannot find the cause of "all" diseases. · Barcoding does not tell the age of a plant or animal.

## Gene Therapy {#gene-therapy}

- Treats disease by adding, replacing, silencing or editing a gene.
- **Somatic** → patient only. **Germline** → inherited.
- Delivery → {{engineered viruses, lipid nanoparticles}}.
- Does !!not!! change the whole DNA of the person.

> [!DYN] Genome India and named gene-therapy approvals are current-layer items.
