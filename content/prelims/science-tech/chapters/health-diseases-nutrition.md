## Immunity {#immunity}

| | Innate | Adaptive (acquired) |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | From birth; non-specific; fast | Specific; has {{memory}} |
| Agents | Skin, mucus, phagocytes | {{B cells}} (antibodies) and {{T cells}} |

| | Active immunity | Passive immunity |
|---|---|---|
| Body gets | Germ or vaccine | {{Ready-made antibodies}} |
| Memory | Yes | No |
| Speed · duration | Slow · long | Instant · short |
| Examples | Vaccination | Anti-venom, rabies immunoglobulin, mother's milk |

## Vaccine Platforms {#vaccines}

| Platform | Contains | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Live attenuated** | Weakened germ | BCG, measles, oral polio |
| **Inactivated** | Killed whole germ | Covaxin, {{Sinovac}} |
| **Toxoid** | Inactivated toxin | Diphtheria, tetanus |
| **Conjugate** | Sugar coat of germ tied to a protein | Pneumococcal (PCV), Hib |
| **Viral vector** | Harmless virus carrying a gene of the germ | {{Covishield}}, Sputnik V |
| **mRNA** | mRNA that tells cells to make the antigen | {{Pfizer, Moderna}} |
| **DNA** | Plasmid DNA | ZyCoV-D |

- Vector vaccines → made by genetic engineering; the carrier can be a virus or a bacterium.
- **Pneumococcal conjugate vaccine** → guards against pneumonia, meningitis, sepsis; less need for antibiotics.

> [!TRAP] "No side effects" is never true of any vaccine.

## Monoclonal Antibodies & Wolbachia {#mab-wolbachia}

- **Monoclonal antibodies** → lab-made identical antibodies that lock on to {{one specific antigen}}; used in treating cancer, COVID, Nipah. Passive immunity — not a vaccine.
- **Wolbachia** → bacterium put into {{Aedes}} mosquitoes → they can no longer pass on dengue, Zika, chikungunya.

## AMR, Biofilms, Probiotics, Toxins {#amr}

- **Antimicrobial resistance** → microbes survive drugs. Causes → {{wrong dose or unfinished course}}, self-medication, {{antibiotics in livestock}}, poor hygiene.
- !!Not!! caused by people's genes.
- **Biofilm** → slimy layer of microbes on {{medical implants, food-processing surfaces}}, teeth; resists antibiotics.
- **Probiotics** → live helpful microbes — bacteria (Lactobacillus) and {{yeast}}. **Prebiotics** → fibre that feeds them.
- **Aflatoxin** → poison from {{fungus}} (Aspergillus) on stored grain and groundnut; not destroyed by cooking; damages liver.
- **Trans fats** → from hydrogenated oils; bad for the heart.
- **Antioxidants** → neutralise free radicals.

## Human Body {#human-body}

- **Digestion** → mouth (amylase, starch) → stomach ({{pepsin}}, protein) → small intestine (most digestion and absorption; trypsin from pancreas).
- **Pancreas** → both exocrine (enzymes) and endocrine: beta cells → {{insulin}}; alpha cells → glucagon. Faulty insulin → diabetes.
- **Blood** → plasma + red cells (haemoglobin, oxygen) + white cells (defence) + platelets (clotting).
- **Heart** → {{four}} chambers. Pulmonary **artery** → impure blood to lungs. Pulmonary **vein** → {{oxygen-rich}} blood to heart.
- **Lungs** → gas exchange in {{alveoli}}.
- **Brain** → medulla (heartbeat, breathing) · cerebellum (balance).

> [!UPPCS] Glands: **pituitary** master gland · **thyroid** thyroxine · **adrenal** adrenaline · **pancreas** insulin · **oxytocin** — a hormone (posterior pituitary). · Blood sugar is measured in mg/dL. · Blue-baby syndrome → nitrate in water.
