## Changes, Mixtures & Solutions {#changes-mixtures}

| | Physical change | Chemical change |
|---|---|---|
| New substance? | {{No}} | {{Yes}} |
| Examples | Melting ice, crystallising salt, dissolving sugar | Souring milk, rusting, burning |

- **Solution** → homogeneous mixture.
- **Sublimation** → solid straight to gas → {{camphor, naphthalene, dry ice}}.
- **Osmosis** → water moves across a membrane towards more salt → salted cucumber loses water.
- **Isotopes** → same atomic number, different mass number. Hydrogen → {{protium, deuterium, tritium}}.
- **Graphite** → most stable form of carbon.
- **Noble gases** → helium, neon, argon, krypton, xenon, radon.
- Sucrose + water → glucose + fructose (invert sugar).

## Acids, Bases & Common Compounds {#acids-bases-compounds}

- Acid → pH {{below 7}}; turns blue litmus red. Base → pH {{above 7}}.

| Common name | Chemical |
|---|---|
| **Baking soda** | {{Sodium hydrogen carbonate}} (NaHCO₃) — antacid, fire extinguishers |
| **Washing soda** | Sodium carbonate |
| **Quicklime** · **Slaked lime** | Calcium oxide · calcium hydroxide |
| **Limestone, chalk, marble** | {{Calcium carbonate}} |
| **Gypsum** · **Plaster of Paris** | Calcium sulphate (2 water · ½ water) |
| **Blue vitriol** | {{Copper sulphate}} |
| **Vinegar** | Acetic acid |
| **Laughing gas** | {{Nitrous oxide}} |
| **Dry ice** | Solid carbon dioxide |

- Silver turns black → {{silver sulphide}}.
- Silk and wool → proteins → contain nitrogen.

> [!UPPCS] **Zinc phosphide** → rat poison. · **Eosin** → red ink. · **Silver iodide** → artificial rain.

## Water & Purification {#water-purification}

- Water dissolves so much because its molecule is {{polar}}.
- **Hard water** → calcium and magnesium salts. Temporary (bicarbonates) → removed by boiling. Permanent → needs ion exchange.

| Method | Does | Does not |
|---|---|---|
| Filtration, settling | Removes suspended dirt | Remove dissolved salts |
| **Activated carbon** | {{Adsorbs}} organics, smell, colour | Kill all germs or remove salt |
| **Chlorine, ozone** | Kills germs | Remove salts |
| **UV** | {{Kills / inactivates microbes}} | Remove turbidity, smell or salts |
| **Reverse osmosis** | Removes dissolved salts | |

Activated carbon in pollution control → [Treatment technologies](@prelims/environment/waste-remediation#treatment-technologies)

## Cells & Batteries — Basics {#electrochemistry}

- Battery discharging → **anode** = oxidation, **cathode** = reduction; electrons flow anode → cathode outside.
- **Primary** cell → use once. **Secondary** → rechargeable (lead-acid, lithium-ion).

EV batteries and fuel cells → [Batteries](@energy-technologies#batteries)

## Polymers & Plastics {#polymers}

| Type | On heating | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Thermoplastic** | Softens, can be remoulded | {{Polythene, PVC, PET}} |
| **Thermoset** | Sets hard for good | {{Bakelite, melamine}} |

- Natural polymers → cellulose, starch, proteins, rubber.
- **PET** → bottles; fibre blended with cotton and wool; recyclable.
- **BPA** → in polycarbonate. **Triclosan** → in soaps. **PFAS** → "forever chemicals".
- Hidden plastic → {{cigarette filters, spectacle lenses, tyres, chewing gum}}.

Their effect on health and environment → [Emerging contaminants](@prelims/environment/pollution#emerging-contaminants)

## Explosives & Cloud Seeding {#explosives-cloud-seeding}

- High explosives → {{CL-20, HMX, LLM-105}}, RDX, TNT. Dynamite → nitroglycerine (Alfred Nobel).
- **Cloud seeding** → {{silver iodide}} (also potassium iodide, dry ice, salt) gives water droplets a seed to form on.
- Needs clouds already present. Not the same as geoengineering → [Geoengineering](@prelims/environment/climate-change#mitigation-taxonomy).
