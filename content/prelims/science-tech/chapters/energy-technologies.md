## Hydrogen & Fuel Cells {#hydrogen-fuel-cells}

| Hydrogen | Made by |
|---|---|
| **Green** | Splitting water with {{renewable electricity}} |
| **Blue** | From natural gas / coal, with the CO₂ {{captured}} |
| **Grey** | From natural gas, CO₂ released |

- Green hydrogen uses → burn directly · blend with natural gas (H-CNG) · run fuel cells · make {{fertiliser (ammonia), refine oil, make steel}}.

**Fuel cell**
- Hydrogen + oxygen → electricity + {{water}} + heat. No burning.
- Gives {{direct current (DC)}}.
- Keeps running as long as fuel is fed — unlike a battery.
- Sizes → from laptops to buses to buildings.
- **Fuel-cell vehicle** → hydrogen tank → fuel cell → motor. Tailpipe emits only water vapour (how the hydrogen was made still matters).

> [!TRAP] Fuel cells do !!not!! give AC directly, and are not only for buildings.

> [!DYN] National Green Hydrogen Mission targets and dates are current-layer.

## Solar & Distributed Energy {#solar}

| | Photovoltaic | Solar thermal |
|---|---|---|
| Converts | Light → {{electricity (DC)}} | Sunlight → {{heat}} |

- **Net metering** → rooftop owner sends surplus power to the grid and gets credit on the bill.
- **Distributed energy resources** → small sources near the user: {{rooftop solar, battery storage, fuel cells, biomass generators}}.
- Solar can run all kinds of pumps — surface and submersible.
- Solar, wind, biogas → renewable. Coal, petroleum → not.

India's solar and energy schemes → [Energy schemes](@prelims/economy/government-schemes#energy-schemes) · Solar Alliance → [Initiatives](@prelims/environment/international-conventions#initiatives)

## Batteries & Electric Vehicles {#batteries}

| Part of a lithium-ion cell | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Anode** | {{Graphite}} |
| **Cathode** | Lithium with {{nickel, cobalt, manganese}} (NMC) or iron phosphate (LFP) |
| **Electrolyte** | Carries lithium ions |
| **Separator** | Keeps electrodes apart |

- **LFP** → safer, cheaper, no cobalt; less energy. **NMC** → more energy.
- **Regenerative braking** → motor works as generator and recharges the battery.

| Vehicle | Runs on |
|---|---|
| **BEV** | Battery only; plug-in |
| **Hybrid (HEV)** | Engine + motor; no plug |
| **Plug-in hybrid** | Engine + bigger battery you can plug in |
| **Fuel-cell (FCEV)** | Hydrogen fuel cell + motor |

Where the metals come from → [Critical minerals](@prelims/geography/world-resources#critical-minerals) · Basic cell chemistry → [Cells & batteries](@chemistry-daily-life#electrochemistry)

## Nuclear Energy {#nuclear}

| Process | What happens | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Fission** | Heavy nucleus splits; chain reaction | Power reactors |
| **Fusion** | Light nuclei join | Sun; {{ITER}} experiment |
| **Radioactive decay** | Isotope decays, gives heat | {{RTG}} — power for deep-space probes |

- **Heavy water** → {{moderator}}: slows neutrons. Not a fuel.
- **Thorium** → more abundant than uranium in India; found in {{monazite}}; cleaner waste; must first be bred into uranium-233.
- Power reactors do !!not!! need 60% enriched uranium.
- **ITER** (France) → to prove fusion power; India is a member.
- **RTG** → radioisotope thermoelectric generator; no chain reaction, no moving parts. Not a small reactor.

Thorium sands and plant locations → [Atomic minerals](@prelims/geography/india-minerals-energy-industry#atomic-beach-minerals) · [Power stations](@prelims/geography/india-minerals-energy-industry#power)

## Bioenergy & Gasification {#bioenergy}

- **Biomass** → recent plant and animal matter. Coal is fossil — !!not!! biomass.
- **Pyrolysis** → heating without oxygen. **Gasification** → with limited oxygen → syngas.
- Biofuels, gasification feedstock, aviation fuel → [Bioenergy](@prelims/environment/bioenergy-bio-inputs#biofuel-generations).
- Coal gasification products → [Coal](@prelims/geography/india-minerals-energy-industry#coal).
