## AI, Machine Learning & LLMs {#ai-ml}

> [!FLOW] AI ⊃ Machine Learning ⊃ Deep Learning ⊃ Large Language Models.

- **AI** → machines doing tasks that need human-like intelligence.
- **Machine learning** → learns patterns from data. **Deep learning** → uses many-layered neural networks.
- **LLM** → trained on huge text; predicts the {{next word (token)}} by probability; trained by mathematical optimisation.
- Outputs are !!not!! guaranteed to be unbiased or correct.
- AI can → cut industrial power use, help diagnose disease, turn text into speech. It cannot transmit electricity wirelessly.

## Blockchain, Crypto, Web3, NFTs {#blockchain}

- **Blockchain** → shared ledger copied across many computers; each block carries the {{hash}} of the previous one → very hard to alter.
- No single controller; anyone on a public chain can view records.
- !!Not!! only for cryptocurrency → land records, supply chains, certificates.
- **Smart contract** → code that runs itself when conditions are met.
- **Bitcoin** → person-to-person transfer by address; no central bank tracks it; users are pseudonymous.
- **Web3** → users control their own data; run by the community on blockchain, not by one company.
- **NFT** → {{unique}} token; can stand for a digital or physical asset; not interchangeable one-for-one like money.
- **Tokenisation of real-world assets** → property, bonds, gold as blockchain tokens; tradable round the clock.

> [!TRAP] A consortium blockchain is !!not!! simply "a blend of public and private blockchains".

Digital rupee and payment systems → [CBDC](@prelims/economy/money-rbi-monetary-policy#cbdc) · [UPI](@prelims/economy/digital-payments#rtgs-neft-imps-upi)

## IoT, Wearables, 3D Printing, Biometrics {#iot-3d-biometrics}

- **Internet of Things** → everyday devices with sensors, linked to the internet, acting on their own.
- **Edge computing** → process data near the device → faster.
- **Wearables** → location, sleep tracking, hearing aid.
- **3D printing** → additive manufacturing: object built {{layer by layer}} from a digital design. Uses → food, artificial ears, car parts, surgery.
- **Biometrics** → fingerprint, {{iris, retina, voice}}, face.

## Quantum Computing {#quantum}

- Classical **bit** → 0 or 1. **Qubit** → can be both at once ({{superposition}}).
- **Entanglement** → linked qubits. **Decoherence** → qubits lose their state through noise — the main hurdle.
- Uses → drug and material design, optimisation, breaking and making codes.
- **Quantum key distribution** → any eavesdropping disturbs the key and is noticed.
- **National Quantum Mission** → quantum computers of {{50–1,000 physical qubits}}; {{four}} thematic hubs.

> [!DYN] New quantum chips (e.g. Microsoft's Majorana 1) and "fastest supercomputer" rankings are current-layer.

> [!UPPCS] **Input** → keyboard, mouse, scanner. **Output** → monitor, printer, projector. · **RAM** → volatile. **ROM** → {{non-volatile}}. · **Blu-ray** holds more than DVD and supports HD.
