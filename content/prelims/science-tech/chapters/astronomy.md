## Life of a Star {#stars}

> [!FLOW] Nebula (gas + dust) → protostar → main-sequence star → **small star**: red giant → white dwarf · **massive star**: supergiant → supernova → neutron star or black hole.

- Bigger stars burn fuel faster → {{shorter}} lives.
- **Chandrasekhar limit** (~1.4 solar masses) → the most a {{white dwarf}} can weigh.
- **Brown dwarf** → too small to start hydrogen fusion — a "failed star".

| Object | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Cepheid** | Star whose brightness {{rises and falls regularly}} — used to measure distance |
| **Nebula** | Cloud of gas and dust |
| **Pulsar** | Fast-spinning {{neutron star}} sending regular radio pulses |

## Black Holes & Gravitational Waves {#black-holes-gw}

- **Black hole** → gravity so strong that light cannot escape; formed when a massive star collapses.
- **Event horizon** → boundary of no return. **Singularity** → the centre.
- **Gravitational waves** → ripples in space-time, e.g. from {{merging black holes}}; predicted by Einstein; first detected in 2015.
- **LIGO** → ground detector using laser interferometry.
- **LISA / eLISA** → planned detector in space — {{three spacecraft in a triangle}}, millions of km apart, linked by lasers.

> [!TRAP] Gravitational waves ≠ gravitational lensing (bending of light by gravity).

## Cosmology {#cosmology}

- Evidence the universe is expanding → {{redshift of galaxies + cosmic microwave background}}.
- **Dark matter** → unseen mass, known by its gravity. **Dark energy** → drives faster expansion.
- **Antimatter** studies → why the universe is made of matter.
- Vocabulary of the field → event horizon, singularity, string theory, Standard Model → [Particles](@physics-everyday#forces-particles).

## Sun & Space Weather {#space-weather}

- **Solar wind** → steady stream of charged particles. **Solar flare** → burst of radiation. **Coronal mass ejection** → cloud of plasma thrown out.
- Earth's magnetic field deflects them; some reach the poles → **aurora**.

A strong solar storm can:
- knock out {{GPS and navigation}};
- damage {{power grids}};
- disturb satellites and their orbits;
- black out shortwave radio over the poles;
- make aurora visible far from the poles.

!!Not!! → tsunami, forest fires.

Effect on Earth's upper air → [Atmosphere layers](@prelims/geography/climatology#atmosphere-layers)

- **Mars** → the planet most searched for life.

## Distance Units & Celestial Navigation {#distance-navigation}

| Unit | Is |
|---|---|
| **Astronomical unit** | Earth–Sun distance |
| **Light-year** | Distance light travels in a year — a {{distance}}, not time |
| **Parsec** | About {{3.26}} light-years |

**Finding position by the sky**
- Measure the angle of the Sun or a star above the horizon with a **sextant**; add exact time and an almanac.
- Height of the **Pole Star** above the horizon ≈ your {{latitude}} (Northern Hemisphere).
- Longitude needs accurate time → [Time and longitude](@prelims/geography/earth-in-space#time).
