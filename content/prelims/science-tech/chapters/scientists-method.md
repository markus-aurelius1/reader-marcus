## Scientific Method {#scientific-method}

> [!FLOW] Observation → hypothesis → prediction → experiment → analysis → repeat.

- **Hypothesis** → testable, can be proved wrong.
- **Theory** → well-tested explanation — not a guess.
- **Law** → states a regular relationship; a theory does not "grow into" a law.
- Correlation ≠ causation.
- **Astrology** is !!not!! a science — its claims are not testable. Astronomy is.
