## Orbits {#orbits}

| Orbit | Height / feature | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| **LEO** — low Earth | Below ~2,000 km | Earth observation, space stations |
| **MEO** — medium | Between LEO and GEO | {{Navigation}} satellites |
| **GTO** — transfer | Long ellipse | Route up to GEO |
| **Geosynchronous** | Period = Earth's rotation (24 h) | |
| **Geostationary** | Geosynchronous + {{circular + over the Equator}}; ~{{35,786 km}}; appears fixed | Communication, weather |
| **Polar** | Passes over the poles | Covers the whole Earth |
| **Sun-synchronous** | Near-polar; crosses each place at the {{same local time}} | Remote sensing |

- In an ellipse → fastest at {{perigee}} (nearest), slowest at apogee.
- A satellite stays up because it is in free fall → [Gravity](@physics-everyday#gravity-orbits).

> [!TRAP] Every geostationary orbit is geosynchronous — not the reverse. · "22,236" is the height in **miles**, not km.

## Launch Vehicles {#launch-vehicles}

| Vehicle | Carries | Stages |
|---|---|---|
| **PSLV** | Remote-sensing satellites to polar / Sun-synchronous orbit | {{4}} — solid, liquid, solid, liquid |
| **GSLV Mk II** | Communication satellites to GTO | 3 — top stage {{cryogenic}} |
| **LVM3** (was GSLV Mk III) | Heaviest; Chandrayaan, Gaganyaan | 3 — two solid boosters + liquid core + cryogenic top |

- **Cryogenic stage** → liquid hydrogen + liquid oxygen at very low temperature; most efficient.
- **Solid** fuel → simple, powerful, cannot be switched off. **Liquid** → controllable.
- **Scramjet** → takes oxygen from the air; rockets carry their own.

> [!TRAP] PSLV has !!no!! cryogenic stage.

## Remote Sensing {#remote-sensing}

- **Passive** sensors → read reflected sunlight; blocked by cloud. **Active (radar / SAR)** → send microwaves; see through cloud, day and night.
- Can measure → crop yield, groundwater, minerals, {{chlorophyll, land-surface temperature, methane from paddy fields}}, forest cover, floods.
- Indian Remote Sensing satellites are !!not!! used for telecommunication.

Maps built from this → [India's drainage](@prelims/geography/india-drainage#groundwater) · [Forest cover](@prelims/geography/india-climate-soils-vegetation#vegetation)
