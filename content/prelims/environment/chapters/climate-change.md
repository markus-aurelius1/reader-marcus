## Greenhouse Science {#climate-science}

- Sun sends shortwave; Earth sends back {{longwave (infrared)}}; greenhouse gases trap it.
- Gases → CO₂, methane, N₂O, ozone, fluorinated gases; water vapour (a feedback).
- !!Not!! greenhouse gases → nitrogen, oxygen, {{argon}}. Black carbon is soot, not a gas.
- **GWP** → warming power relative to CO₂ over a stated period (usually 100 years).

| Gas | Main sources | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Methane** | {{Paddy fields, cattle, landfills}}, gas leaks, wetlands | Strong but short-lived (~decade) |
| **N₂O** (laughing gas) | {{Fertilised soils}}, manure | Long-lived; also harms ozone |
| **CO₂** | Fossil fuel, cement, deforestation | No single lifetime |

- Rice → methane **and** N₂O. Alternate wetting and drying (and SRI) cuts methane.
- **Methane hydrates** → warming could release them → more warming.
- **Carbon fertilisation** → more CO₂ can boost plant growth — limited by water, nutrients, heat. Not unlimited.

The physics of the heat budget → [Heat budget](@prelims/geography/climatology#heat-budget)

## Carbon Sinks {#carbon-sinks}

- **Sink** → absorbs more carbon than it releases.
- Soil · forests · oceans (phytoplankton) · **peatlands** — waterlogged, very carbon-dense; draining or burning turns them into a source.
- Largest tropical peatland → {{Congo Basin}}.
- **Blue carbon** → [Mangroves & blue carbon](@marine-ecology#mangroves-blue-carbon).

## Mitigation — Five Boxes {#mitigation-taxonomy}

| Box | Does | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Cut emissions** | Stops release | Renewables, efficiency |
| **CCS** | Captures CO₂ at the chimney, stores it | Power, cement plants |
| **CCUS** | Captures and uses it | Fuels, chemicals — may be released again |
| **CDR** (carbon removal) | Takes CO₂ {{out of the air}} | Trees, soil carbon, biochar, direct air capture, enhanced weathering |
| **SRM** (solar radiation management) | Reflects sunlight | Sulphate aerosols, cloud brightening — {{does not remove CO₂}} |

**Removal and storage methods**
- **Geological storage** → {{depleted oil and gas fields, deep saline formations, unmineable coal seams}}.
- **Enhanced rock weathering** → crushed basalt on land absorbs CO₂.
- **Ocean alkalinity enhancement** → sea takes up more CO₂.
- **Direct air capture** → chemicals pull CO₂ from air → stored, or used for fuels and plastics.
- **Biochar** · **zero tillage** → carbon in soil.

**Geoengineering**
- **Stratospheric aerosol injection** → like a volcano; cools.
- **Cirrus cloud thinning** → lets more heat escape.
- Ocean acidification continues under SRM.

## Carbon Markets {#carbon-markets}

- **Carbon credit** → tradable unit = {{1 tonne of CO₂-equivalent}} reduced or removed.
- **Compliance market** (by law, cap-and-trade) vs **voluntary market**.
- **Allowance** = permit to emit. **Offset** = certified reduction elsewhere.
- Price → from the market. No UN body fixes it.
- **Social cost of carbon** → rupee value of the damage from {{one extra tonne}} of CO₂. Not a tax or a market price.

Treaty side — Kyoto, Paris Article 6 → [Climate treaties](@international-conventions#climate-treaties)

## Ozone {#ozone}

- Stratospheric ozone → shield against UV ("good"). Ground-level ozone → pollutant ("bad").
- Destroyers → chlorine and bromine from {{CFCs, halons, carbon tetrachloride, methyl bromide, HCFCs}}.

> [!FLOW] **Antarctic ozone hole** → very cold polar vortex → polar stratospheric clouds → chlorine activated on them → spring sunlight → ozone destroyed.

- CFC uses → refrigerants, foams, aerosol sprays, cleaning electronics.
- **HFCs** → do {{not}} harm ozone, but are strong greenhouse gases.
- **Montreal Protocol** → ozone-depleting substances. **Kigali Amendment (2016)** → phase down HFCs.

Where the ozone layer sits → [Atmosphere layers](@prelims/geography/climatology#atmosphere-layers)
