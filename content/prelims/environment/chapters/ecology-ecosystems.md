## Ecosystem, Food Chain & Energy Flow {#energy-flow}

- **Ecosystem** = living community + non-living environment working as one unit.
- **Energy** → flows {{one way}}: Sun → producers → consumers → lost as heat.
- **Nutrients** → {{cycle}} between living and non-living pools.
- **Food chain** = who eats whom, in sequence. **Food web** = interlinked chains → more stable.
- **Trophic level** = feeding position. An omnivore can sit at more than one level.
- **Decomposers** → bacteria and fungi; return nutrients to the soil.

## Ecological Pyramids {#pyramids}

| Pyramid | Shape | Exception |
|---|---|---|
| **Energy** | {{Always upright}} | None |
| **Biomass** | Usually upright on land | {{Inverted in oceans}} — little phytoplankton standing crop, fast turnover |
| **Numbers** | Varies | Inverted for a single tree or a parasite chain |

- **10% rule** → roughly a tenth of the **energy** passes to the next level. A rough guide, not an exact law.

> [!TRAP] The 10% rule is about energy — !!not!! "10% of organic matter / biomass must reach the next level".

## Productivity {#productivity}

- **GPP** = total energy fixed by photosynthesis.
- **NPP** = {{GPP − plant respiration}} → what is left for consumers.
- Decreasing productivity → {{mangroves > grasslands > lakes > open oceans}}.
- Limits → light, water, temperature, nutrients.

## Nutrient Cycles {#nutrient-cycles}

| Cycle | Type | Key point |
|---|---|---|
| **Carbon** | Gaseous | Photosynthesis removes CO₂; {{respiration, decay, volcanoes, burning}} add it |
| **Nitrogen** | Gaseous | N₂ must be **fixed** before most life can use it |
| **Phosphorus** | {{Sedimentary}} | Enters by {{weathering of rocks}}; no real gas phase |
| **Sulphur** | {{Mostly sedimentary}} | Has an atmospheric phase too (SO₂, H₂S) |

**Nitrogen steps**

| Step | Change |
|---|---|
| **Fixation** | N₂ → ammonia |
| **Nitrification** | Ammonia → nitrite → {{nitrate}} |
| **Assimilation** | Plants take up nitrate / ammonium |
| **Ammonification** | Dead matter → ammonia |
| **Denitrification** | Nitrate → {{N₂}} (and N₂O), where oxygen is low |

- Fixers → **Rhizobium** (legume nodules) · **Azotobacter** (free-living) · **Nostoc, Anabaena** (blue-green algae) → [Biofertilisers](@bioenergy-bio-inputs#biofertilisers).

Leached rainforest soil as an example → [Rainforest soil](@prelims/geography/climatic-regions#rainforest-nutrients)

## Succession & Niche {#succession-niche}

- **Succession** = orderly change of communities over time. Whole sequence = **sere**; end stage = **climax**.
- **Primary** → on bare ground with no soil; {{lichens}} pioneer on rock.
- **Secondary** → after a disturbance where soil remains → {{faster}}.
- Grassland stays grassland because of {{limited water + fire}} (and grazing).
- **Habitat** = where a species lives (address). **Niche** = its {{role}} — what it eats, tolerates, and how it interacts (profession).
- **Ecotone** = transition zone between two ecosystems.
- Two species cannot occupy exactly the same niche for long.

## Bioaccumulation vs Biomagnification {#bioaccumulation}

- **Bioaccumulation** → build-up inside {{one organism}} over time.
- **Biomagnification** → concentration rises {{up the food chain}}.
- Worst offenders → persistent, fat-soluble chemicals (DDT, methylmercury, some PFAS).
