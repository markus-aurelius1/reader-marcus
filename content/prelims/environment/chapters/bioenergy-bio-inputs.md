## Biofuel Generations {#biofuel-generations}

| Generation | Feedstock | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **1G** | Food crops — sugar, starch, oil | Sugarcane / maize ethanol; food-vs-fuel issue |
| **2G** | {{Non-food residue}} — straw, bagasse, wood | Cellulosic ethanol |
| **3G** | {{Algae}} | High yield; costly to scale |
| **4G** | Engineered biomass + carbon capture | |

- Ethanol is 1G or 2G {{depending on feedstock}}.
- USA → {{maize}}. Brazil → {{sugarcane}}.
- **Bagasse** → fuel for sugar-mill power. **Molasses** → ethanol.
- **Biogas** → anaerobic decay → mostly methane; renewable.
- Algae fuel → not limited to the sea; scale and cost are the limits.

Hydrogen, fuel cells and other energy routes → [Hydrogen & fuel cells](@prelims/science-tech/energy-technologies#hydrogen-fuel-cells)

## Biofuel Policy Feedstocks {#biofuel-policy}

- Allowed for ethanol → sugarcane juice, sugar beet, sweet sorghum · maize, {{cassava}} · {{damaged wheat, broken rice, rotten potatoes}} · surplus grain (with approval).
- !!Not!! in the tested list → groundnut seeds, horse gram.
- **Sustainable Aviation Fuel** → from farm residue, maize, sewage sludge, wood waste — many feedstocks.

## Biomass Gasification {#gasification}

- Biomass + limited air → **producer gas / syngas** → {{CO + H₂}} (with some CH₄, CO₂).
- Feed → rice husk, coconut shell, groundnut shell, wood.
- Uses → heat **and** running engines / generators.

Same process for coal → [Coal gasification](@prelims/geography/india-minerals-energy-industry#coal)

## Microbial Fuel Cells {#microbial-fuel-cells}

- Microbes act as {{living catalysts}} → break down organic matter → release electrons → electricity.
- Can clean {{wastewater}} while making power.

## Biofertilisers {#biofertilisers}

| Input | Role |
|---|---|
| **Rhizobium** | Fixes nitrogen in {{legume root nodules}} |
| **Azotobacter** | Free-living nitrogen fixer |
| **Nostoc, Anabaena** | Nitrogen-fixing blue-green algae; rice fields |
| **Azolla** | Water fern carrying Anabaena → {{paddy}} biofertiliser |
| **Mycorrhiza** | Fungus on roots → more {{phosphorus}} and water; drought and disease tolerance; helps restore degraded land |

> [!TRAP] Agaricus (mushroom) and Spirogyra are !!not!! biofertilisers.
