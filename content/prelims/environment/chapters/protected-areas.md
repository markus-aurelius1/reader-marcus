## Categories — Do Not Merge {#pa-categories}

| Category | Basis | Human use |
|---|---|---|
| **National Park** | Wild Life (Protection) Act, 1972 | Strictest; {{no grazing}}; rights settled |
| **Wildlife Sanctuary** | Same Act | Some rights and regulated grazing may continue |
| **Conservation Reserve** | Same Act | {{Government land}} next to parks or as corridors; local people consulted |
| **Community Reserve** | Same Act | {{Private or community land}} offered voluntarily; run by a Management Committee |
| **Tiger Reserve** | Same Act + NTCA | Core + buffer |
| **Biosphere Reserve** | Government programme + UNESCO MAB | Zoned: conservation with sustainable use. {{Not a category under the Act}} |
| **Ramsar Site** | International convention | "Wise use". Not automatically a park or sanctuary |

- Hunting is banned in all four statutory categories.
- Community Reserve → governed by its {{Management Committee}}, not by the Chief Wildlife Warden alone; land use cannot be changed freely.

> [!TRAP] "No human activity at all in a National Park" is too absolute. · A sanctuary is not open to unrestricted use.

In-situ vs ex-situ → [Conservation types](@biodiversity#in-situ-ex-situ) · The Act itself → [Wildlife Act](@environmental-laws#wildlife-act)

## Tiger Reserves {#tiger-reserves}

- **Project Tiger** → {{1973}}. **NTCA** → statutory body under the Wildlife Act.
- **Core / Critical Tiger Habitat** → parts of parks and sanctuaries kept inviolate.
- **Buffer** → lesser protection; people and wildlife coexist.
- A reserve can span several parks, sanctuaries and forests.
- **M-STrIPES** → app-based {{patrol and monitoring}} system for tigers.
- Critical **Tiger** Habitat (Wildlife Act) ≠ Critical **Wildlife** Habitat ([Forest Rights Act](@environmental-laws#forest-rights-act)).

## Wetlands & Ramsar {#wetlands-ramsar}

- Wetland → land saturated with water; water-loving plants; waterlogged soils.
- Services → flood control, groundwater recharge, shoreline protection, carbon store, fish nursery.
- "{{Kidneys of the landscape}}" → filter sediment, nutrients and pollutants.

**Ramsar Convention** (Iran, {{1971}})
- Wise use of all wetlands · list wetlands of international importance · cooperate across borders.
- **Montreux Record** → Ramsar sites whose ecological character {{has changed or is likely to change}} due to human activity. India → {{Keoladeo and Loktak}}.

**Wetlands Rules, 2017** (under the Environment Protection Act; replaced the 2010 Rules)
- {{National Wetlands Committee}} at the Centre + State / UT Wetlands Authorities.
- Bars conversion, dumping and untreated discharge in notified wetlands.

> [!TRAP] There is !!no!! "National Wetland Authority" under the 2017 Rules.

> [!DYN] Ramsar-site totals, new sites and State-wise counts (including Uttar Pradesh) change every year.
