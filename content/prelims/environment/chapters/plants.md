## Native, Introduced, Endemic {#native-introduced}

- **Red Sanders** → endemic to the southern {{Eastern Ghats}} (Andhra Pradesh); dry deciduous forest. !!Not!! a rainforest tree.
- Brought by the {{Portuguese}} from the Americas → cashew, papaya, pineapple, guava (also chilli, potato, tobacco).
- **Foxtail orchid** → grows on trees (epiphyte); State flower of Assam and Arunachal; not confined to the North-East.

When and how the Portuguese came → [Portuguese](@prelims/history-culture/europeans-british-expansion#portuguese)

## Legumes & Nitrogen {#legumes}

- Pea family (Fabaceae) → {{groundnut, soybean, horse gram}}, pulses.
- Root nodules hold **Rhizobium** → fix nitrogen.
- Cowpea, green gram, pigeon pea → pulse + fodder + green manure.
- **Green manure** = crop ploughed back in. **Biofertiliser** = living microbes → [Biofertilisers](@bioenergy-bio-inputs#biofertilisers).

## Adaptations {#adaptations}

| Type | Features |
|---|---|
| **Xerophyte** (dry) | Spines, thick cuticle, fleshy stem, deep roots |
| **Hydrophyte** (water) | Air spaces, weak stem |
| **Halophyte / mangrove** | Salt-tolerant; breathing roots |
| **Epiphyte** | Sits on another plant for support — {{not a parasite}} |
