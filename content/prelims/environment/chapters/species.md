## Vocabulary & IUCN Categories {#vocabulary}

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Native** | Occurs naturally in the region |
| **Endemic** | Naturally found {{only}} in that region |
| **Alien / exotic** | Introduced by people |
| **Invasive** | Alien that spreads and harms → [Invasives](@biodiversity#invasive-species) |

- IUCN ladder → {{EX → EW → CR → EN → VU → NT → LC}}; plus DD (Data Deficient), NE (Not Evaluated).
- **Threatened** = {{CR + EN + VU}}. Near Threatened is not "threatened".
- **Red List / Red Data Book** → extinction risk of species. Not a list of protected areas.
- IUCN category ≠ schedule under Indian law → [Wildlife Act schedules](@environmental-laws#wildlife-act).

> [!DYN] A species' IUCN status changes with reassessment — check the current status of any species in the news.

- Found in India → black-necked crane, flying squirrel, snow leopard, star tortoise, monitor lizard, pygmy hog. !!Not!! → cheetah (extinct, reintroduced), spider monkey, marsupials.
- **Asiatic lion** → wild only in India. **One-horned rhino** → also Nepal. **Double-humped camel** → also Central Asia.
- Abroad → **Indri** Madagascar · **Bonobo** DR Congo · **Elk** North America, Eurasia.

> [!TRAP] "Naturally found" ≠ kept in a zoo or reintroduced.

## Behaviour & Classification {#behaviour}

| Animal | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Dugong (sea cow)** · sea lion | {{Mammals}} |
| **Seahorse** | {{Fish}} |
| **Echidna, platypus** | Egg-laying mammals |
| **Kharai camel** | Swims in the sea; eats {{mangroves}}; domesticated (Kachchh) |
| **Honeybee** | Waggle dance → direction and distance of food |
| **Orangutan** | Uses tools |
| **Malabar civet** | Nocturnal |
| **Hedgehog, pangolin** | Roll into a ball |
| **Leaf-cutter ants** | Farm fungus |
| **Indian squirrels** | Store food; omnivorous |
| **Tarantula** | Arachnid, not crustacean |
| **Dolphins, whales** | Breathe through {{blowholes}} |
| **Earthworm** | Breathes through skin |
| **Oryx** vs **Chiru** | Hot desert vs cold Tibetan plateau |

- **Parasitoid** → larva lives on one host and kills it; common in {{wasps, flies}}, some beetles.
- Plant-eaters exist among turtles, fish and marine mammals. Some snakes give birth to live young.
- **Xericoles** → desert-adapted animals.
- Butterflies → pollinators and prey; their decline hits pollination and the food chain.

## Threats {#threats}

- **Vultures** → killed by {{diclofenac}}, a painkiller given to cattle.
- **Ganges river dolphin** → dams and barrages, fishing nets, pollution.
- Across species → habitat loss and fragmentation first.
