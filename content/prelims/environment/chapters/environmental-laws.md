## Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 {#epa}

- Umbrella law (after Bhopal). Centre can set standards, restrict where industries locate, handle hazardous substances, {{order closure}}.
- Rules and notifications under it → EIA, Coastal Regulation Zone, waste rules, wetland rules, eco-sensitive zones.

| Body | Created under |
|---|---|
| **Central Ground Water Authority** | {{Environment (Protection) Act}} |
| **GEAC** (clears GM crops) | Rules under the same Act |
| **CPCB, State Boards** | {{Water Act, 1974}} — statutory |
| **NTCA, National Board for Wildlife, Central Zoo Authority, Wildlife Crime Control Bureau** | Wild Life (Protection) Act |
| **National Biodiversity Authority** | Biological Diversity Act |
| **Animal Welfare Board** | Prevention of Cruelty to Animals Act, 1960 |

> [!TRAP] CPCB was !!not!! created by executive order or under the 1986 Act.

What a GM crop is → [GM crops](@prelims/science-tech/genetics-biotechnology#gm-crops) · Groundwater on the map → [Groundwater](@prelims/geography/india-drainage#groundwater)

**Eco-Sensitive Zones**
- Notified under the 1986 Act around parks and sanctuaries → a "shock absorber".
- Activities → prohibited (mining, polluting industry) · regulated · permitted. Not all human activity is banned.
- No single fixed width for every zone.

**Ganga**
- National Ganga River Basin Authority (under the 1986 Act) → {{dissolved in 2016}}.
- Now → **National Ganga Council** (chaired by the PM) + **NMCG**.

## Wild Life (Protection) Act, 1972 {#wildlife-act}

Schedules after the 2022 amendment (in force {{1 April 2023}}) → {{four}}:

| Schedule | Covers |
|---|---|
| **I** | Animals with the highest protection |
| **II** | Other protected animals |
| **III** | Protected plants |
| **IV** | Species listed under CITES |

- Old law had six schedules; **Schedule VI** listed plants whose cultivation needed a licence — historical now.
- Hunting of scheduled animals → banned; permitted only by the Chief Wildlife Warden on specific grounds.
- Wild animals and trophies covered by the Act → {{government property}}.
- **National Board for Wildlife** → chaired by the {{Prime Minister}}. State Boards → Chief Minister.

Parks, sanctuaries, tiger reserves → [Categories](@protected-areas#pa-categories)

## Forest Rights Act, 2006 {#forest-rights-act}

- Recognises rights of **forest-dwelling Scheduled Tribes** and **Other Traditional Forest Dwellers**.
- Nodal ministry → {{Ministry of Tribal Affairs}}.
- {{Gram Sabha}} starts the process of deciding rights.
- Rights → land under occupation (up to {{4 hectares}}) · ownership of **minor forest produce** · grazing, fishing · habitat rights of PVTGs · right to protect community forest.
- {{Bamboo}} → minor forest produce.
- "Three generations / {{75 years}}" → applies to {{Other Traditional Forest Dwellers}} only.
- **Critical Wildlife Habitat** → parts of parks and sanctuaries kept inviolate, defined under this Act.

Its place among tribal safeguards → [Forest Rights Act](@prelims/polity/scheduled-tribal-areas#forest-rights-act-2006)

## Biological Diversity Act, 2002 {#biodiversity-act}

- Gives effect to the Convention on Biological Diversity → conservation, sustainable use, benefit sharing.
- Three tiers → **National Biodiversity Authority** ({{Chennai}}) → **State Biodiversity Boards** → **Biodiversity Management Committees** in every local body.
- Committee → keeps the {{People's Biodiversity Register}}; may charge {{collection fees}} for commercial collection.
- Link to the Nagoya Protocol → [CBD family](@international-conventions#cbd-family).

> [!DYN] The 2023 amendment changed who needs approval — check current provisions.

## National Green Tribunal {#ngt}

- NGT Act, {{2010}}. Civil cases on a substantial environmental question under the laws in its **Schedule I**.
- Schedule I → Water Act · Forest (Conservation) Act · Air Act · Environment (Protection) Act · Public Liability Insurance Act · Biological Diversity Act.
- !!Not!! in it → Wild Life (Protection) Act, Indian Forest Act, Forest Rights Act.
- Follows {{natural justice}}, not the Civil Procedure Code. Tries to decide in six months.
- Appeal → {{Supreme Court}}.

Tribunals in general → [Tribunals](@prelims/polity/judiciary#tribunals)

## Forest Conservation & CAMPA {#campa}

- Forest (Conservation) law → Centre's approval needed to divert forest land.
- **Compensatory Afforestation Fund Act, 2016** → National and State Funds; **CAMPA** at {{both National and State level}}.
- People's participation is !!not!! made compulsory in every afforestation programme.

## Waste Rules {#waste-rules}

- **Solid Waste Management Rules, 2016** → segregation at source; apply beyond municipal areas; lay down {{criteria for landfill and processing sites}}.
- **Extended Producer Responsibility** → first in the {{E-Waste Rules, 2011}}; maker takes back end-of-life product.

How waste is treated → [Waste & remediation](@waste-remediation#waste-to-energy)

> [!DYN] Western Ghats eco-sensitive area notification, plastic thickness limits and recycling targets, and new platforms such as NIRANTAR (lead institutes: Botanical Survey of India; Indian Institute of Forest Management) are current-layer items. · Annual Ground Water Quality Report → published by the **Central Ground Water Board**.
