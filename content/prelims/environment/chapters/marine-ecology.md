## Producers & the Marine Food Web {#producers}

- Producers → **phytoplankton**: {{diatoms, cyanobacteria}}.
- Consumers → copepods (zooplankton), foraminifera.
- Chain → {{diatoms → crustaceans → herrings}}.
- Phytoplankton make about {{half}} of the world's oxygen and absorb CO₂.
- Lose phytoplankton → food chain collapses + ocean absorbs less carbon.
- Plankton drift · **nekton** swim (adult fish) · **benthos** live on the bottom. Fish eggs and larvae are often plankton.

## Upwelling & Algal Blooms {#upwelling}

> [!FLOW] Wind pushes surface water away → cold, {{nutrient-rich}} deep water rises → phytoplankton bloom → rich fisheries.

- **Harmful algal bloom** → from nutrient-rich sewage and farm runoff; upwelling can add to it.

Where upwelling happens → [Upwelling and fishing grounds](@prelims/geography/oceanography#equatorial-currents-fishing) · Nutrient overload in lakes → [Eutrophication](@pollution#water-pollution)

## Coral Reefs {#corals}

- Coral polyp → an {{animal}} (Cnidaria) living with a photosynthetic alga, {{zooxanthellae}}.
- Needs → warm, clear, shallow, sunlit, salty water.
- India → {{Andaman & Nicobar, Lakshadweep, Gulf of Mannar, Gulf of Kachchh}}. !!Not!! the Sundarbans.
- Mostly in tropical waters; hold more animal phyla than rainforests.

| Threat | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Bleaching** | Heat stress → coral expels its algae → turns white; can recover, or die if stress lasts |
| **Ocean acidification** | More CO₂ dissolves → pH falls → less carbonate → {{shells and skeletons harder to build}} |

- Acidification = sea becoming **less alkaline** — not literally acid.
- **Biorock** → low electric current through a steel frame → {{limestone deposits}} → corals attached to it grow faster.

Sea temperature and salinity → [Ocean properties](@prelims/geography/oceanography#temperature-salinity)

> [!TRAP] Coral is an animal, not a plant. · Bleaching (heat) ≠ acidification (CO₂ chemistry).

## Mangroves & Blue Carbon {#mangroves-blue-carbon}

- Mangroves → salt-tolerant trees of the tidal zone; dense roots hold mud and break waves.
- Roles → {{shield against cyclones and tsunami}} · fish nursery · carbon store · livelihoods.
- **Blue carbon** → carbon stored by {{mangroves, salt marshes, seagrass}}.

Where India's mangroves are → [Natural vegetation](@prelims/geography/india-climate-soils-vegetation#vegetation)

## Dugong {#dugong}

- Plant-eating marine mammal; feeds on {{seagrass}}.
- Gulf of Mannar, Palk Bay, Gulf of Kachchh, Andamans — not the whole coast.

> [!UPPCS] Coral bleaching in the Andamans → sea warming. · The ocean is the largest and most stable ecosystem.
