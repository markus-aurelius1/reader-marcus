## UNFCCC → Kyoto → Paris {#climate-treaties}

- **UNFCCC** → adopted 1992, in force 1994. COP = its yearly meeting of Parties.
- COP3 → Kyoto · COP16 → Cancún (Green Climate Fund) · COP21 → {{Paris}}.

**Kyoto mechanisms**

| Mechanism | Between | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| **Clean Development Mechanism** | Developed country funds a project in a {{developing}} country | CER |
| **Joint Implementation** | Two countries that both have targets | ERU |
| **Emissions Trading** | Country under target sells spare units | |

- Carbon credits began here. Price is set by the {{market}} — !!not!! by UNEP.

**Paris Agreement**
- Adopted 12 Dec 2015; in force {{4 Nov 2016}}. A {{legally binding treaty}}.
- Goal → well below {{2°C}}, pursue {{1.5°C}}, above pre-industrial level.
- **NDCs** → each country sets its own; updated every 5 years. Global stocktake reviews progress.
- **Article 6** → 6.2 country-to-country transfers · 6.4 UN-supervised crediting · 6.8 {{non-market}} approaches.

> [!TRAP] Paris is !!not!! "non-binding", and Article 6 is not only about markets.

How carbon markets work → [Carbon markets](@climate-change#carbon-markets)

## Forests & REDD+ {#redd}

- **REDD** → reducing emissions from deforestation and forest degradation.
- **REDD+** → adds {{conservation, sustainable management, enhancing forest carbon stocks}}.
- **UN-REDD** → UN programme (FAO, UNDP, UNEP).
- **Forest Carbon Partnership Facility** · **BioCarbon Fund (ISFL)** → {{World Bank}}.
- **New York Declaration on Forests** → voluntary, not binding.

## CBD Family {#cbd-family}

- **CBD** objectives → {{conservation · sustainable use · fair sharing of benefits from genetic resources}}.
- **Cartagena** → biosafety; advance informed agreement before moving living modified organisms.
- **Nagoya** → access and benefit sharing; prior informed consent. Indian arm → [Biological Diversity Act](@environmental-laws#biodiversity-act).
