## Classification {#classification}

| Type | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | Emitted directly | CO, SO₂, NO, dust, ammonia |
| **Secondary** | Formed in the air | {{Ground-level ozone, PAN, smog}}, acid |
| **Point source** | One identifiable outlet | Chimney, drain |
| **Non-point** | Diffuse | Farm and city runoff |

## Air Pollutants {#air-pollution}

| Pollutant | Source | Effect / trap |
|---|---|---|
| **Carbon monoxide** | Incomplete burning | Binds {{haemoglobin}} → blood carries less oxygen |
| **Sulphur dioxide** | Coal, oil, smelters | Acid rain, breathing trouble |
| **Nitrogen oxides** (NO + NO₂) | Vehicles, power plants | Smog, ozone, acid rain |
| **Nitrous oxide** (N₂O) | Fertilised soil, manure | Greenhouse gas — !!not!! the urban NOₓ |
| **Ground-level ozone** | {{Secondary}} — NOₓ + VOCs + sunlight | Lung irritant; peaks on sunny days |
| **PM10** | Dust, construction | ≤ 10 microns |
| **PM2.5** | Combustion, secondary particles | ≤ 2.5 microns; reaches deep lung and blood |
| **Benzene** (a VOC) | Vehicle exhaust, tobacco smoke, wood burning, paints, polyurethane products | Cancer-causing |
| **Ammonia** | Fertiliser, livestock | Forms secondary particles |
| **Black carbon** | Diesel, biomass burning | Soot — an {{aerosol, not a gas}}; warms air, darkens snow; short-lived |
| **Fly ash** | Coal power plants | Has arsenic, lead, mercury traces |
| **Magnetite particles** | Vehicle brakes and engines, power plants | Found in the brain; linked to neuro-disease |

## Smog & Acid Rain {#smog-acid-rain}

| | Classical (London) smog | Photochemical (Los Angeles) smog |
|---|---|---|
| Weather | Cool, humid | {{Warm, sunny}} |
| From | Coal smoke + SO₂ | NOₓ + VOCs + sunlight |
| Nature | Reducing | Oxidising — {{ozone, PAN}}, aldehydes |

- **PAN** → strong eye irritant.
- **Acid rain** → {{SO₂ + NOₓ}} → sulphuric and nitric acid.
- Normal rain is already slightly acidic (pH ~{{5.6}}) from dissolved CO₂.
- Effects → leaches soil nutrients, acidifies lakes, corrodes marble.

> [!TRAP] CO₂ and nitrous oxide are !!not!! the main causes of acid rain.

## Water Pollution Indicators {#water-pollution}

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **DO** | Oxygen actually dissolved in water |
| **BOD** | Oxygen microbes need to break down {{biodegradable organic matter}} → high BOD = polluted |
| **COD** | Oxygen needed to oxidise almost everything chemically → usually higher than BOD |

> [!FLOW] **Eutrophication** → excess {{nitrogen and phosphorus}} → algal bloom → algae die and rot → oxygen used up → fish die ("dead zone").

- Reactive nitrogen sources → fertilised soil, cattle, poultry.

## Heavy Metals & Groundwater {#heavy-metals}

| Contaminant | Source | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Lead** | Batteries, paint, smelting, old pipes | Brain damage in children |
| **Mercury** | Gold mining, coal power | Nerve poison; {{Minamata disease}}; biomagnifies |
| **Cadmium** | Batteries, e-waste | Kidney, bone ({{itai-itai}}) |
| **Arsenic** | Natural, in groundwater (Bengal, Bangladesh) | Skin lesions, cancer |
| **Fluoride** | Natural, in groundwater | Fluorosis — teeth, bones |
| **Nitrate** | Fertiliser, sewage | {{Blue-baby syndrome}} |
| **Uranium** | Natural, in groundwater | Kidney damage |

- Heavy metals are elements → they cannot be broken down, only removed or locked up.
- Pencil "lead" is graphite — not a source of lead.

Groundwater on the map → [Groundwater](@prelims/geography/india-drainage#groundwater)

## Emerging Contaminants {#emerging-contaminants}

| Substance | Found in | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Microbeads** | Face wash, toothpaste | Tiny plastic; harms marine life |
| **Microplastic** | Broken-down plastic | Less than {{5 mm}} |
| **BPA** | {{Polycarbonate}} bottles, can linings | Hormone disruptor |
| **Triclosan** | {{Soaps, toiletries}} | Antimicrobial |
| **PFAS** ("forever chemicals") | Non-stick, waterproof, food packaging | Do not break down; build up in the body |
| **Nanoparticles** | Natural and man-made; cosmetics | Can enter the food chain |
| **DDT** | Old pesticide | Persistent; biomagnifies |
| **Chewing gum** | Many gum bases are plastic | |

How they move up the food chain → [Bioaccumulation](@ecology-ecosystems#bioaccumulation)
